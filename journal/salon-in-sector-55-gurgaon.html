02 What to check before you pick a salon

A salon you trust should be easy to judge in the first five minutes. Tools should look clean, the chairs and basins should be tidy, and the staff should ask about your hair or skin before starting anything. If a salon skips the consultation and goes straight to work, that is a warning sign, especially for colour or facials.

Honest pricing matters too. Ask for a price range before the service starts, not after it ends. For hair colour, a good salon does a patch test for first-time clients and tells you honestly if a shade needs bleach your hair cannot handle. If a salon promises every shade on the chart with no questions asked, be careful.

Also ask how the salon handles patch tests and allergy history. A salon that takes two minutes to ask about past reactions is a salon that takes your skin and hair seriously. That small conversation prevents most problems before they start.

Karva Chauth on Thursday 29 October and Diwali on Sunday 8 November make October and November our busiest months. Facials, threading, hair spa, and mehndi appointments fill days ahead, and evening slots are always the first to go.
