01 What a de-tan actually does

A tan is your skin defending itself: sun exposure triggers extra melanin that darkens exposed areas. A salon de-tan works on the surface, gentle exfoliation lifts the dull top layer, and a de-tan pack evens out the tone underneath.

Afterward, your skin sits closer to its natural tone, with harsh lines softened or gone. A light, recent tan often clears in one sitting. A de-tan does not bleach you lighter than your natural complexion, and it does not fix marks unrelated to the sun. It restores your even tone; it does not give you a new one.

02 How a session works

A session takes 30 to 45 minutes: deep cleanse, gentle exfoliation, then a de-tan pack on the tanned areas for 15 to 20 minutes. A short massage while it works, finished with moisturiser and sunscreen.

Face, neck, and arms are the usual areas; back or shoulders can be added for weddings or holidays. The appointment is relaxed, you lie still while the pack works. Most clients book face and neck together.

03 How many sittings you will need

A recent, surface-level tan, the kind from a beach holiday or a sunny month of driving, usually improves visibly in one to two sessions. The tan lines soften and the overall tone looks brighter and more even.

A deeper tan needs two to three sessions spaced a week apart; each sitting can only lift so much without irritating the skin. Your therapist can judge the depth at the first visit. Be wary of anyone selling a fixed package before looking at your skin.

04 Who needs it most in Gurgaon

Two-wheeler riders top the list: helmet strap lines, collar lines, forearms darkened by daily rides. Car drivers get the one-arm gradient from the window side. Outdoor sports players collect it across the arms and neck.

Brides and grooms book it before the wedding, when tan lines ruin otherwise perfect shots. Treat a holiday tan soon; recent tans lift far more easily than old, set-in ones.

The real treatment is sunscreen: a de-tan clears the current tan, but only sunscreen stops the next one. Apply it every morning on the face, neck, and any exposed skin, and reapply if you are out for hours. Without it, the tan rebuilds within weeks and you are paying for the same session again. With it, one session can hold for months.

Questions we hear at the salon

How many de-tan sittings will I need?

A recent tan often improves visibly in one to two sittings. A deeper tan built over months usually needs two to three sessions spaced a week apart. Your therapist can judge at the first visit.

Will a de-tan make me fairer?

No. A de-tan removes the extra darkening caused by sun exposure and brings your skin back toward its natural tone. It does not lighten your natural complexion. Anyone promising a set number of shades lighter is overselling.

Can men get a de-tan?

Absolutely. Men are some of our most frequent de-tan clients: driver-arm tan lines, helmet strap marks, and sports tans are exactly what the treatment is built for. The session is the same; only the areas treated differ.

How soon before an event should I book a de-tan?

Book your last sitting at least 3 to 4 days before the event so the skin settles. Never get a de-tan the day before a wedding or function. Pair it with daily sunscreen afterward or the tan rebuilds within weeks in Gurgaon sun.
