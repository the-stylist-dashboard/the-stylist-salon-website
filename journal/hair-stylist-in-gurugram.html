Hair Stylist in Gurugram

The Stylist Unisex Salon · 30 October 2026 · 6 min read

A good hair stylist in Gurugram starts by asking about your face shape, hair type, and daily routine, then cuts a style you can manage at home, not just one that looks good in the salon chair.

01 What a good stylist asks first

Before any cut, a good stylist asks how you usually wear your hair, how much time you spend styling it, and whether you tie it up for work or workouts. A style that needs thirty minutes every morning is a bad fit for someone who has five. Honest stylists say this upfront.

They also ask about past cuts, colour, and treatments, because old colour and smoothening change how hair falls. If you had keratin or rebonding, say so, because it affects layers, texture, and how much volume a cut can give you.

02 Cuts for face shape and hair type

Round faces usually suit layers that add length and a side part, while longer faces suit softer layers and fringe that break up length. Thick hair needs weight removed carefully, while fine hair needs blunt ends and light layers so it does not look thinner. These are starting points, not rules, and your stylist should adapt them to you.

Curly and wavy hair should be cut with its natural pattern in mind, not straightened first and cut as if it were straight. Ask how your stylist handles curls, and be wary if the answer is to straighten it every time. A cut that only works when straightened is not a cut for curly hair.

03 Colour, styling, and upkeep

A stylist who also colours well can plan a cut and colour together, so layers show off highlights and the shape grows out gracefully. Ask how the colour will look in six weeks, not just on day one, because grow-out is where cheap colour work shows.

For styling, ask for a quick lesson at the end of the visit: how to blow dry, which product to use, and how much. Two minutes of guidance at the salon saves weeks of frustration at home, and it is a sign of a stylist who wants you back for the right reasons.

04 Men, women, and unisex styling

As a unisex salon, we cut for men and women, and the consultation matters for both. Men should say how often they want to return for shape-ups, because a sharp fade needs more frequent upkeep than a longer textured cut. Women should say whether they tie hair up, colour it, or heat style it often.

Teenagers and first-time clients get the same consultation, because a first cut that goes wrong puts people off salons for years. If you are unsure, start with a smaller change and build up over two visits rather than one dramatic cut.

Questions we hear at the salon

How do I pick a hair stylist in Gurugram?

Look for a proper consultation, work that suits your hair type, and honest advice about upkeep. Return visits and detailed reviews are good signs.

How often should I get a haircut?

Every six to eight weeks for most styles, every three to four weeks for short fades and sharp shapes. Your stylist can set a rhythm that fits your cut.

Should I wash my hair before a haircut?

Come with clean, dry, normally styled hair if you can, so the stylist sees how it really falls. Tell us if you used heavy oil or styling product that day.

Can one stylist handle cut and colour together?

Yes, and it usually works better, because the cut and colour are planned together for how they grow out.
