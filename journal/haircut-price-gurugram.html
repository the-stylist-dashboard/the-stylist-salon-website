How Much Does a Haircut Cost in Gurugram?

The Stylist Unisex Salon · 27 September 2026 · 5 min read

Search for haircut prices in Gurugram and you get everything from Rs 99 deals to four-figure luxury cuts, with no explanation of what the money buys. So here is the plain version: our prices, printed without asterisks, plus what moves the number up or down at any salon in the city. Then you can judge for yourself whether a price is fair.

The short answer

At The Stylist Unisex Salon, a men's haircut costs Rs 149 and a women's haircut costs Rs 349. Across Gurugram, prices run from budget neighbourhood rates to Rs 800 and above at premium salons. What you pay elsewhere usually tracks the salon's location and rent, the stylist's experience, and how much time the appointment takes.

01 Our prices, without asterisks

Men's haircut: Rs 149. Women's haircut: Rs 349. That is the price, confirmed before we pick up the scissors, with no surprise add-ons at billing.

We keep the board simple on purpose. A haircut price should not need decoding, and you should never discover the real number at the payment counter.

02 What moves the price at any salon

Location and rent. A salon inside a Gurugram mall pays several times the rent of a high-street shop in Sector 52, and the haircut price carries that cost. Same scissors, different postcode economics.

Stylist seniority. A senior stylist with a decade of precision cutting charges for judgement built over thousands of heads. For complex corrective work, that premium is worth it. For a maintenance trim, it rarely matters.

Time in the chair. A 15-minute rush cut and a 45-minute consultation cut are different services wearing the same name. Longer appointments cost more because the salon can serve fewer clients a day.

What is bundled. Wash, styling, finishing: some salons include them, some itemise them. A Rs 99 cut with separate wash and styling charges is not cheaper than an all-in Rs 149. Always ask what the number covers.

03 Where we sit in the market

Our pricing is deliberately on the accessible end. For context on the range: premium salons in the city charge several times our rates. A premium Arjun Marg salon, for example, lists its men's haircut at Rs 826 on its Google profile. That premium buys a luxury address, senior celebrity stylists and a longer appointment.

Whether it is worth it depends on what you value. Plenty of clients prefer a sharp Rs 149 cut every three weeks over one Rs 800 cut every two months. Hair grows; the maths favours frequency over luxury for most people.

04 How to tell a fair price

Ask three questions before you book anywhere:

  1. What is included? Wash, cut, finish: get the list, not a shrug.
  2. Is this the final price? If the answer has conditions, keep asking.
  3. Who is cutting? A trainee and a senior at different prices is fair. A trainee at senior prices is not.

And trust the consultation. A stylist who looks at your hair, asks about your routine and suggests something specific is worth more than one who reaches for the scissors while you are still explaining.

The cheapest haircut is the one you never have to fix. A rushed Rs 99 cut followed by a Rs 500 correction elsewhere is a Rs 600 haircut with extra regret.

Haircut Prices in Gurugram questions, answered

Why do women's haircuts cost more than men's?

Time and complexity. Women's cuts usually involve longer hair, layering and texturising, plus a finished style, taking 30 to 45 minutes against 15 to 25 for most men's cuts. The price tracks the appointment length.

Do I pay extra for wash and styling?

Ask before you book, everywhere including here. Unbundled wash and styling charges are the most common surprise at salon billing counters. Our quotes are confirmed upfront, so the number you hear is the number you pay.

How often should I budget for a haircut?

Short men's cuts every 3 to 4 weeks, women's trims every 6 to 8 weeks. At Rs 149, a monthly men's cut costs less than most streaming subscriptions.

Are expensive haircuts worth it?

For complex work, yes: corrective cuts, precision bobs and major restyles reward an experienced eye. For a maintenance trim on healthy hair, a skilled neighbourhood stylist at a fair price delivers the same result.
