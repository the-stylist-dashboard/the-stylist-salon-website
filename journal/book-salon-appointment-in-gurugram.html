01 How booking works

Booking is simple: one phone call, no app, no account, no form to fill. Tell us the service you want, whether it is a haircut, colour, facial, manicure, mehndi, or makeup, and we will suggest a slot and tell you honestly how long it will take. If the service needs more time than you have, we will say so and offer another day.

Morning slots from 11 AM suit colour, bridal work, and full prep days, because they leave buffer time if a treatment runs long. Evening slots suit quick services like threading, trims, and cleanups. If you are unsure, ask us, and we will suggest a time based on the service, not just the first free slot.

02 What to tell us when you call

Tell us about your hair and skin history in a few sentences: past colour, smoothening, keratin, sensitivity, or allergies. This helps us pick the right stylist, the right product, and enough time. It also avoids surprises at the salon, where changing the plan mid-visit wastes your time.

Mention your function date, even if it is weeks away, because festive and wedding bookings fill in order. A Diwali colour booked now is a calm visit; the same colour booked on Diwali morning is a rushed one, if a slot exists at all.

03 Group and family bookings

For weddings and festivals, families often book together: mothers, sisters, and friends getting ready for the same function. Tell us the group size when you call, so we can plan chairs and stylists together instead of splitting your group across the day.

Group bookings work best when everyone arrives on time and the service list is agreed in advance. One late arrival can push the whole group back, so plan travel time from your sector, especially around Golf Course Extension Road traffic in the evening.

04 Changing or cancelling a booking

If your plans change, call us as early as you can, so we can offer the slot to someone else and rebook you without stress. A quick call is enough, and we would rather move your booking than have you rush in late for a treatment that needs its full time.

If you arrive late, tell us by phone on the way, and we will tell you honestly what can still be done well in the time left. Some services, like colour, cannot be rushed safely, and we will say so rather than cut corners.
