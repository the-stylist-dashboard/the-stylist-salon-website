01 What to look for in a hair salon

Start with the consultation. A salon that rushes you straight to the chair without asking about your hair history, past colour or daily routine is guessing, and guessing is how bad cuts happen. A proper consultation takes a few minutes and covers what you want, what your hair can handle, and what it will cost. If a salon skips this, think twice.

Cleanliness and tools matter too. Combs, scissors and brushes should be clean, capes fresh, and the station tidy. These are basics, but they tell you how seriously a salon takes its work. Ask what will be used on your hair, especially for colour and keratin: a good salon answers plainly.

Nails, skin and makeup services are available too, so a hair appointment can sit alongside a facial or party makeup. Booking them together saves you a second trip, and our team plans the order so nothing clashes.

03 Colour done properly, not rushed

Colour is where salons differ most. A good colour job starts with checking your hair's current state, including old colour and treatments, because these change how new colour takes. Your stylist should show you the shade in natural light, explain how it will grow out, and tell you how many sessions a big change will take.

Going much lighter in one sitting usually damages hair, and a salon that promises it anyway is selling speed over health. We space out big colour changes and use care treatments in between, so the result lasts and your hair stays strong. Ask how your colour will look in four weeks, not just on day one.

04 Prices, honesty and no surprises

Nothing spoils a salon visit like a bill that doubles at the counter. Before any service starts, we confirm the full price, including extras like a wash, a treatment or a toner. If something extra comes up mid-service, we check with you first. That is a basic courtesy, but it is also how trust is built visit after visit.

We also tell you when a service is not worth it. If your hair does not need keratin yet, or a colour idea will not suit your skin tone, we say so. A salon that always agrees with you is not advising you, it is just taking the booking.
