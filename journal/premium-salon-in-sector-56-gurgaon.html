01 What premium actually means at a salon

Premium gets thrown around a lot in Gurgaon salons. At The Stylist, it means something specific: skilled stylists, good products, unhurried appointments, and a chair you leave feeling better than when you sat down. Here is what premium looks like at our salon, and who it suits.

Premium is not gold chairs and loud music. It is time. A premium appointment is never rushed, so your stylist studies your hair, talks through the plan, and checks the result before you leave. Rushed work is where mistakes happen.

It is also consistency. The same standard every visit, whichever stylist you sit with. We keep notes on your hair history, so a colour or cut from three months ago is still on record when you return.

02 The premium experience, step by step

Your visit starts with a consultation, not a cape. We ask about your hair history, your routine, and what you want changed. Then we suggest a plan and quote the price before anything starts.

During the service, we use professional products suited to your hair type, and we explain what we are doing as we go. After the finish, we show you the home routine in two minutes, not a sales pitch for ten products.

Premium also shows in the small details: a chair adjusted to your height, a cape that actually stays put, and a stylist who checks the back of your cut with a mirror before you leave. These details take seconds, but they are what separate a careful salon from a hurried one.

03 Premium hair, nails and skin services

On hair, premium means careful colour placement, balayage that grows out softly, keratin and smoothening done with proper aftercare advice, and hair spa sessions that leave hair genuinely softer. Our hair spa is a hair and scalp treatment only, with a deep mask, steam, and a head massage.

On nails and skin, it means hygienic tools, patch tests where needed, and artists who take their time. A rushed manicure chips in two days. A careful one lasts. The same goes for facials and cleanup: skin typed first, products chosen second.

04 Who a premium salon suits, and who it does not

A premium salon suits you if you value the result lasting: a cut that holds its shape, colour that fades evenly, and skin that stays calm after a facial. It suits brides, grooms, and anyone before a big event, when there is no room for a redo.

It may not suit you if you want the fastest, cheapest option every time. We will not rush a service to fit a queue, and we will not cut corners on products. If speed matters more than finish, a quick neighbourhood chair may fit better, and we would rather say that than disappoint you.
