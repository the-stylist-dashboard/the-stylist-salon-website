01 How booking works at The Stylist

Booking a salon appointment should take two minutes, not a chain of missed calls and confusion. At The Stylist, one call to 81781 59945 sorts your service, your stylist, your time, and your price. Here is exactly how it works, and what helps us serve you better.

Call 81781 59945 any day we are open, Wednesday to Monday, 11 AM to 9 PM. Tell us the service you want, and we will offer the nearest slots and the stylist best suited to it. You can also walk in on weekdays, but weekends are booking-only in practice because chairs fill fast.

We confirm your slot on the call and note the service, so the right products and time are ready when you arrive. No advance payment is needed for regular services. For bridal or large group bookings, we may take a small advance to hold the chairs.

02 What to tell us when you call

The more you tell us, the better the appointment fits. Mention your hair length, any colour or keratin history, and what you want done. If you are adding services, like a haircut plus a hair spa, say so, because that changes the time we need.

For colour or bridal work, tell us your event date so we can plan backwards. Colour needs a patch test for sensitive skin, and bridal makeup needs a trial. Mention allergies or sensitive skin upfront, and we will plan around them.

03 Booking for weekends, weddings and festivals

Saturday and Sunday slots go first, and festive weeks like Karva Chauth and Diwali book out days ahead. If your event is on a weekend, call at least three to four days early, and a week early for bridal parties.

If your preferred slot is full, we will offer the nearest alternative or put you on a short waitlist and call you if a chair opens. We would rather be honest about a full day than squeeze you in and rush the work.

For large groups, like a bridal party or a family getting ready together, tell us the headcount when you book. We can line up extra stylists and chairs so everyone finishes on time, instead of the last person rushing through their makeup.

04 Changing or cancelling your booking

Plans change, and that is fine. Call 81781 59945 as early as you can, and we will move your slot to another day at no charge. For bridal or group bookings, a little more notice helps us reassign the chairs.

If you are running late, call us too. We hold your chair for a short grace period, and if the delay is long, we may adjust the service so the next client is not kept waiting. A quick call keeps everyone on schedule.

05 Getting to us from Sector 44

From Sector 44, our Sector 52 salon is a short drive along Golf Course Extension Road. We are at Shop No. 86, High Street 52, Sector 52, Gurugram, opposite Artemis Hospital.

Our second branch is at Ground Floor, The Arzoo Apartments, Sector 51, Gurugram, handy for clients from the Sushant Lok side. Both branches take bookings on the same number, 81781 59945.

We are open Wednesday to Monday, 11 AM to 9 PM, and closed on Tuesdays. If you are unsure which branch is nearer, ask on the call and we will point you to the quicker route.

The honest test: A good booking leaves nothing to guess. You should hang up knowing your day, your time, your service, and your price. If any of those are unclear, ask before you come in.
