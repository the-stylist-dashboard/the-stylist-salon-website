If you live in Sector 53, calmer mornings are minutes away. Hair smoothening promises frizz-free, straighter hair that behaves in Gurugram humidity, but it is widely misunderstood. It is not rebonding, not keratin, and not permanent. It is a semi-permanent treatment that relaxes frizz and curl into softer, straighter hair for a few months. Here is what it does, who it suits, and how to keep the results.

01 What hair smoothening is

Smoothening uses a mild chemical relaxer plus heat to loosen your curl pattern. The formula softens the hair's structure enough to relax frizz and waves, then a flat iron sets the straighter shape. Think of it as persuading the hair rather than rebuilding it from scratch.

The result is straighter, calmer hair with some natural movement. It is not pin-straight, and that is the point. Because the change is partial, it fades gradually over 3 to 6 months, with no harsh line between treated hair and new growth. The appointment usually takes 2 to 3 hours.

02 Who it suits, and who should skip it

It suits wavy to mildly curly hair, frizz-prone hair that poufs in humidity, and first-timers who want easier mornings. Skip it if your hair is bleached or fragile, since chemical treatments on compromised hair can cause breakage. And skip it if you want flat, pin-straight hair that never moves; that is rebonding's job. When in doubt, ask for a strand test first.

04 Aftercare that decides how long it lasts

The first 72 hours matter most. Do not wash, tie, clip, or tuck your hair behind your ears. Creases that set in this window stay. If it rains, cover your hair.

After the waiting period, switch to sulphate-free shampoo and conditioner, and wash less often. Sulphates strip the treatment faster, so this one change adds real weeks to your results. A light anti-frizz serum handles humid days, and you will need far less heat styling.

Gurugram's water is hard, which shortens the life of chemical treatments. A shower filter is a small investment that protects what you paid for.

Before you book: tell your stylist your full hair history, old colour, previous smoothening or keratin, at-home straightening. Smoothening over recent bleach needs a different approach, and honesty here keeps your hair safe.

Questions we hear at the salon

How long does hair smoothening last?

Usually 3 to 6 months. It fades gradually as your natural texture returns, so there is no harsh regrowth line. Your hair texture, wash frequency, and sulphate-free products decide the exact timeline.

Is smoothening or keratin better for frizzy hair?

Keratin smooths frizz while keeping your natural wave mostly intact and lasts 2 to 4 months. Smoothening straightens more, lasts 3 to 6 months, but involves stronger chemistry. For frizz on otherwise manageable hair, keratin is often the gentler answer.

Can I wash my hair the day after smoothening?

No. Wait 48 to 72 hours before the first wash, and avoid tying, clips, or tucking hair behind your ears in that window; creases can set into freshly treated hair. Afterward, switch to a sulphate-free shampoo.

Will smoothening make my hair pin straight?

No. Smoothening relaxes and softens but does not give rebonding's flat, pin-straight finish. Expect straighter, calmer hair with natural movement. For pin-straight, ask about rebonding and get a strand test first.
