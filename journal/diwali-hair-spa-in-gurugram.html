01 Why a hair spa before Diwali

Diwali week means long evenings, heat from diyas and cooking, and many photos, so hair that is dry or frizzy shows up clearly. A hair spa adds moisture back, smooths the outer layer, and makes hair easier to comb and style for functions. It also helps colour look richer, because moisturised hair holds shine better than dry hair.

If you coloured your hair recently, a hair spa a few days after the colour helps the shade settle and softens any dryness from the colour process. If you are planning colour for Diwali, a hair spa a week before the colour helps the shade take evenly. Either way, a spa before the festival is far better than a spa after it, when the damage is already done.

02 What a Diwali hair spa session includes

A proper hair spa starts with a wash, followed by a mask chosen for your hair type, a head massage to work the product in, and steam to help it absorb. The session usually takes about 45 to 60 minutes, and you should leave with hair that feels softer and easier to comb, not heavy or coated. If your hair feels sticky after a spa, too much product was used or the wrong mask was picked.

A head massage is part of the hair spa routine at The Stylist, and it is a hair and head massage only. The massage helps blood flow to the scalp and helps the mask spread evenly, but it should never feel rough or rushed. Tell your stylist if the pressure feels too strong, because a good massage is firm yet comfortable.

03 Timing your Diwali hair spa

Book your Diwali hair spa three to five days before your main function, in the week before Diwali on 8 November 2026. That gap lets the softness settle, so your hair styles easily on the day, and it leaves time for a colour or a cut in the same week without rushing. A spa done too early in the month may lose its effect by Diwali, while a spa done on the day itself is hard to fit around functions.

If you have several functions across the week, book the spa before the first big one, and keep your home routine simple after: gentle washing, lukewarm water, and no heavy oiling right before styling. Ask your stylist for home care tips at your visit, so the spa result lasts through the whole festive week.
