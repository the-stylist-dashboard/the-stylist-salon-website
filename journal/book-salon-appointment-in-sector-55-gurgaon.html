Book a Salon Appointment in Sector 55, Gurgaon

The Stylist Unisex Salon · 21 October 2026 · 6 min read

Booking a salon appointment should take two minutes, not ten phone calls and a guessing game. If you live or work in Sector 55, Gurgaon, this is exactly how to book a visit with us: which days are calm, which days fill up, and what to tell us when you call so your chair is ready and waiting when you arrive.

01 The fastest way to book: one phone call

Call 8178159945. That is the whole system. Tell us your name, the service you want, and the day and time that suits you. We confirm on the spot and write it in the book. No app to download, no form to fill, no waiting for a reply.

Our Sector 52 studio is a short drive from Sector 55, just off Golf Course Extension Road, and we also have a branch in Sector 51 at Ground Floor, The Arzoo Apartments. When you call, tell us which studio you prefer and we will slot you there.

02 The best days and times to come

Weekday mornings, Wednesday to Friday, 11 AM to 1 PM, are the quietest hours. The salon is calm, stylists have full attention to give, and you are in and out without a wait. If your schedule allows it, these are the best slots of the week.

Weekends are a different story. Saturday afternoons are our busiest hours, and Sundays fill up fast too. For a weekend slot, call a day or two ahead. Also note: we are closed on Tuesdays. That catches people out, so check the day before you plan your visit.

03 What to tell us when you book

The more you tell us on the call, the better your appointment goes. Give us these details:

  • The service. Haircut, hair colour, hair spa, keratin, manicure, facial, beard trim, whatever you need. If you want more than one service, say so, so we keep enough time.
  • Your hair length. Shoulder-length, waist-length, short. This matters for colour and treatment pricing and timing.
  • Your hair history. Any colour, bleach, keratin, or straightening you already have. Colour behaves very differently on pre-treated hair, and we need to plan for it.
  • Allergies. Especially for colour or facial services. Tell us on the call so we can plan a patch test if needed.
  • Your time budget. A quick trim is 30 minutes. A global colour is 2 to 3 hours. Tell us how much time you have so we pick the right service and slot.

For bridal and party bookings, tell us the event date and how many people are coming. Those bookings need more planning, and we like to start the conversation at least two weeks out.

04 Walk-in vs appointment: which one suits you

Walk-ins are welcome, but they suit weekday mornings best. Come in, and if a stylist is free, we take you right away. On busy days you may wait, and for longer services like colour, keratin, or a hair spa, waiting eats into your day.

An appointment means your stylist is ready for you and everything is prepped. For colour services we can even do the patch test at the booking stage, so nothing delays the main visit. If you are coming from Sector 55 after office hours or on a Saturday, an appointment is honestly the smarter move. It costs nothing extra and saves you the wait. If you are booking from a nearby area too, the same system works for you: read our guide to booking a salon appointment in Sushant Lok.

A small habit that helps: after we confirm your booking, take a quick screenshot of the day and time or save it as a note. A surprising number of mix-ups happen because someone remembered "Saturday" but we wrote down "Sunday".

Questions we hear at the salon

Can I walk in without an appointment?

Yes, walk-ins are welcome on weekday mornings when the salon is quiet. On weekends, book ahead by calling 8178159945, because the chairs fill up fast and you may have to wait.

How early should I book a weekend slot?

A day or two ahead is usually enough. Saturday afternoons are the busiest slots of the week, so if you need one, call early. Weekday mornings rarely need more than a same-day call.

What should I tell you when I call to book?

Tell us your name, the service you want, and your preferred day and time. Also mention your hair length, any colour or chemical treatments you already have, any allergies, and how much time you have. That lets us keep the right stylist and slot ready for you.

Which of your studios is closest to Sector 55?

Our Sector 52 studio is the closest: Shop No. 86, High Street 52, opposite Artemis Hospital. Sector 55 residents also visit our Sector 51 branch at Ground Floor, The Arzoo Apartments. Both are open Wednesday to Monday, 11 AM to 9 PM, closed on Tuesdays.
