01 Gel or acrylic: which extensions suit you

Gel extensions are built with a soft gel that is cured under a light, and they feel light and flexible on the nail. They suit most people, look natural, and are easy to remove. Acrylic extensions are built with a powder and liquid mix that sets hard, and they suit people who want very long nails or a shape that needs extra strength.

The nail technician will look at your natural nails before suggesting one or the other, because weak or bitten nails need a gentler approach than strong nails. Tell the technician how you use your hands, because long nails and heavy typing or housework do not mix well. A shorter, squoval shape is the safest pick if you use your hands a lot.

02 How the extensions session works

An extensions session takes one and a half to two hours. The technician preps your natural nails, fits the tips or forms, builds the extension, shapes it, and finishes with gel polish in the colour you pick. The finish can be plain, French, ombre, or nail art, and brides often pick a soft nude with a little shimmer for the wedding functions.

Bring a photo of the shape and colour you want, and tell the technician about any allergies or sensitivity, because the products sit on your nails for weeks. If your nails are damaged, the technician may suggest a manicure and a break first, instead of extensions right away. Healthy nails hold extensions far better than damaged ones.

03 Caring for nail extensions

Extensions last three to four weeks before they need a fill, as your natural nails grow out. Do not pick or pull at them, because that lifts the natural nail with the extension and leaves it thin and sore. Wear gloves for cleaning and washing up, and use cuticle oil daily to keep the nails and the skin around them soft.

Book a fill every two to three weeks to keep the extensions neat, and have them removed properly at the salon instead of peeling them off at home. A proper removal keeps your natural nails healthy, while peeling takes layers off and leaves them weak for months. If an extension lifts or cracks, come in for a fix instead of gluing it yourself.
