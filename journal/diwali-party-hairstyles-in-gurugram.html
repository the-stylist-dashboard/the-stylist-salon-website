01 Styles that suit Diwali outfits

For a heavy lehenga or saree, a neat bun or a low chignon keeps the hair tidy and shows off the jewellery, while soft waves suit a lighter outfit and a more relaxed function. A half-up style suits medium length hair, because it keeps the front tidy while leaving length to move. Bring a photo of your outfit, because the style should sit well with the outfit shade and the neckline, not fight it.

Hair length decides what is possible, so be honest about it. Very short hair suits a neat, polished finish with a side part or soft texture, while long hair suits braids, buns, and waves. If your hair is too short for the style you want, ask about a temporary extension or a different style, rather than forcing a look that will fall apart by mid-evening.

02 Hair that holds through the evening

A party style holds best on hair that is clean, healthy, and not freshly oiled, because oil makes the style slip. Wash your hair the day before, not on the day itself, and skip heavy oiling right before styling. A hair spa a few days before Diwali helps too, because soft, moisturised hair holds a style far better than dry, frizzy hair.

Tell your stylist how long the evening will be and whether you will dance, because a style for a long evening needs stronger setting than a style for a short pooja. A good stylist will set the style in stages, with light product at each step, so it holds without feeling stiff or heavy. If the style feels too tight or too loose, say so before you leave, not after.

03 Styling with a fresh cut or colour

If you want a fresh cut or colour for Diwali, book it three to five days before your main function, in the week before Diwali on 8 November 2026, so the cut settles and the colour shade is true. A cut done on the day itself can look too fresh and sharp in photos, while a colour done on the day leaves no time for a correction. A gap of a few days also lets you wash once and see how the hair truly looks.

A hair spa a week before the colour helps the shade take evenly, and a spa a few days after colour helps the shade settle. If you have never coloured your hair before, a consultation and a patch test are needed first, and both need time. Never leave a first colour to the final days before a function, because a reaction or a wrong shade would leave no time to fix it.
