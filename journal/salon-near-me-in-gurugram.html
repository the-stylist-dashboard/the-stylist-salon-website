01 Why distance alone is a weak test

The nearest salon is not always the best salon for the service you want. A salon two minutes away that rushes a colour job will cost you more in correction than a salon ten minutes away that consults first. Distance should decide between two good salons, not replace every other check.

Use distance as a first filter, then check the rest: does the salon ask about your hair or skin before starting, does it quote before work begins, are tools visibly clean, and do clients come back. Those four answers tell you more than any star rating alone.
