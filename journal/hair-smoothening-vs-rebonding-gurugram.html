Hair Smoothening vs Rebonding: Which One Survives Gurugram?

The Stylist Unisex Salon · 27 September 2026 · 7 min read

You want straight hair that stays straight. The salon menu offers two roads, smoothening and rebonding, and most clients pick whichever word the last salon used. They are different treatments with different chemistry, different grow-out and different upkeep. Pick the wrong one and you either pay for permanence you did not want, or softness that gives up by August.

The short answer

Smoothening relaxes curls and frizz into softer, straighter hair that gradually loosens over 3 to 6 months. Rebonding breaks and rebuilds the hair's bonds for pin-straight results that stay straight permanently on the treated length, with only regrowth needing touch-ups. In Gurugram's humidity, rebonding holds its shape better, but smoothening grows out more naturally.

01 What smoothening does

Smoothening uses a mild chemical relaxer plus heat to loosen your curl pattern without rebuilding the hair's structure. Think of it as persuading the hair rather than reconstructing it.

The result is straighter, calmer, far less frizzy hair that still keeps some natural movement. It is not pin-straight, and that is the point. Because the change is only partial, it fades gradually: after 3 to 6 months your natural texture is mostly back, which makes the grow-out painless. There is no harsh line where treated hair meets new growth.

Smoothening suits wavy or mildly curly hair, first-time chemical treatments, and anyone who wants easier mornings without a dramatic change.

02 What rebonding does

Rebonding is the full commitment. A stronger formula breaks the disulphide bonds that give your hair its shape, the hair is ironed perfectly straight, and a neutraliser then rebuilds those bonds in the new straight configuration.

The treated length stays straight permanently. It does not loosen and it does not revert in humidity. Only your new growth comes in with its natural texture, which is why rebonded hair needs root touch-ups every 6 to 9 months, and why the line between straight lengths and wavy roots needs managing as it grows.

Rebonding suits tight curls and coils that smoothening cannot tame, and anyone who wants a permanent straight result and accepts the maintenance contract that comes with it.

04 Which holds up in Gurugram

NCR humidity is the stress test for any straightening treatment. Rebonded hair barely notices it: the bonds were rebuilt straight, and moisture in the air does not undo that chemistry.

Smoothened hair will pouf a little on the worst July days, because the underlying texture was only relaxed, not rebuilt. A good anti-frizz serum and a quick blow-dry handle it on most days.

So the local rule of thumb: if your hair rebels hard every monsoon and you are tired of fighting it, rebonding gives the calmer result. If you like some volume and movement and your frizz is moderate, smoothening's softer finish will make you happier.

05 The damage question

Both are chemical treatments, so damage-free is marketing, not chemistry. Rebonding is harsher by nature; on already coloured, bleached or fragile hair it can push strands past recovery. Smoothening is gentler, but repeated sessions stack up over time.

What protects your hair is not the brand name on the bottle but the stylist's judgement: strand tests before committing, correct processing time for your hair's condition, and refusing to treat hair that is not healthy enough. A salon that never says no to a chemical service is a red flag. We turn treatments away when the hair cannot take them.

The regrowth line is the whole game with rebonding. Plan root touch-ups before the contrast between straight lengths and natural roots gets obvious, usually around month six. With smoothening there is no such deadline.

Smoothening vs Rebonding questions, answered

Which lasts longer, smoothening or rebonding?

Rebonding, by a wide margin. The treated hair stays straight permanently and only new growth needs attention. Smoothening gradually loosens over 3 to 6 months until your natural texture returns.

Can I get smoothening or rebonding on coloured hair?

Sometimes, with caution. Healthy coloured hair can usually take smoothening. Rebonding over colour is riskier and needs a strand test first. Bleached or heavily damaged hair should recover before either treatment.

Will smoothened hair go back to normal?

Yes, gradually. Smoothening does not permanently restructure the hair, so your natural texture returns over a few months. Rebonded hair does not revert; it stays straight until it is cut off.

How soon can I wash my hair after the treatment?

Most stylists advise waiting 48 to 72 hours before the first wash, and avoiding tying the hair or tucking it behind your ears in that window, since creases can set into freshly treated hair.
