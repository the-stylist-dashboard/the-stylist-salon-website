Book a Salon Appointment in Sushant Lok, Gurgaon

The Stylist Unisex Salon · 20 October 2026 · 6 min read

If you live in Sushant Lok, you are only minutes from a good salon visit, and booking ahead turns that into a zero-wait trip. To book a salon appointment in Sushant Lok, all it takes is one phone call: tell us the service and the time, and your chair is held.

01 How booking by call actually works

It is simpler than most people expect. You call 81781 59945, say which service you want, and give a day and time that suits you. The salon confirms a slot and sets aside the right amount of time. A haircut gets 30 to 45 minutes, a hair spa about an hour, and colour or keratin a longer block based on your hair.

When you book a salon appointment in Sushant Lok with us, mention your hair length if you want colour or chemical treatments, because the time and product needed depend on it. If you are flexible, give two or three time options. Flexible callers get better slots, especially on weekends.

02 The best times to book

Weekday mornings are the quietest. If you can come on a Wednesday to Friday morning, you will get your pick of times and the most relaxed appointment. We are open Wed to Mon, 11 AM to 9 PM, and closed on Tuesdays.

Weekends are the busiest, especially Saturday afternoons. If you can only come on a weekend, book at least two or three days ahead. Evening slots after office hours also fill fast on weekdays.

Running late? Call the salon if you are delayed. Most salons will hold your chair for about 10 to 15 minutes. If you are running much later than that, it is kinder to reschedule than to rush the stylist and the customer after you.

03 What to tell us when you call

The more specific you are, the better we can plan. Say the service you want, for example a men's haircut, a layered cut, a hair spa, or global colour. Mention your hair length if the service involves products. If it is for an occasion, give us the date so we can time it right.

If you have reference photos, keep them on your phone and show them when you arrive. Photos cut through the confusion of describing haircuts in words. If you have allergies to hair products or sensitive skin, mention it on the call.

04 Sushant Lok to Sector 52, in minutes

Sushant Lok is roughly 2 to 3 km from our Sector 52 salon, so the drive is short and predictable. Leave 10 minutes before your slot and you will be there on time. No long drive, no parking headache, no need to block half your day for a haircut.

Our salon is at Shop No. 86, High Street 52, Sector 52, right opposite Artemis Hospital. The location is easy to find on the first visit, and High Street 52 has straightforward parking. Plenty of Sushant Lok residents are already regulars because the trip is so easy that a farther salon makes no sense.

05 Walk-ins versus booking

Walk-ins are welcome on quieter weekday mornings, and if a chair is free, you will be seated right away. But chairs fill up, especially in the evenings and on weekends, and then walk-ins wait. Booking by call removes that risk entirely: your chair is held, and the stylist knows what you are coming for.

There is another advantage to booking: the consultation. A properly reserved slot has time to talk through what you want before any work starts. Rushed walk-in slots sometimes skip that, and skipped consultations are where bad haircuts come from. One phone call is the difference.
