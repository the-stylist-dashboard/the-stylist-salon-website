01 What to decide before you call

Decide the service first, because that sets how much time the salon needs to keep for you. A simple trim needs a short slot, while colour, a facial, or a combined visit needs a longer one. Also decide your preferred day and time, and which location suits you better, Sector 52 opposite Artemis Hospital or Sector 51 at The Arzoo Apartments.

If you have a function coming up, keep the date in mind when you call, so the booking can be placed with enough gap before it. Facials and colour are best done three to five days before a function, not on the day itself. A short note about your plan at booking helps the salon pick the right slot for you.

02 Booking by phone, step by step

Call 81781 59945 and tell us the service you want, the day and time you prefer, and which location you would like. Mention any past colour, keratin, smoothening, or scalp sensitivity, because that history changes which products are safe and how much time is needed. A clear note at booking avoids a wrong product choice later.

We will confirm the slot, the service, and the location on the call itself, so there is no confusion on the day. If you need more than one service in the same visit, such as a cut with a facial or a manicure with a pedicure, say so at booking, because a combined visit needs a longer slot and a calmer pace.

03 Booking for Diwali week and wedding season

In the week before Diwali on 8 November 2026, book early, because cuts, colour, facials, and grooming all get booked in the same few days. A weekday slot in that week is often calmer than a weekend slot, and it gives the stylist more time for your service. The salon is closed on Tuesdays, so keep that in mind when you pick a day.

For a wedding, book as soon as the functions are fixed, because wedding season dates go first. Tell the salon which function is your main one, so hair, skin, and makeup bookings can be spaced sensibly across the week. A plan made early is far calmer than a last-minute round of bookings, and it leaves room for a small change if needed.
