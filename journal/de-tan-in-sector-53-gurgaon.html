01 What a de-tan does, and what it does not do

A de-tan works on the top layer of skin where sun tan sits. It loosens and lifts that tanned layer, so your natural skin tone shows through more evenly. It is not a skin-lightening treatment, and it will not change your natural colour. It simply brings you back to the tone you had before the sun did its work.

That honesty matters. If someone promises to make you several shades fairer in one sitting, be careful. A good de-tan leaves your skin even, fresh, and calm, not bleached or patchy.

02 Face, neck and arms: where tan shows most

Tan shows up first where the sun hits hardest: the face, the neck, the forearms, and the backs of the hands. Sleeves and collars leave lines that stand out in festive outfits, especially in photos. That is why most clients ask for face, neck and arms together before a function.

If you drive a lot, your right arm often tans more than your left. Tell us where the tan is worst, and we will spend the extra time there instead of giving every area the same quick pass.

03 What happens during your session

We start by cleansing your skin to remove dust, oil, and sunscreen. Then we apply the de-tan product and let it work for the time your skin needs. The session is gentle and relaxing, and most clients find it comfortable throughout. We finish with a soothing application and moisturiser, so your skin feels soft, not tight.

After the session, your skin may look slightly pink for a short while. That settles quickly. We tell you what to expect before we start, so there are no surprises when you look in the mirror.

04 How many sessions you need

Fresh tan from a recent holiday or a few weeks of sun usually lightens well in one session. Older tan that has built up over months may need two or three sessions spaced a week apart. We look at your skin first and give you an honest plan, not a fixed package.

Avoid direct sun for a day after your session, and skip scrubs and harsh face washes for 24 to 48 hours. Use a gentle moisturiser and sunscreen every morning, because Gurgaon sun will tan your skin again fast if you skip it.

Questions we hear at the salon

How long does a de-tan session take?

A face de-tan takes 45 minutes to an hour. Adding neck and arms takes a little longer. We tell you the time when you book, so you can plan around it.

Will one session remove my tan completely?

One session visibly lightens fresh tan and evens out your tone. Older, deeper tan may need two or three sessions spaced a week apart. We will tell you honestly after seeing your skin.

Is de-tan safe for sensitive skin?

Yes, for most people. Tell us about any sensitivity, allergies, or active breakouts before we start, and we will pick a gentler product and test it on a small area first.

Can I get a de-tan the day before Karva Chauth?

Yes, but two days before is better. It gives your skin a day to settle, and any mild redness fades. Book early, because the days before Karva Chauth fill fast.
