Hands get noticed more than you think. At work, at weddings, every time you hand someone a cup of chai. Feet carry you through Gurgaon summers in sandals and through wedding season in heels. Yet most people book a manicure and pedicure in Sector 54 only when an event forces it. Here is the thing: regular nail care is cheap, it takes two hours, and it changes how put-together you look every single day. This is what a proper session includes, how to pick the right option, and how to spot a salon that takes hygiene seriously.

01 What a proper manicure includes

A manicure is not just nail polish. It starts with shaping the nails to a length and shape that suits your hands, then a warm soak to soften the skin. The cuticles get pushed back and cleaned, never cut aggressively, because deep cutting causes infections. Then comes exfoliation of the hands, a proper massage with cream, and finally polish or gel if you want it. The massage is the part people underestimate. Hands hold tension like shoulders do, and a good five minutes of kneading makes the whole service worth it. A basic manicure takes 45 minutes to an hour. Anything much faster is being rushed.

02 What a proper pedicure includes

A pedicure follows the same logic, with extra attention to the parts shoes punish. After the soak, the feet get scrubbed to remove dead skin, calluses get filed down, and the heels get worked on. Toenails are trimmed straight across, which prevents ingrown nails, and the cuticles are cleaned. Then a foot and lower-leg massage, which feels amazing after a day in heels or on your feet at work. The whole thing takes about an hour. If your heels are badly cracked, tell the therapist before the session. Mild cracks improve with a good pedicure, but deep, painful cracks need a dermatologist first.

03 Basic, spa, or gel: which one to pick

The menu can be confusing, so here is the honest breakdown. A basic manicure and pedicure covers shaping, cuticle care, massage, and regular polish. A spa version adds exfoliating scrubs, hydrating masks, and sometimes warm towels, which is worth it for dry hands and feet in winter. Gel polish goes on top of either option: it is cured under a lamp, lasts two to three weeks without chipping, and costs extra. Pick gel when you need the staying power, like a wedding or a trip. For everyday life, regular polish is cheaper and gentler on the nails. Do not let anyone talk you into gel you do not need.

04 Hygiene: check before you sit down

This matters more for nails than almost any other salon service, because tools touch broken skin. Before you book a manicure and pedicure in Sector 54, look around. Metal tools should come out of a steriliser or a sealed packet, not a drawer. Files and buffers should be single-use or freshly replaced. The foot tub should be cleaned between clients. If the salon looks annoyed by the question, leave. At our salon, tools are sterilised between every client, and we are happy to show you. Good salons expect these questions. Only bad ones get defensive about them.

05 How often to book

Every three to four weeks is the sweet spot for most people. Nails grow out, polish chips, and cuticles need attention on that cycle. If you get gel, book your removal and re-do together so you are not tempted to peel the gel off at home, which damages the nail surface. In wedding season or summer sandal season, some people go every two to three weeks. There is no rule beyond what your hands and feet need. If you are booking a manicure and pedicure in Sector 54 for the first time, watch for hangnails, dry cuticles, and rough heels. Those are your signs that it is time to book.

The gel removal rule: never peel gel polish off yourself. It takes the top layer of your nail with it and leaves the nail thin and weak for months. A proper soak-off takes 15 minutes at the salon and costs little. Always worth it.

How much does a manicure and pedicure in Sector 54 cost?

How long does a manicure and pedicure take?

Plan for about two hours when you book both together. A manicure alone takes 45 minutes to an hour, a pedicure about an hour. Rushing either one is how cuticles get nicked, so give it the full time.

Gel or regular polish, which lasts longer?

Gel lasts two to three weeks without chipping; regular polish lasts three to five days. Gel costs more and needs proper removal, so pick it when you need the staying power, like a wedding or a trip. For everyday, regular polish is fine and easier on the nails.

Is a pedicure safe if I have cracked heels?

A professional pedicure is the right fix for mildly cracked heels, because the soaking, exfoliation, and moisturising are done properly. But deep cracks that bleed or hurt should be seen by a dermatologist first. Tell the therapist about any cracks before the session starts.
