01 The trial comes first, four to six weeks out

Book the trial four to six weeks before the wedding, because that leaves time to change the look if it does not feel right. A trial too close to the wedding leaves no room to fix anything, and a trial too early means your skin and your outfit plans may still change. Four to six weeks is the sweet spot, and it also gives the artist time to order any product your skin needs.

Call to book the trial, rather than walking in, because a trial needs a proper slot of two to three hours and the artist needs to be free. Tell the salon your wedding date and your functions when you call, so the trial is planned around the real day. A weekday trial is usually calmer than a weekend one, and it gives the artist more time with you.

02 Lock the wedding day slot right after the trial

Once the trial goes well, lock the wedding day slot on the same day, because good bridal slots fill fast in wedding season and the artist you liked may not be free if you wait. The wedding day booking should cover all your functions, not just the main day, so the artist can plan the timings for each function in one schedule.

When you lock the slot, tell the salon how many people need looks, because mothers, sisters, and friends all need time on the day, and the schedule has to fit everyone. A written plan with timings for each person and each function keeps the morning calm, while a booking that only covers the bride leaves the rest of the family scrambling.

03 What to do in the weeks before the wedding

In the weeks before the wedding, look after your skin, because makeup sits far better on healthy skin. Drink enough water, get a facial or a cleanup two weeks before the wedding, and avoid trying any new skin product in the last week, because a reaction days before the wedding is the last thing you need. Tell the artist about your skin routine at the trial, so the makeup is planned around it.

Book your hair spa sessions in the same weeks, because healthy hair holds the bridal hairstyle better, and the two plans should run together. The last hair spa should be two to three days before the first function, and the last facial or cleanup should be at least a week before, so the skin is settled and not sensitive on the day.
