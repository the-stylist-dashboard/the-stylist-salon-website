01 Hair first: cuts, colour, and care

Most visits start with hair, and it is where a good salon earns trust. A cut that suits your face, colour that flatters your skin tone, and regular care like a hair spa or head massage keep hair looking good between visits. If you are new to a salon, start with a cut or a hair spa before committing to a big colour change. You will learn how the stylist works and whether you trust their judgement.

Ask questions during the consultation. A good stylist will explain what they plan to do and why, and will tell you if your idea needs adjusting. That honesty is worth more than agreement that ends in regret.

02 Skin: facials, cleanups, and waxing

Facials and cleanups serve different needs. A cleanup is a quicker, deeper cleanse for congested skin, while a facial adds massage, masks, and relaxation, and suits skin that needs hydration or glow before an event. If your skin is sensitive or acne-prone, tell the therapist first so products can be matched to you.

Waxing, threading, and eyebrow shaping make the biggest daily difference. Clean brows frame the whole face, and regular waxing keeps regrowth finer over time. Book these with your hair appointment.

03 Nails: manicure, pedicure, and gel

Nails are the detail people notice in handshakes, photos, and close conversation. A regular manicure keeps cuticles neat and nails shaped, while a pedicure takes care of feet that spend all day in closed shoes. Gel nails suit anyone who wants colour that lasts two to three weeks without chipping, which is handy before travel or events.

If you are trying gel for the first time, keep the first set simple. A classic shade lets you judge how your nails respond and whether the length feels comfortable, then go bolder next time.

04 Makeup and mehndi for events

For weddings, engagements, and festivals, makeup and mehndi need planning, not last-minute panic. Book a makeup trial if the event matters, and bring your outfit or a photo of it so the artist can match tones properly. Mehndi should be booked a day or two before the event so the colour deepens in time.

Our artists cover bridal, party, and engagement makeup, plus bridal and party mehndi. For Karva Chauth, Diwali, and wedding season, slots go fast, so call early. One salon handling hair, makeup, and mehndi keeps the whole look coordinated.

05 How to plan a full appointment day

Order matters when you combine services. Do hair colour before a facial, since colour can irritate freshly treated skin, and do nails last so wet polish is not ruined by hair washing. For a hair spa plus facial in one visit, allow at least three hours.

Questions we hear at the salon

Can I get a facial and hair colour on the same day?

Yes, but do the colour first. Colour chemicals can irritate skin that has just had a facial. Tell us when booking so we can order the services correctly and leave enough time between them.

Do you offer bridal packages?

Yes. Bridal packages combine hair, makeup, mehndi, and skin prep, built around your event dates. Book early during wedding season, and ask for a trial so the final look holds no surprises.

How early should I book for a wedding?

For bridal services, four to six weeks ahead is safest, with a trial one to two weeks before the event. For regular appointments, a few days ahead is usually enough, except on weekends.
