Bridal Packages in Gurugram: What's Usually Included

The Stylist Unisex Salon · 28 September 2026 · 6 min read

You are getting married in December and three salons have sent you three completely different package lists. One says HD makeup plus hair. Another lists pre-bridal, trial, draping and a touch-up kit. The third is twice as long and you do not recognise half the terms. This is normal. Bridal packages in Gurugram are built from the same basic blocks, just bundled differently. Learn the blocks and you can compare any two packages in five minutes.

01 The wedding-day core

Almost every bridal package starts with the same four things: makeup, hairstyling, draping and lashes. The makeup is usually listed as traditional, HD or airbrush. Ask what that actually means at the salon you are considering, because the labels get used loosely. A good artist will tell you which finish suits your skin and your venue lighting instead of pushing the priciest option.

  • Makeup for the day: base, eyes, lips and setting, matched to your outfit colours.
  • Hairstyling: chosen after a trial, factoring in your jewellery, neckline and how long the function runs.
  • Draping: saree or lehenga dupatta pleating and pinning so it survives hugs and dancing.
  • Lashes, nails and setting: false lashes, a nail polish touch-up and bindi or accessory placement.
  • Touch-up kit: a small pouch with blotting sheets, lipstick, safety pins and tissues for the day.

02 The pre-bridal sessions

Pre-bridal is the part that happens in the weeks before the wedding. It is where the real glow comes from, not the wedding morning. Sessions are spaced out so your skin gets recovery time between treatments, which is why pre-bridal starts early and never in the wedding week itself.

  • Cleanups or facials: spaced across the month for clarity and even tone. Our one-month bridal skincare timeline maps this out week by week.
  • Waxing and threading: full body waxing and brow shaping, timed so redness settles.
  • Hair prep: a hair spa or colour refresh so hair photographs with shine.
  • Manicure and pedicure: hands show up in every ring photo.
  • Body polishing or bleach: offered by some salons for arms, back and neckline.

03 The fine print most brides miss

Two packages with the same headline price can be very different once you read the details. These are the questions that separate a smooth wedding morning from a stressful one.

  • Who is the actual artist? The person in the trial should be the person on your wedding day. Get the name.
  • Is a trial included? Some packages charge for it separately. Never skip it.
  • Start time and duration: confirm when the artist arrives and how long the look takes.
  • Venue travel charges: getting ready at the venue often adds a fee.
  • Family makeup: ask the rate for your mother or sister upfront.
  • Cancellation terms: know what happens if dates shift.

04 Booking during Gurugram's wedding season

November to February is peak wedding season here and good artists book out early. Lock your date six to eight weeks ahead, and do the trial only after your outfit and jewellery are final. Bring a fabric swatch and photos of the jewellery to the trial. If any new product will touch your skin, ask for a patch test a day or two before the trial itself.

Tip: Schedule the trial on a day you have evening plans. You will see how the makeup wears over eight hours, which is exactly what the wedding day demands.

Questions we hear at the salon

What is usually included in a bridal package in Gurugram?

A typical package covers wedding-day makeup, hairstyling, outfit draping, false lashes and a touch-up kit. Pre-bridal sessions add facials or cleanups, waxing, threading, hair spa, manicure and pedicure. Trials, venue travel and family makeup are sometimes charged separately, so always ask for the full written list before paying an advance.

Should I book a makeup trial before the wedding?

Yes. A trial shows how the makeup photographs, how long it lasts and whether your skin reacts to the products. Do it after your outfit and jewellery are final, and wear it for a full evening. If the artist cannot match the trial look on the wedding day, you want to know that weeks early, not hours before the baraat.

How far in advance should I book my bridal package?

For a November to February wedding in Gurugram, book six to eight weeks ahead. Top artists fill their calendars early in peak season. Pre-bridal sessions need that lead time anyway, since facials and hair treatments are spaced across weeks. Last-minute bookings are possible in off-season months, but your choice of artist and trial slots will be limited.

What is the difference between bridal and pre-bridal packages?

Bridal covers the wedding day itself: makeup, hairstyling, draping, lashes and touch-up kit. Pre-bridal covers the preparation in the weeks before: facials or cleanups, waxing, threading, hair spa, manicure and pedicure. Most brides book both. Skipping pre-bridal and relying on one heavy makeup session is the most common regret we hear after weddings.
