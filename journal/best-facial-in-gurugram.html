01 Why skin type decides the facial

Skin type decides the products, the massage pressure, and how long each step lasts. Dry skin needs gentle cleansing and rich moisture, while oily skin needs a deeper cleanse and a lighter finish. Sensitive skin needs a slower pace and fewer active products. A facial that ignores skin type can leave skin red, patchy, or broken out, even if the treatment name sounded premium.

Tell your therapist about recent breakouts, allergies, medicines on the skin, and any facial or cleanup you had in the last few weeks. That history changes what is safe to use. If you have an active rash or a skin infection, a good therapist will ask you to wait rather than push ahead with the booking.

02 Facial, cleanup, and de-tan: what is different

A facial is a full routine: cleanse, exfoliation, massage, mask, and finish. A cleanup is shorter and focuses on clearing clogged pores and surface dullness, and it suits clients who want a quick refresh without a long session. A de-tan targets tanned, uneven skin from sun exposure and works on the outer layer to bring back an even tone.

Pick by need, not by name. If your skin feels dull after weeks of heat and dust, a cleanup may be enough. If your skin feels dry and tired, or you have a function coming up, a full facial gives more time for massage and masking. Ask the therapist to explain the difference for your skin, and pick the shorter option if you are short on time.

03 When to book before Diwali or a wedding

Book a facial three to five days before a function, not on the day itself. Skin needs a day or two to settle, and a facial done too close to an event can leave mild redness that shows in photos. For a festive function, book in the week before, so your skin looks calm and fresh for the functions.

If you have never had a facial at a salon before, book your first one even earlier, at least a week before the event. First-time skin reactions are uncommon but possible, and an early booking gives time to settle. After the facial, avoid harsh scrubs and strong home remedies for a day or two, and let the skin rest.

04 Aftercare, and booking in Gurugram

After a facial, keep it simple for a day: lukewarm water, no heavy makeup for a few hours, and sun protection when you step out. Drink water, avoid touching your face with unwashed hands, and skip steam rooms and harsh exfoliation for a day. The glow from a good facial shows best when the skin is left alone to settle.
