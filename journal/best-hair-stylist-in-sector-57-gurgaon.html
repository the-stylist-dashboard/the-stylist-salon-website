Best Hair Stylist in Sector 57, Gurgaon

The Stylist Unisex Salon · 20 October 2026 · 6 min read

Finding the best hair stylist in Sector 57 is not about fancy interiors or long menus. It is about one person with scissors who listens, understands your hair, and gives you a cut that still looks good two weeks later. Here is how to judge that before you sit in the chair.

01 How to tell a good stylist from an average one

Watch the first five minutes. A good stylist asks questions before touching your hair: how it behaves on humid days, how often you wash it, what you hated about your last cut. An average one says "okay, what do you want" and starts cutting. That conversation decides whether the cut works with your life or only looks good in the salon mirror.

Look at real customer work, not magazine shots. Ask to see photos of cuts on hair like yours, curly, thick, thin, or chemically treated. The best hair stylist in Sector 57 for you is the one whose portfolio matches your hair and the look you want to live with.

02 What to ask in the consultation

Come with three photos of what you want and one honest sentence about what you will not do at home. Tell the stylist if you never use a dryer, wash every morning, or tie it up most days. An honest answer back sounds like: "That cut needs blow-drying, so let me adjust it for air-drying." A bad answer is "yes, no problem" to everything.

Ask about grow-out too. A well-cut bob or layered cut should look fine for six to eight weeks. If the stylist cannot say how it will change as it grows, they are cutting for today, not for your month.

Try a trim first: before committing to a big change, book a small trim with a new stylist. It is the cheapest way to learn how they consult and handle your hair. Ten minutes of conversation tells you more than any review.

03 Fair prices around Sector 57

Pricing should be clear before the cut starts. Around Sector 57 and Golf Course Extension Road, a solid men's haircut runs roughly Rs 400 to Rs 800. A women's cut with a proper finish usually runs Rs 900 to Rs 2,000 depending on length. Colour and chemical work are quoted separately after the consultation.

Cheap prices often mean rushed appointments or skipped hygiene steps. Sky-high prices do not automatically mean skill either. The middle range with transparent pricing and a proper consultation is where the value sits.

04 Hygiene is a stylist's resume

You can learn a lot before anyone picks up scissors. Combs and brushes should come out of a clean container, not lie loose in a drawer. Capes should be fresh for each customer, not damp from the last one. The chair and mirror should be wiped down between clients, and clippers should be brushed clean.

This matters as much as skill. Dirty tools spread dandruff and scalp infections, and a salon that skips the basics is probably skipping other basics too. Do not feel awkward walking out if something feels off.
