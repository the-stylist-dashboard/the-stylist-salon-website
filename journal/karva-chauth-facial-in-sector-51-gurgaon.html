01 What a Karva Chauth facial actually does

A Karva Chauth facial is a deep cleanse, a gentle exfoliation, a relaxing face massage, and a glow mask, finished in about 60 to 75 minutes. It suits any woman who wants her skin to look fresh for the evening without redness or peeling. Done on 28 October, the day before the fast, it leaves your skin calm, soft, and ready for the sargi morning and the evening puja.

Our Sector 51 salon at Ground Floor, The Arzoo Apartments is an easy stop for most Sector 51 residents, and our Sector 52 salon near Artemis Hospital is close too. Call 81781 59945 and tell us it is for Karva Chauth, so we pick the right facial for your skin and leave enough time in the chair.

02 Which facial should you pick

If your skin looks dull after sun and late nights, a glow facial with de-tan steps is usually the best pick. It lifts surface tan, evens out the tone, and leaves a soft shine that shows up well in evening light. If you have blackheads around the nose and chin, ask us to add a cleanup first so the facial works on clean skin.

Tell us honestly about sensitive skin, recent threading, acne treatment, or any product that reacted badly in the past. We will not use a strong peel or a harsh scrub the day before a big event, because a calm face on Karva Chauth evening matters more than an aggressive treatment. For very sensitive skin, a gentle hydrating facial is the safer choice, and it still gives a visible glow.

03 Why the day before beats the same day

Karva Chauth falls on Thursday 29 October this year, and the fasting day is long. A facial on the same day leaves no room for your skin to settle, and you will be rushing between the puja prep and the salon chair. Booking on Wednesday 28 October means you wake up on the fast day with settled, rested skin and one less thing to worry about.

If you have already missed the day-before slot, a gentle cleanup on the morning of the 29th is a safer option than a full facial. We are open Wednesdays to Mondays from 11 AM to 9 PM and closed on Tuesdays, so plan around that. Evening slots in Karva Chauth week fill first, so call early rather than walking in and hoping.

04 What happens during your appointment

We start with a short talk about your skin: how it behaves, what you use at home, and what you want for the evening. Then comes cleansing, light steam, extraction only where needed, a face massage that helps you relax, and a mask picked for your skin type. We finish with moisturiser and sunscreen, and we tell you what not to put on your face afterwards.

The whole visit takes 60 to 75 minutes, and a cleanup alone takes about 30 to 40 minutes. You can add threading or eyebrow shaping in the same visit to save a second trip. Just tell us when you book on 81781 59945 so we plan the order properly and keep the visit comfortable.

05 How to keep the glow through the fast and the evening

Drink plenty of water before the fast starts, use a light moisturiser in the morning, and do not scrub or pick at your face during the day. Skip any new cream or serum on the 29th itself, because a new product is the most common cause of a surprise reaction before an event. A good night of sleep on the 28th helps your skin as much as the facial does.

One more thing worth knowing: our flat 40% off runs till 30 October only, so a facial booked on 28 October counts. It does not extend to Diwali, so if you are planning a Diwali facial too, that will be a separate visit at the regular price. Book the Karva Chauth facial first, while slots are still open.

The one-day rule: If you are trying a new facial for the first time, do it at least a day before any big event, never on the day itself. Your skin deserves a day to settle.

Questions we hear at the salon

Should I get a facial on Karva Chauth day itself?

It is better to get it the day before, on 28 October, so your skin has time to settle. If you can only come on the 29th, ask for a gentle cleanup in the morning instead of a full facial.

Will a facial cause pimples before the evening?

Mild redness for an hour or two is normal, but real breakouts are rare when the facial suits your skin. Tell us about sensitive skin or acne treatment when you book, and we will keep the treatment gentle.

How long does a Karva Chauth facial take?

A full facial takes about 60 to 75 minutes. A cleanup alone takes 30 to 40 minutes. If you add threading or eyebrow shaping, allow a little extra time.

Can I get threading done in the same visit?

Yes, and many women do. Book both together on 81781 59945 so we plan the order and leave enough time for each service without rushing.
