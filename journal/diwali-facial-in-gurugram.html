01 Pick a facial for your skin, not for the name

Dry skin needs gentle cleansing and rich moisture, oily skin needs a deeper cleanse and a lighter finish, and sensitive skin needs a slower pace with fewer active products. Tell your therapist about recent breakouts, allergies, and any facial or cleanup in the last few weeks, because that history changes what is safe. A facial picked from a menu name alone can leave skin red or patchy.

If you have never had a facial before, book your first one at least a week before Diwali, so any first-time reaction has time to settle. A cleanup may suit a quick refresh, while a full facial suits a function week because it gives more time for massage and masking. Ask the therapist to explain the difference for your skin before you decide.

02 The week before Diwali: a simple plan

In the week before Diwali, book your facial first, then plan nails and hair around it, so the freshest services land closest to your main function. Avoid trying a new scrub, a new home remedy, or a strong new product in that week, because new products can irritate skin right before photos. Keep your routine simple: gentle cleansing, moisture, and sun protection when you step out.

After the facial, keep it simple for a day: lukewarm water, no heavy makeup for a few hours, and no harsh exfoliation. Drink water, avoid touching your face with unwashed hands, and skip steam rooms for a day. The glow from a good facial shows best when the skin is left alone to settle.

03 Diwali glow without overdoing it

More treatments in one week do not mean more glow. A facial plus a cleanup plus a de-tan in the same few days can tire the skin and cause redness, so pick one main skin service for Diwali week and keep the rest simple. If your skin looks tanned or uneven, a de-tan can be planned earlier in the week, with the facial closer to the function, but ask your therapist to space them sensibly.

Eat and sleep normally in Diwali week if you can, because late nights and heavy food show on the skin too. A facial helps, but it cannot undo a week of no sleep on its own. A calm plan, with services spaced out and booked early, gives a better Diwali look than a rushed last-minute round of treatments.
