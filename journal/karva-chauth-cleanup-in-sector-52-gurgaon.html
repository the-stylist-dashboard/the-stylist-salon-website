Karva Chauth Cleanup in Sector 52, Gurgaon

01 Why a cleanup works on the day itself

A Karva Chauth cleanup is a gentle cleanse, light steam, and careful extraction only where needed, finished in about 30 to 40 minutes. It suits any woman who wants fresh, clean skin today without the redness a full facial can leave behind. Done this morning, it leaves your face settled well before the puja muhurat at 5:38 PM.

A full facial needs a day for skin to settle, but a cleanup is lighter and kinder. We do not use strong peels or harsh scrubs on Karva Chauth day itself, because your skin has to look calm under evening light and in photos. The aim is clean pores, a soft texture, and a rested look, not a dramatic change.

If you had a facial yesterday, a cleanup today is not needed. Come in only if your skin feels dull, or you have visible blackheads around the nose and chin. Tell us what you already did this week, and we will be honest about whether you need anything more today.

02 What happens in your 30 to 40 minute visit

We start with a short talk about your skin and anything you are sensitive to. Then comes cleansing, light steam, extraction only where it is safe, and a soothing mask picked for your skin type. We finish with moisturiser and sunscreen, and we tell you what to skip for the rest of the day.

You can add threading or eyebrow shaping in the same visit, but say so when you book so we plan the order. Threading first and cleanup after is usually the wrong order, so we will set it right for you. Call 81781 59945 and tell us you need a Karva Chauth morning slot.

03 How to keep skin calm through the fast

Drink water before the fast starts, use a light moisturiser in the morning, and do not try any new cream or serum today. A new product is the most common cause of a surprise reaction before an event, and today is not the day to test one. Skip scrubbing or picking at your face during the day.

If your skin feels warm after the cleanup, a cool splash of water and plain moisturiser is enough. Do not pile on layers of makeup straight away. Let the skin breathe for an hour, then do your regular makeup as usual.

04 When to come in today

We are open today, Thursday 29 October, from 11 AM to 9 PM, but the useful window for fasting clients is 11 AM to about 5:30 PM, before the puja muhurat at 5:38 PM. Evening slots between the puja and moonrise do not suit most fasting clients, so morning and early afternoon chairs go first.

Our Sector 52 salon is at Shop No. 86, High Street 52, opposite Artemis Hospital, and our Sector 51 salon is at Ground Floor, The Arzoo Apartments. Both are a short drive for most Sector 52 residents. Book by phone rather than walking in and hoping, because today fills fast.

05 What about the 40 percent off

Our flat 40 percent off runs till 30 October only, so a cleanup booked today counts. It does not extend to Diwali, so a Diwali facial will be a separate visit at the regular price unless the offer is extended. Book today first, while morning slots are still open.

If you are also planning Diwali skin prep, tell us today and we can talk through the right gap between treatments. Skin that is rested between visits always looks better than skin that is over-treated in one week.

The morning rule: On the day of a big event, pick the gentlest treatment that still does the job. Calm skin photographs better than over-treated skin.

Questions we hear at the salon

Is a cleanup safe on Karva Chauth day itself?

Yes, when it is kept gentle. We skip strong peels and harsh scrubs on the day itself, so your skin stays calm for the evening puja.

How long does a Karva Chauth cleanup take?

About 30 to 40 minutes on its own. Add 15 to 20 minutes if you also book threading or eyebrow shaping in the same visit.

Can I wear makeup right after a cleanup?

Wait about an hour, then do your regular makeup as usual. Skip any brand new cream or serum today, because a new product can react unexpectedly.

What time should I book today?

Morning or early afternoon is best. We are open 11 AM to 9 PM, but the puja muhurat starts at 5:38 PM, so aim to finish by about 5:30 PM.
