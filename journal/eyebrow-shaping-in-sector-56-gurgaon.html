01 How we shape your brows

Eyebrow shaping is the careful removal of stray hair to give your brows a clean, natural line that suits your face. It takes about 10 to 15 minutes, suits almost everyone, and makes the biggest small difference to how your face looks in photos. Done well, people notice you look fresh without knowing why.

We start by looking at your natural brow line, your brow bone, and the shape of your face. Then we clean up stray hair above and below the brow, and shape slowly, checking with you as we go. Nothing is taken off without you knowing, because a brow that is over-plucked takes weeks to grow back.

Bring a photo if you have a shape you like, but be ready for honest advice. A shape that suits someone else may not suit your face, and we will tell you that plainly. The goal is a brow that looks like yours, only tidier.

02 Thick, thin, or somewhere in between

Thick brows suit many faces, but only when the edges are clean. Thin brows can look neat too, if the line is even and the arch sits in the right place. What matters most is symmetry and a line that follows your own brow bone, not a trend from a photo.

If you are growing your brows out after over-plucking, tell us. We will only remove the obvious stray hair and leave the rest alone. In a few weeks you will have a fuller line to shape properly, and the wait is worth it.

03 Before a wedding or festive evening

For Karva Chauth today, Diwali on 8 November, or a wedding function, book eyebrow shaping a day before if you can. That leaves any mild redness plenty of time to settle. If you are coming on the day itself, book in the morning, not the late afternoon.

Pair eyebrow shaping with upper lip threading in the same visit if you need both, and add a cleanup only if your skin needs it. One well-planned visit beats two rushed ones, especially in festive week when chairs fill fast.

04 Keeping brows tidy between visits

Most people need shaping every three to four weeks, depending on how fast their hair grows. Between visits, do not pluck from the middle of the brow yourself. Tidy only the obvious stray hair well away from the main line, or leave it and let us handle it.

A spoolie brush and a little brow gel keep the shape in place day to day. If you fill your brows with pencil or powder, fill lightly and follow the natural line. Heavy filling on a poorly shaped brow only draws attention to the shape.

Questions we hear at the salon

How long does eyebrow shaping take?

About 10 to 15 minutes on its own. Add a few minutes if you also book upper lip threading in the same visit.

How often should I get my brows shaped?

Most people need it every three to four weeks. Fast hair growth may need a visit every three weeks, slower growth can stretch to four or five.

Will my brows look too thin?

Not if we shape to your natural line. We check with you as we go, and we will not take off more than you agree to.

Can I get brows done on Karva Chauth day?
