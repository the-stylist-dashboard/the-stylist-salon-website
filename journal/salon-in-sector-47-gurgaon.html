01 What to expect from a salon visit near Sector 47

A first visit should start with a talk, not with scissors or a brush. The stylist should ask what you want, look at your hair or skin, and tell you plainly what will suit you before any service starts. That short talk is what separates a careful salon from one that rushes through appointments, and it is the reason regular clients keep coming back to the same chair.

Clean tools and a tidy station matter just as much as skill. A good salon sterilises its metal tools, uses fresh towels and capes, and keeps the work area clean between clients. If the station looks untidy or the tools look worn, it is better to walk away, because dirty tools can cause skin trouble that takes weeks to clear up.
