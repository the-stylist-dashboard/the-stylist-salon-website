01 The consultation test

The first five minutes tell you everything. A good stylist asks what you like, what you hate, how you style your hair at home, and studies your hair's texture before suggesting anything. A careless one asks "short or long?" and starts cutting. Consultation is not small talk. It is the stylist reading your hair, your face shape, and your routine, then matching a cut to all three. If the consultation is skipped, the cut is a guess. We never skip it, even for regulars, because hair changes with the seasons, colour history, and stress.

03 Honest advice beats upselling

The clearest sign of a top stylist is what they talk you out of. Hair that is already damaged should not get another round of bleach that day, no matter what the photo shows. Fine hair does not hold the same layers as thick hair. A stylist who says "let us fix the health first" is protecting your hair and their reputation. Questions we hear at the salon

How can I judge a stylist before the haircut starts?

Watch the consultation. A good stylist studies your hair texture, asks about your routine and past cuts, and explains what will and will not work. If they skip straight to cutting without questions, that is your answer. You can also ask to see photos of their recent work.

Should a stylist ever say no to what I ask for?

Yes, and the good ones do. If a style will not suit your face shape or your hair cannot handle the process, an honest stylist says so and suggests what will work instead. A stylist who agrees to everything is not being polite; they are avoiding the hard conversation.
