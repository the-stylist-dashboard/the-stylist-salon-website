01 What sangeet makeup is

Sangeet makeup is evening makeup built for a night of music and dancing, so it uses a long-wear base, a defined eye, and a lip colour that stays put through dinner, dancing, and photographs. It suits brides who want a fun look for the sangeet, and guests who want to look their best without going as heavy as a bridal look. The finish is polished and camera-friendly, because the sangeet means a lot of photographs and video.

The look is planned around your outfit and your dance, so bring a photo of what you will wear, or tell the artist the colour and the style. A bright outfit suits softer eyes and a stronger lip, while a dark outfit suits a smoky eye and a nude lip, so the makeup and the outfit work together instead of fighting each other. The artist will also check your skin type, because oily skin needs a different base than dry skin.

02 Colours and finish for dancing

For a sangeet, the makeup needs to survive sweat, heat, and hours of movement, so the base is set with powder and the eye makeup is fixed with a long-wear liner and mascara. Shimmer on the eyes suits the stage lights, and a bold lip adds colour that shows up well in photographs. The trick is to keep the base light enough to breathe, while making the eyes and lips strong enough to last the night.

If you are performing, tell the artist, because stage makeup needs to be a little stronger than guest makeup, so the face reads clearly from a distance and on video. False lashes are a good add-on for a performance, because they open up the eyes and show up well in photographs. Keep a small powder compact and your lip colour in your bag for a quick touch-up between songs.

03 Hair pairing for the sangeet night

Sangeet makeup looks best with hair that is styled to match, such as soft curls, a braided updo, or a half-up style that stays neat through dancing. Book the hair and the makeup together in one visit, so the stylist and the artist can plan the full look as one, and so the timing works out without a rush between services. A combined booking also means you leave the salon ready for the evening.

Keep your jewellery in mind when you pick the lip colour, because a bold lip needs simpler jewellery, while a soft lip leaves room for statement earrings. If you are unsure, ask the artist to show you two options, one bold and one soft, and pick the one that feels more like you. The right look is the one you feel comfortable dancing in all evening, not just the one that looks best in a photo.

Questions people ask

How is sangeet makeup different from party makeup?

Sangeet makeup is bolder and built to last through dancing and stage lights, while party makeup is lighter and made for a few hours at a dinner or a gathering.

Will my sangeet makeup last through dancing?

Yes, the base is set with powder and the eye makeup is long-wear, so it holds through hours of dancing. Keep a compact and your lip colour handy for a quick touch-up.

Should I book a trial for sangeet makeup?

Yes, a trial lets you see the look in daylight and change anything you do not like. It also helps the artist learn your skin, so the final look is smoother and faster.
