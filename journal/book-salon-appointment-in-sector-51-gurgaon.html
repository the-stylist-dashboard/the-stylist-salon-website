02 How to book in one call

Call 81781 59945 and tell us three things: what you need, which day, and what time suits you. For example, cleanup plus threading today between 11 AM and 1 PM, or a hair spa tomorrow. We will confirm the time, the location, and how long to allow, all in that one call.

If you are booking for more than one person, say so at the start. We can often seat two people together or back to back, but only if we know in advance. The same goes for a service mix like facial plus threading, where the order matters and we need extra time.

03 What to book for Karva Chauth day

For today, the safest picks are a gentle cleanup, threading, eyebrow shaping, and a hair spa. These leave skin and hair settled well before the evening. A full facial is better done the day before, so if you missed yesterday, a cleanup this morning is the smarter choice.

If you need mehndi, be aware that mehndi needs hours to develop a deep stain, so it is not a same-day evening job. For Diwali and wedding functions ahead, book mehndi a day or two before the event, not on the morning of it.

Our flat 40 percent off runs till 30 October only, so an appointment booked for today or tomorrow counts. It does not cover Diwali on 8 November, so do not plan a Diwali booking expecting the same discount unless the offer is extended. We will be straight about that when you call.

If you want both a Karva Chauth visit and a Diwali visit, book the Karva Chauth one first, while the offer and the slots are still there. Then we can talk through a Diwali date at the regular price, with no surprise at the chair.

05 If your plans change

Life changes, and we understand that. If you need to move a booking, call 81781 59945 as early as you can, and we will try to find another slot the same day or the next. Early notice also frees the chair for someone else waiting for a festive slot.

We are open Wednesdays to Mondays from 11 AM to 9 PM and closed on Tuesdays, so a Tuesday booking is not possible. Keep that in mind when you plan a Diwali week visit, and book ahead for the days around the festival.
