01 What a hair spa actually is

A hair spa is a deep wash, a relaxing head massage, a nourishing mask, and steam to help the mask sink in. It suits dry, frizzy, dull, or stressed hair, and anyone who wants softer, calmer hair before a function. It takes about 45 to 60 minutes, and you leave with hair that feels softer the same day and stays easier to manage for a couple of weeks.

From Sector 54, our Sector 52 salon at Shop No. 86, High Street 52, opposite Artemis Hospital, is a short drive along Golf Course Extension Road. Call 81781 59945 and tell us about your hair, and we will pick a mask that suits it and book a slot that leaves enough time.

02 Who benefits most from a hair spa

Coloured hair benefits a lot, because colour, heat styling, and hard Gurgaon water dry the hair out over time. A hair spa puts moisture back and makes coloured hair look fresher between colour visits. If you use a straightener or dryer daily, a regular hair spa helps undo some of that weekly damage.

A dry or itchy scalp is another good reason to book one. The head massage improves blood flow to the scalp, and the mask calms dryness. If you have a function coming up, a hair spa a day or two before leaves your hair soft, settled, and easy to style on the day itself.

Men benefit too. Short hair still gets dry from sun, hard water, and daily washing, and a hair spa keeps it soft and easy to shape. Many of our male clients book a hair spa with their regular haircut, and it adds only about 45 minutes to the visit.

03 What happens step by step

We start by washing your hair and checking its condition: dry ends, oily scalp, recent colour, or past smoothening all change which mask we use. Then comes the head massage, which most clients say is the best part of the visit. It relaxes you and helps the scalp absorb the treatment that follows.

Next we apply the mask from roots to ends, and use steam so it sinks in properly. After a short rest, we rinse it out and finish with a blow dry so you can see and feel the result before you leave. The whole visit takes 45 to 60 minutes, and you walk out with hair that feels noticeably softer.

04 Hair spa versus head massage

A head massage on its own is mainly about relaxation and scalp care. It uses oil or cream, eases tension, and leaves your scalp feeling fresh. It is a great pick if you mainly want to unwind after a long week, and it pairs well with a haircut.

A hair spa goes further. It adds a deep wash, a treatment mask, and steam, so it actually repairs and softens the hair, not just the scalp. If your hair feels rough, dull, or hard to manage, the hair spa is the better choice. You can also combine the two: a longer head massage inside a hair spa visit, if you ask when you book.

Questions we hear at the salon

How long does a hair spa take?

About 45 to 60 minutes, including the wash, head massage, mask, steam, and blow dry. If you add a haircut, allow extra time.

Is a hair spa good for hair fall?

It helps when hair fall is linked to dryness, a dry scalp, or breakage from heat styling. If hair fall is heavy or sudden, we will tell you honestly and suggest you check with a doctor too.

How often should I get a hair spa?

Once a month works well for most people. If your hair is coloured or heat styled daily, every three to four weeks keeps it in better shape.

Do you offer a head massage with a hair spa?

Yes, a relaxing head massage is part of every hair spa. If you want a longer massage, tell us when you book on 81781 59945 and we will allow extra time.
