01 Why one salon for everyone just works

Running the family across three different salons is a weekend nobody wants. A proper unisex salon handles men's and women's hair, skin, and nails under one roof, which means one booking, one trip, and one place that knows your household. Our stylists keep notes on regulars: which shade of brown you liked last time, how short your son wants his sides. That memory is the real convenience, not just the location.

Couples book back-to-back slots and walk out together. Parents bring kids for trims while getting their own services done. It sounds small, but it turns a salon visit from an errand into an hour that fits your day.

02 What to check before you trust a new salon

Unisex on the signboard means nothing by itself. Check the basics first. Tools should be clean and disinfected between clients, capes should be fresh, and the wash area should not smell like yesterday. Pricing should be visible or quoted before the service starts, not revealed at the bill. Real Google reviews tell you more than any Instagram grid.

Watch how the stylist handles the first five minutes. Do they ask what you want and actually listen, or do they start cutting while pitching packages? A good salon consults first and sells second. If someone pushes a membership before touching your hair, that tells you what they care about.
