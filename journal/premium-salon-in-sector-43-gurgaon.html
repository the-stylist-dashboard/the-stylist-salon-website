Salon guides What premium should actually mean

Premium Salon in Sector 43, Gurgaon

The Stylist Unisex Salon · 25 October 2026 · 6 min read

Sector 43 sits close to Golf Course Extension Road and Sushant Lok, and many people here search for a premium salon. Premium is not a fancy chair or a scented towel. It is time, attention, and skill: a proper consultation, products matched to your hair, and a finish checked properly before you leave. If a salon charges premium prices but rushes you through in twenty minutes, you are paying for decor, not quality.

01 The consultation comes first

In a premium salon, the appointment starts with conversation, not scissors. Your stylist should ask about your hair history, past colour or treatments, daily routine, and what you liked and disliked about previous cuts. This takes ten minutes and prevents most bad results. If a stylist starts cutting without asking anything, that is a warning sign, whatever the price list says.

A proper consultation also includes honest limits: some looks need multiple sessions, some shades will not work on your base colour, and some styles need daily effort you may not want. Hearing that upfront is part of what you pay for.

02 Products matched to your hair

Premium salons use professional products and, more importantly, choose them for your hair type. Fine hair needs light formulas that do not weigh it down. Thick, dry hair needs richer masks and oils. Colour for Indian hair needs careful handling of warmth. One shelf of generic products used on everyone is not premium, it is just expensive.

Ask what is being used on your hair and why. A confident stylist will explain without hesitation. Mention allergies or sensitive skin at booking so a patch test can be arranged before colour or chemical treatments.

03 Time, not rush

The clearest difference between a premium salon and a budget one is time. A good cut takes forty-five minutes to an hour. Balayage takes two to three hours. A keratin treatment takes longer still. When appointments are stacked every thirty minutes, corners get cut, literally and figuratively.

At The Stylist, appointments are spaced so your stylist is not watching the clock. You get a proper wash, a careful cut, and a finish that is dried and checked, not rushed out damp. That time is the real product.

04 Clean tools and honest pricing

Premium also means hygiene you can see: clean combs, sanitised tools, fresh towels, and a tidy station. It sounds basic, but it is the first thing to check in any salon, and it matters more than the decor. Skin and scalp are sensitive, and clean tools prevent irritation and infection.

Pricing should be clear before work starts. Know the full cost of your cut, colour, or treatment at consultation, including add-ons. Surprise charges at the bill are not premium behaviour. Ask for the total upfront.

05 Who premium suits, and who does not need it

Premium suits anyone with a complex job: corrective colour, a major cut change, bridal styling, or damaged hair. It also suits people who value consistency and want the same stylist to know their hair over time.

If you just need a quick trim and your hair is healthy, a standard appointment may be all you need, and a good salon will tell you that. Premium should be a recommendation based on your hair, not a default upsell.

The premium test: Judge a premium salon by three things: the consultation, the time given, and the finish. If all three are careful and unhurried, the price is justified. If any one is missing, you are paying for the room, not the work.

Questions we hear at the salon

Is a premium salon worth the extra cost?

For complex work like corrective colour, bridal styling, or damaged hair repair, yes, because consultation, time, and skill directly affect the result. For a simple trim on healthy hair, a standard appointment is often enough.

How much more does a premium salon cost?

It depends on the service, not just the label. A cut may cost somewhat more than a budget salon, while colour and treatments vary with hair length and product use. Ask for the full price at consultation.
