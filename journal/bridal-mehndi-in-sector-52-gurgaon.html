Mehndi & wedding Mehndi that stays dark through every wedding function

Bridal Mehndi in Sector 52, Gurgaon

The Stylist Unisex Salon · 28 October 2026 · 6 min read

Bridal mehndi at The Stylist means full, detailed mehndi on both hands up to the elbows and both feet, done by an experienced mehndi artist at our Sector 52 salon. It suits any bride who wants a deep, even stain that holds up through every function, from sangeet to pheras. A full bridal set takes three to five hours depending on how much detail you choose, and you leave with your hands wrapped and clear aftercare so the colour deepens overnight.

01 What a full bridal set covers

A full bridal set at The Stylist covers both hands, front and back, up to the elbows, and both feet. Brides usually pick a mix of traditional motifs: paisleys, jaali work, peacocks, and small dulha and dulhan figures tucked into the design. Many brides also ask us to hide the groom's name or initials in the pattern, which guests love hunting for during the sangeet.

If you want something lighter on the arms and heavier on the palms, tell us at the start. We plan the design around your outfit so the mehndi and the lehenga sit well together in photos. Feet are often left for last, so plan to wear open sandals on the day.

02 How to get the darkest stain

Fresh mehndi starts out orange-brown and deepens over 24 to 48 hours. The darkest stains come from simple habits. Wash your hands before the appointment and skip hand cream that day, because lotion stops the mehndi from gripping the skin. Once the mehndi dries, we dab it with a lemon and sugar mix to keep it stuck to the skin longer.

Keep it on for at least six to eight hours, overnight if you can. Avoid water, soap, and scrubbing while the stain sets. Gentle warmth helps too: hold your hands near a warm pan or sip hot chai while you wait. Avoid waxing or threading on the same day as mehndi, since freshly treated skin can take the colour unevenly.

03 When to book around your wedding week

Book your bridal mehndi two to three days before the wedding, so the stain is at its darkest for the pheras and the reception. If your mehndi night falls earlier, tell us and we will work around it. Wedding season fills our artists' diaries fast, so call at least three to four weeks ahead to reserve your slot, especially for a Saturday.

When you call on 81781 59945, tell us your wedding date, whether you want feet done as well, and how detailed you want the design. That helps us block the right amount of time, because a full bridal set can run to five hours.

04 What happens at your appointment

We start by looking at photos you like and agreeing on the design, the coverage, and how heavy you want the palms. Then you settle in with your hands resting on cushions while the artist works. You can take breaks to stretch, drink water, or eat, just tell the artist before you move a hand.

Hands are done first, then feet, and we wrap your hands loosely before you leave so the mehndi stays put on the way home. You get clear aftercare: when to scrape it off, what to avoid, and how to keep the stain dark for the next few days.

05 Pairing mehndi with hair, skin and nails in the same week

A wedding week runs smoother when the order is right. Get your hair colour and hair spa done the week before, so nothing disturbs the mehndi later. Book a facial or cleanup two to three days before the wedding, but not on mehndi day. Threading and eyebrow shaping should be finished the day before mehndi, never after, because the mehndi needs clean, calm skin.

Nails are best done before mehndi day too, so polish and filing do not smudge fresh designs. If your mother or sisters want party mehndi, we can book them in the same week, often on the same day once your hands are done. Our Sector 52 salon sits opposite Artemis Hospital on High Street 52, so family coming from nearby sectors can reach us easily.

The overnight rule: scrape the dried mehndi off, do not wash it off. Rub a little mustard or coconut oil over the stain, keep water away for the first twelve hours, and the colour will deepen nicely by the next morning.

Questions we hear at the salon

How long does bridal mehndi take at your salon?

A full bridal set takes three to five hours, depending on how detailed the design is and whether you include feet. Lighter bridal designs take closer to three hours. We tell you the expected time when you book, so you can plan your day around it.

How many days before my wedding should I get mehndi?

Two to three days before the wedding is ideal. The stain deepens over 24 to 48 hours, so mehndi done two days before looks its darkest for the pheras and the reception.

Will the colour be dark enough for photos?

Yes, if you follow the aftercare. Keep the mehndi on overnight, avoid water for the first twelve hours after scraping it off, and use the lemon and sugar dab we give you. Most brides reach a rich dark brown by the second day.

Do you do bridal mehndi on feet as well?

Yes, feet are part of a full bridal set. Tell us when you book so we allow enough time, and wear open sandals on the day so nothing rubs the fresh design on the way home.
