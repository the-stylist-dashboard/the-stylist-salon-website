01 What a hair spa actually is

Let us clear up one thing first. A hair spa at The Stylist is a hair and scalp treatment, not a body treatment. We work on your hair and scalp only, and you walk out with softer, calmer hair.

A session starts with a wash to remove buildup, then a deep-conditioning mask is massaged into your scalp and lengths. Steam helps the product sink in properly instead of just sitting on the surface. After that comes a relaxing head massage, a final rinse, and a gentle blow dry so you can see the result straight away.

The point is repair and moisture. Gurgaon water is hard, the sun is harsh, and colour or heat styling dries hair out further. A hair spa puts back what daily life takes out.

02 Who benefits most from a hair spa

If your hair feels rough, tangles easily, or looks dull even after a wash, a hair spa will make a visible difference. Coloured or chemically treated hair benefits most, because colour and smoothening leave it more porous and thirsty.

It also helps if your scalp feels tight, itchy, or flaky. The massage step improves blood flow to the scalp and the mask calms irritation. It is not a medical treatment for hair fall or serious scalp conditions, and we will tell you honestly if what you need is a dermatologist instead of a salon chair.

For most people, once a month keeps hair in good shape. Very damaged hair may need one every two to three weeks at first, then monthly.

03 What happens during your appointment

Your visit starts with a quick look at your hair. We check texture, dryness, split ends, and any recent colour or keratin work, because that decides which mask we use. A fine, oily scalp needs a lighter product than thick, dry hair, and using the wrong one is how people end up with flat, greasy results.

Next comes the wash, the mask, and steam for about fifteen to twenty minutes, then the head massage. We finish with a rinse and a blow dry so you can see the result straight away.

The appointment takes about an hour. If you are adding a haircut or colour, say so when you book on 81781 59945 so we set aside enough time.

04 Hair spa or hair massage: which one do you need

People often mix these two up, so here is the simple difference. A hair massage focuses on the scalp massage itself, usually with oil, and it is great for relaxation and scalp health. A hair spa is the fuller treatment: wash, deep mask, steam, massage, and finish together.

If dryness, frizz, or damage is your main problem, go for the hair spa. If you mainly want to relax and look after your scalp, a head massage does the job.

Both treatments at our salon are for hair and scalp only. If you are unsure what you are booking, ask us on the phone before you come in.

05 How to keep the result going at home

Use a mild shampoo, lukewarm water rather than very hot water, and skip daily heat styling for a few days after your session. A small amount of serum on the ends after every wash keeps frizz down between visits.

Questions we hear at the salon

Is a hair spa a body treatment?

No. A hair spa is a hair and scalp treatment only: washing, a deep-conditioning mask, steam, a head massage, and a blow dry.

How long does a hair spa take?

About an hour on its own. If you add a haircut or colour, mention it when you book so we allow enough time.

How often should I get a hair spa?

Once a month suits most people. Very dry or damaged hair may need one every two to three weeks at first, then monthly.

Will a hair spa stop my hair fall?

It improves hair health and scalp condition, which helps, but it is not a medical cure for hair fall. If shedding is heavy or sudden, see a dermatologist.
