01 What a hair spa does for your hair

A hair spa adds moisture back to dry hair, smooths the outer layer so hair feels softer, and makes combing and styling easier. It helps with frizz, dryness, and dullness caused by Gurugram heat, dust, and daily styling. It does not change your hair colour or texture permanently, and it does not straighten your hair. It simply leaves your hair healthier and easier to manage for a few weeks.

A hair spa also cares for the scalp. The head massage helps blood flow to the scalp, and the mask is worked in so it reaches the hair evenly. If your scalp feels dry, itchy, or flaky, tell your stylist, because the mask and the massage pace can be adjusted. A hair spa is a hair and head massage treatment only, and it is for hair and scalp care.

02 Who a hair spa suits, and who should wait

A hair spa suits most people, especially those with dry, frizzy, coloured, or chemically treated hair. It suits clients who style their hair with heat often, and clients whose hair feels rough after a colour or a long summer. If your hair is healthy, short, and easy to manage, a spa is still pleasant, but you may need it less often.

Wait or ask first if you have an active scalp infection, open cuts on the scalp, or a recent reaction to a hair product. Also tell your stylist about recent colour, keratin, or smoothening work, because that changes which mask is safe. A good stylist will say plainly if your hair needs a different treatment first, rather than booking a spa that will not help.

03 How often a hair spa is worth it

For most people, a hair spa once a month keeps hair soft and manageable, especially in Gurugram heat and dust. If your hair is very dry, coloured, or exposed to daily heat styling, a session every three weeks may suit better. If your hair is healthy and short, a session every six weeks can be enough. Your stylist can set a gap that fits your hair, not a fixed package.

Do not book a hair spa every week just because an offer is running. Overdoing masks can leave fine hair flat and limp, and it wastes money. A monthly rhythm, with a simple home wash routine in between, gives better results than frequent heavy sessions. Ask for home care tips at your visit, so the spa result lasts longer.
