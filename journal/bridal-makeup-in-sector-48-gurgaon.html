01 When to book bridal makeup and what to ask first

Book bridal makeup as soon as your wedding date is fixed, especially for peak wedding season in Gurgaon. Good artists fill up months ahead, and a late booking often means settling for whoever is left.

Ask what is included: trial, hairstyling, draping, lashes, touch-up kit, and travel to your venue if needed. A quote that looks cheap but leaves out hair and draping is not really cheaper.

From Sector 48, come and meet us at our Sector 52 salon near Artemis Hospital, or our Sector 51 salon at Ground Floor, The Arzoo Apartments, and talk through your lehenga colour, your venue, and your ceremony timing before you pay anything.

02 What a makeup trial covers and why it matters

A trial is a full run-through before the wedding day. We test the base shade, eye look, lip colour, and hairstyle together, so you see the whole look, not just one piece of it.

Wear your trial makeup for a few hours, take photos in daylight and indoors, and see how it holds up. If the base feels heavy, or the lip shade washes you out in photos, we adjust it now, not on your wedding morning.

Bring your jewellery and a photo of your outfit if you can. Makeup that suits a red lehenga may not suit a pastel one, and the trial is where we get that balance right.

03 Looks that photograph well and last for hours

Wedding makeup needs to survive heat, tears, hugs, and hours of photos. That means a long-wear base, proper setting, and waterproof eye makeup, applied in layers rather than one thick coat.

For most brides, a soft glam look works best: even skin, defined eyes, and a lip colour that matches the outfit. Very heavy contour and very pale bases often look harsh in daylight photos.

Tell us about your skin type too. Oily skin needs a matte base and blotting papers in your touch-up kit, while dry skin needs more hydration under the base so it does not crack by evening.

04 Your wedding morning: how the day usually runs

On the wedding day, arrive with clean, moisturised skin and washed hair if we are doing your hairstyle. Eat something light first, because a long morning on an empty stomach helps no one.

We usually start with hair, then makeup, then draping and jewellery, leaving buffer time for photos. Tell us your photographer call time and your baraat or ceremony time when you book, so we plan backwards from there.

Bring your touch-up kit: lipstick, blotting paper, and a few pins.

05 Hair, skin prep, and the weeks before the wedding

Good bridal makeup starts weeks earlier. A facial or cleanup three to four weeks before, not the week of, gives skin time to settle. A hair spa or a trim in the same window keeps hair smooth for styling.

Avoid new skin products, new facials, or a brand-new hair colour in the final week. Last-minute experiments are the most common cause of wedding-week breakouts and colour surprises.

If you want party makeup for sangeet or engagement as well, book those looks together. It is easier to plan a set of looks that suit you than to start from scratch for each event, and our team along Golf Course Extension Road can cover the full wedding week.

The honest test: After your trial, take a photo in daylight and one indoors, then look at them an hour later. If you still like the look in both lights and your skin feels comfortable, the look is right.

Questions we hear at the salon

How far ahead should I book bridal makeup?

As soon as your date is fixed, ideally two to three months ahead for peak season. Late bookings are possible, but choice of artist and time slot gets tighter.

Is a makeup trial included in bridal makeup?

We recommend a trial for every bride, and it is quoted separately so you know exactly what it costs. A trial avoids surprises on the wedding day.

Do you travel to the venue for bridal makeup?

Yes, venue travel can be arranged for bridal parties in Gurgaon. Tell us your venue and start time when you book so we can confirm travel and timing.

What should I bring to my bridal trial?

Bring your outfit photo, jewellery, and any reference photos you like. If you have a skin concern, bring the products you normally use so we can plan around them.
