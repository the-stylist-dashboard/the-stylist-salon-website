01 What threading covers

Threading is a quick, precise way to shape eyebrows and clear fine facial hair, using a twisted cotton thread to lift hair from the root. It suits women who want clean lines without wax or cream on their skin. A full face threading visit takes about 20 to 30 minutes, and eyebrow shaping alone takes about 10 to 15 minutes.

At our salon, threading covers eyebrows, upper lip, chin, forehead, and full face, in any mix you need. Most clients book eyebrows plus upper lip together, because that pairing makes the whole face look tidy. Tell us the shape you like, or show us a photo, and we will shape to your face rather than forcing one standard arch.

If your skin is sensitive, say so before we start. We can work more gently, skip areas that are already irritated, and tell you honestly if a spot needs a day to rest. Threading on skin that has just had a peel or a strong facial is not a good idea, so tell us about any treatment this week.

02 How to avoid redness before a function

Redness for 20 to 30 minutes after threading is normal, especially on the upper lip. It settles on its own for most people. A cool splash of water and plain aloe or moisturiser helps, and you should skip makeup on the threaded area for at least an hour.

For a wedding, Karva Chauth, or Diwali evening, book threading a day before if you can, so any redness is long gone. If you are coming on the day itself, book in the morning. That leaves the whole afternoon for skin to settle before evening light and photos.

03 Eyebrows that suit your face

Good eyebrow shaping starts with your own brow bone and face shape, not with a trend. Thick brows suit some faces and a softer arch suits others. We clean up stray hair first, then shape slowly, checking with you as we go so nothing is taken off by surprise.

If you are growing your brows out, tell us. We will only tidy the obvious stray hair and leave the shape alone. That patience pays off in a few weeks, when the brow has a fuller, natural line to shape properly.

04 Threading or waxing for the face

Threading gives more control on small areas like eyebrows and upper lip, and it does not leave wax or cream on the skin. Face waxing is faster for larger areas, but it can irritate sensitive skin more easily. For most women, threading is the safer pick for eyebrows and upper lip.

If you have very sensitive skin or you use acne treatment, threading is usually still fine, but tell us first. We will test a small area and stop if your skin reacts. Honest advice matters more than finishing the booking quickly.

Questions we hear at the salon

How long does threading take?

Eyebrow shaping alone takes about 10 to 15 minutes. Full face threading takes about 20 to 30 minutes, depending on how much hair needs clearing.

Will threading leave my skin red?

Mild redness for 20 to 30 minutes is normal, especially on the upper lip. It settles on its own. Skip makeup on the area for an hour afterwards.

Is threading better than face waxing?

For eyebrows and upper lip, yes for most women, because it gives more control and leaves no wax on the skin. For larger areas, waxing can be faster.

Can I get threading and a facial in one visit?
