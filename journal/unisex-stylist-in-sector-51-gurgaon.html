Unisex Stylist in Sector 51, Gurgaon

The Stylist Unisex Salon · 23 October 2026 · 6 min read

Unisex is one of those words you see on every other salon board in Gurgaon. A unisex stylist is trained in both men's and women's haircuts, colour, and styling, so one salon can look after the whole family. At The Stylist, our Sector 51 branch sits on the ground floor of The Arzoo Apartments, and this is what the word means in practice: men, women, and teenagers walk in through the same door every day, and each gets a stylist who knows their kind of cut.

01 What "unisex" really means

It is not a special technique. It means the team is trained across the board: short fades and classic scissor cuts for men, long layers and bobs for women, and colour for anyone who wants it. You book a chair, the stylist studies your hair, your face shape, and your at-home effort, and then the cut happens. Same standard whether the client is 16 or 60.

02 Cuts for men

Men's cutting is its own craft. A good fade needs clean blending, a classic side part needs the right weight left on top, and textured crops need a stylist who understands how short hair grows out. We do all of it, plus beard shaping and grooming to finish the look. Most men come in with a phone photo and leave with something better than the photo, because the stylist adjusts the cut to the actual head shape and hair texture in front of them. A cut takes about 30 to 45 minutes, and you are back to your day.

03 Cuts and colour for women

Women's work is where consultation matters most. Long layers, U-cuts, bobs, and curtain bangs behave differently with different hair density and texture, and a stylist who rushes this step is how bad haircuts happen. Colour starts with a conversation about maintenance, because the prettiest colour is the one you can live with. We use professional colour lines and show you the shade chart before anything is mixed. If your hair is dry or stressed from past colour, ask about our hair spa.

04 One salon for the whole family

This is the real reason unisex works. Book back-to-back appointments and the whole family is done in one trip: your cut, your wife's colour touch-up, your son's first proper haircut. Parents like that their teenager sits in a professional chair. Over a few visits, your stylist learns the family: how your hair grows, which products you actually use, what to suggest and what to skip.

The family booking trick: ask for back-to-back slots with the same stylist. One stylist who knows the whole family's hair gives more consistent results, and the appointments run smoother.

Questions we hear at the salon

Is a unisex salon good for men's haircuts?

Yes, when the stylists are trained for it. A proper unisex salon does not treat men's cuts as an afterthought; fades, classic cuts, and textured crops need specific skill. Our stylists cut men's and women's hair all day, so neither is a side job. If you want proof, ask to see recent men's cuts before you sit down.

Do you do women's hair colour at the Sector 51 branch?

Yes. Global colour, highlights, and balayage are available at both branches, and every colour service starts with a consultation and a shade chart. Tell the stylist how much maintenance you are willing to do, and they will steer you toward a colour that still looks good six weeks later.

Can my whole family get appointments together?

Yes, and this is one of the most common bookings we get. Call 81781 59945 and ask for back-to-back slots. A family of three is usually done in about two hours, and everyone leaves with a cut from a stylist who knows their hair.
