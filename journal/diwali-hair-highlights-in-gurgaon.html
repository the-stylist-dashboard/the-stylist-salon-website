01 Which highlight placement suits you

Face framing highlights lighten the strands around your face and suit clients who want a visible change with less upkeep. Full highlights spread lighter pieces through the hair for more overall brightness, while a few scattered pieces suit first timers who want a subtle lift.

Bring a photo and say what you like about it, but let the stylist adjust for your hair texture and colour history. Highlights on previously coloured hair need a different approach from highlights on untouched hair, and that check keeps the result even.

02 When to book before Diwali

Book highlights three to five days before Diwali on 8 November 2026. That gap lets the tone settle, lets you check it in daylight, and leaves room for a toner adjustment if the shade looks too warm or too cool.

Slots in Diwali week go early, so book the consultation first if you have never had highlights. The consultation covers patch testing, upkeep, and whether your hair needs a hair spa before or after the service.

03 Aftercare for highlighted hair

Use products made for coloured hair, wash with lukewarm water, and limit daily heat styling. A hair spa a week after highlights helps with dryness, because lightening opens the hair and moisture keeps it soft and shiny.

Avoid home colour or toning products between visits unless your stylist suggests one. The wrong home product can shift the tone and make correction harder at your next appointment.

04 Pairing highlights with your Diwali look

Highlights pair well with a blowout or soft waves for Diwali night, and with a hair spa earlier in the week for shine. Plan the order as highlights first, then spa, then styling on the day, so each step builds on the last.

If mehndi or a facial is also on your list, spread the services across the week. A calm plan beats a single packed day, and your hair, skin, and hands all get proper attention instead of a rushed finish.

05 Highlights and hair health

Lightening opens the hair, so highlighted hair needs more moisture than untouched hair. A hair spa after highlights, and a nourishing wash routine at home, keep the hair soft instead of dry and rough.

Trim split ends before highlights if they are visible. Split ends travel up the hair if left alone, and a small trim before colouring gives the highlights a cleaner base to work on.

If your hair has been coloured or treated with keratin recently, tell the stylist before highlights. Old chemical work changes how lightener behaves, and a strand test can save you from uneven colour on the day.

After Diwali, book a hair spa and a trim to close the festival week well. Highlights look their best when the ends are tidy and the hair is moisturised, and that one follow-up visit keeps the colour fresh into the wedding season.
