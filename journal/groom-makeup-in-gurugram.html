01 What groom makeup is, and who it suits

Groom makeup is a light base, a touch of concealer where needed, and a matte finish that stops the face from shining in photographs. It suits every groom, whether the skin is oily, dry, or a mix of both, because the products are picked for the skin type and the finish is kept natural. The aim is not to change how the groom looks, but to make sure the camera sees the groom at his best, without shine, redness, or dark circles.

Most grooms have never worn makeup before, and that is fine, because the artist will explain each step and keep the look light. The products used are the same ones used for bridal makeup, but far less of them, so the skin still looks like skin. If you have a beard, the makeup is worked around it carefully, and the beard itself is trimmed and shaped first so the whole face looks tidy.

02 What groom makeup covers

The main things groom makeup covers are dark circles from late nights, shine on the forehead and nose, and any redness or uneven patches on the skin. A light concealer under the eyes makes the groom look rested even after a week of functions, and a matte powder keeps the shine away through a long ceremony. The base is matched to the skin tone, so it blends in and does not show in daylight or in photographs.

Groom makeup also helps with the small things that show up in close photographs, such as dry lips, a shiny nose, and uneven skin after shaving. A little lip balm, a light powder, and a careful base take care of all three without anyone noticing. If the groom has sensitive skin or a recent shave rash, tell the artist before the session, so the products can be picked to suit the skin.

03 Hair and beard pairing for the groom

Groom makeup looks best when the hair and the beard are done in the same visit, because the whole look is planned as one. A fresh haircut, a shaped beard, and a light makeup base together give the groom a clean, finished look for the wedding day. Book the haircut a day or two before the wedding, and the makeup on the day itself, so the hair has time to settle and the makeup is fresh.

Tell the stylist about your outfit, because a sherwani, a suit, and a kurta each suit a slightly different hair finish, and the makeup is kept the same light base for all three. If you wear glasses, mention it, because the makeup around the eyes is adjusted so the frames do not smudge it. Bring a photo of your outfit if you can, so the stylist and the artist can plan the full look together.
