01 What groom makeup covers

Groom makeup covers the basics that show up in photographs: an even skin tone, dark circles covered, shine controlled, and a neat, clean finish that looks like good skin rather than makeup. It usually includes a light base, a touch of concealer where needed, a matte finish to control shine under bright lights, and a light setting so the look lasts through the ceremonies. The groom's hair is usually styled in the same visit, so the full look comes together at once.

The look is kept natural on purpose, because the groom should look like himself, only fresher and more rested. A good artist will ask about the groom's skin type, because oily skin needs a different base than dry skin, and will check for any sensitivity before starting. If the groom has never had makeup before, a short trial before the wedding helps him get comfortable with how it feels.

02 When to book the groom makeup appointment

Book the groom makeup appointment a week or two before the wedding, and pick a slot on the day that leaves enough time before the first ceremony, because rushing the makeup shows in the photographs. Most grooms need about an hour, including the hair styling, so plan the morning around it. If there are several functions, the groom may need makeup for the main day only, or for the sangeet and the reception too, depending on the photographs planned.

In wedding season, groom slots fill fast, especially on weekends, so an early booking is the safest move. If the groom wants a trial, book it a few days before the wedding, so there is time to change anything he does not like. Tell the salon at booking which functions need makeup, so the right amount of time is set aside for each one.

03 What to ask for at booking

At booking, tell the salon the date and the time of the first ceremony, the groom's outfit colour, and whether he wants a trial first, so the artist can plan the look and the timing. Mention any skin concerns, such as acne, sensitivity, or a recent shave rash, because the artist needs to know before picking products. If the groom wants a beard trim or a shave as well, book it in the same visit, so the skin is calm before the makeup goes on.

Ask what is included in the appointment, so you know whether hair styling is part of it or a separate booking. A clear booking avoids a rush on the day, and it avoids the groom waiting around for a service that was not booked. Bring a photo of the outfit to the trial, because it helps the artist pick the right finish for the look.
