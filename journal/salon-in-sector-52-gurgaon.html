Salon in Sector 52, Gurgaon

If you are hunting for a salon in Sector 52, Gurgaon, you do not want to travel far or gamble on quality. Here is what to check before you book, what fair prices look like in this sector, and where to find us if you want to skip the search.

01 Why Sector 52 is an easy salon trip

Sector 52 sits well connected to Artemis Hospital, Golf Course Extension Road, and the residential blocks of Sectors 51, 53, and 56. Most residents can reach a salon here in five to ten minutes. High Street 52, where we are, has easy parking and a layout you can find on the first visit.

A salon in Sector 52 Gurgaon serves more than just the sector. People come from Sushant Lok, Ardee City, and Koyal Vihar because the drive is short and the area is central. If you live anywhere in this pocket, you do not need to cross town for a decent haircut.

02 Three things to check before you book

First, hygiene. Walk past any salon you are considering and look at the tools: combs and brushes should be clean and stored properly, capes fresh for each customer, and stations wiped down between clients. Dirty tools spread scalp problems, and a salon that skips this is skipping other things too.

Second, pricing. A salon in Sector 52 should tell you the price before the service starts, not after. Roughly: Rs 400 to Rs 800 for a men's haircut, Rs 900 to Rs 2,000 for a women's cut with styling, Rs 1,000 to Rs 2,500 for a hair spa depending on length. If a salon cannot quote you over the phone, be cautious.

Third, the consultation. Before any real service, the stylist should ask about your hair, your routine, and what you want. Ten minutes of honest conversation prevents most bad haircuts. Skipping straight to the chair is not saving you time, it is risking your hair.

Our honest note: we are a hair-focused salon: haircuts, hair spa, hair colour, and head massage. When a salon's menu promises everything under the sun, ask how deep each service goes. Specialists usually do better work than generalists.

03 What a first visit should feel like

You walk in, you are offered a seat, and someone asks what you need before anything else happens. For a haircut, expect the consultation first, then the wash, then the cut, then the finish. A men's cut takes about 30 to 45 minutes, a women's cut with styling about an hour.

For a hair spa, the visit runs 45 minutes to an hour: cleanse, a mask for your hair type, steam, a head massage, and a blowout. You should leave knowing exactly what was done and what to do at home to keep the result.

04 Hair spa and head massage, done properly

A quick clarification, because it matters: our salon is hair only. Hair spa, hair colour, cuts, and head massage. The head massage is part of the hair spa ritual, it relaxes you and helps the treatment absorb, and it is done at the chair, not in a separate therapy room.

This focus is deliberate. Hair is all we do, so every product, tool, and technique in the salon is chosen for hair. When a salon spreads itself thin across everything, the hair services often become the side business. Here, they are the whole business.
