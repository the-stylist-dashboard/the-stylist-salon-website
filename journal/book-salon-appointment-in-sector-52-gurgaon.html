01 How booking works at The Stylist

Booking takes one phone call to 81781 59945. Tell us the service, the day, and a time window that suits you, and we hold the slot for you. There is no app to install, no advance payment, and no long form to fill. It suits anyone in Sector 52 and nearby sectors who wants a confirmed slot instead of waiting at the door.

Most services take 30 minutes to 3 hours depending on what you book, and we tell you the time needed when you call. We have two salons: Shop No. 86, High Street 52, Sector 52, opposite Artemis Hospital, and Ground Floor, The Arzoo Apartments, Sector 51. Tell us which one you want when you book.

02 Karva Chauth slots go first

Karva Chauth is on Thursday 29 October, and this is the busiest week of the festive season for us. Facials, threading, eyebrow shaping, hair spa, and mehndi appointments fill up days ahead, and the evening slots between 4 PM and 9 PM are always the first to go. If you need an evening slot, book it before you book anything else.

The day before, Wednesday 28 October, is the smartest day for a facial or a hair spa, because your skin and hair get a day to settle before the evening. Our flat 40% off runs till 30 October only, so a booking in Karva Chauth week counts, but do not count on it for Diwali. Call 81781 59945 early in the week for the best choice of times.

03 What to tell us when you call

A few details help us book you properly. Tell us the exact service, whether it is for a man or a woman, and any past colour, keratin, or smoothening treatment if you are booking hair work. For colour, a photo of your current hair and a photo of the look you want saves a lot of back and forth.

Also tell us your time window, not just one exact hour, so we can fit you in if your first choice is taken. If you want two services in one visit, like a haircut and a hair spa, or a facial and threading, say so upfront. We will block enough time so neither service feels rushed.

04 Weekends, evenings, and walk-ins

Saturdays and Sundays are our busiest days, and evenings from 5 PM onwards fill next. If your schedule allows it, a weekday visit between 11 AM and 2 PM is the quietest time in the salon and the easiest to book at short notice. We are open Wednesdays to Mondays from 11 AM to 9 PM and closed on Tuesdays.

Walk-ins are welcome, and we will fit you in if a chair is free. But in festive weeks, walk-ins often wait or miss out entirely, especially for facials and hair spa. A phone booking costs you nothing and guarantees your chair, so it is always the better option in October and November.

05 If you need to change or cancel

Plans change, and we understand that. If you need to move or cancel your appointment, call us as early as you can so we can offer that slot to someone else who is waiting. There is no charge for moving a booking, and we would rather reschedule you than have you rush in late and stressed.

If you are running late on the day, call us on 81781 59945 and let us know. We will try to adjust, though a long delay may mean shortening the service or moving it to another slot. A quick call keeps things smooth for you and for the next client after you.

If you are booking for two people, like a mother and daughter before Karva Chauth, tell us when you call. We will try to place you in chairs at the same time, though in the busiest slots that may not always be possible. Booking together also means one trip for both of you, which is easier in festive week traffic.

Book the evening first: If you need an evening slot in Karva Chauth week, book it before you book anything else, because evening slots go first every single year.
