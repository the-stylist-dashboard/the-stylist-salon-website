01 What a cleanup actually does

A cleanup is a focused skin treatment that deep-cleans your face. It usually includes cleansing, gentle exfoliation, steam, blackhead and whitehead removal, and a soothing mask. It is quicker and lighter than a full facial, and it is the right choice when your main problem is clogged pores, dullness, or oily buildup rather than deep dryness or ageing concerns.

In Gurgaon, pollution, dust, and humidity clog pores faster than most people expect. Sunscreen, makeup, and sweat all add to the buildup, and a home face wash alone cannot clear it properly.

The whole session takes about thirty to forty five minutes, and you walk out with skin that looks cleaner and feels lighter straight away.

02 Cleanup or facial: which one do you need

This is the question we hear most, and the answer is simple. A cleanup focuses on cleaning: unclogging pores, removing blackheads, and refreshing the skin surface. A facial does that too, but it adds massage, longer mask time, and targeted serums for concerns like pigmentation, dryness, or ageing.

If your skin is generally fine but looks dull or feels congested, a cleanup is enough and costs less. If you want relaxation plus treatment for a specific concern, or you have an event coming up, a facial is the better pick.

Many clients alternate: a cleanup every month for maintenance, and a facial every couple of months or before a big occasion.

03 What happens during your cleanup

Your visit starts with a look at your skin. We check whether it is oily, dry, sensitive, or combination, and whether you have active acne or irritation. That decides which products we use, because a harsh scrub on sensitive skin does more harm than good.

Next comes cleansing, gentle exfoliation, and steam to open the pores. Blackhead removal follows, done carefully and hygienically. We finish with a mask suited to your skin type and a light moisturiser.

You may see slight redness for an hour or two afterwards, especially after extraction. That is normal and settles quickly. Avoid heavy makeup and harsh scrubs for a day after your cleanup.

04 How often should you get a cleanup

Once a month suits most people in Gurgaon, because that matches how fast pores clog up here. Oily skin may need one every three weeks, while dry or sensitive skin is usually fine every five to six weeks.

More often is not better. Over-cleansing strips the skin and can trigger more oiliness or irritation, which is the opposite of what you want. If you are unsure, ask our skin therapist to look at your skin and suggest a gap that suits it.

Between visits, the basics matter most: a gentle cleanser, a moisturiser that suits your skin type, and sunscreen every morning.

Questions we hear at the salon

Is a cleanup painful?

Mild discomfort during blackhead removal is normal, but it should not be painful. Tell your therapist if anything hurts and they will adjust pressure or skip that area.

How is a cleanup different from a facial?

A cleanup focuses on deep cleaning and extraction and takes about thirty to forty five minutes. A facial is longer, includes massage and targeted serums, and treats specific concerns like dryness or pigmentation.

Can I get a cleanup if I have acne?

Yes, but tell your therapist about active breakouts first. They will work gently around inflamed areas and may suggest a facial or a dermatologist visit if acne is severe.

How often should I get a cleanup?
