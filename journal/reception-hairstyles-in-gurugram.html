01 Styles that work for a reception

Soft curls, a sleek blowout, a low bun, and a half-up style are the most popular reception picks, because they look polished and they hold well through a long evening. A low bun suits a heavy lehenga or a gown with a detailed back, because it keeps the hair off the outfit and shows the work on it. Soft curls suit a lighter outfit, because they move well when you dance and they photograph beautifully from every angle.

The right style follows your outfit and your face shape, not just the trend of the season. A round face suits height at the crown, while a long face suits softness at the sides, so ask the stylist what suits you rather than copying a photo blindly. Bring a photo of your outfit to the booking, because the stylist can then plan a style that sits well with the neckline and the jewellery.

02 Making the style last all evening

A reception style lasts when the hair is prepared properly, with the right products for your hair type and enough setting to hold through dancing. Fine hair needs volume at the roots, while thick hair needs the weight managed so the style does not drop halfway through the evening. A good stylist will pin the style securely but comfortably, so it holds without giving you a headache by midnight.

Avoid washing your hair on the morning of the reception, because freshly washed hair is too soft to hold a style well. Hair washed the night before, with a little dry shampoo at the roots if needed, holds a style far better. If your hair is very fine or very silky, tell the stylist, because the prep and the products will need to be different.

03 Hair and makeup together for the reception

Book the hair and the makeup together in one visit, so the stylist and the artist can plan the full look as one, and so the timing works out without a rush between services. A combined booking also means you leave the salon ready for the evening, with the hair and the makeup in tune with each other. Tell the salon at booking if you want both, so enough time is set aside.

If you have a trial, use it to test the full look, hair and makeup together, because a style that looks good on its own may not sit well with the makeup or the outfit. A trial also lets the stylist learn how your hair behaves, so the final style on the day is smoother and faster. Book the trial a few days before the reception, so there is time to change anything you do not like.
