01 What "hair treatment" actually covers

A hair treatment is any professional service meant to repair, strengthen, or change how your hair behaves. Hair spa, keratin smoothening, nanoplastia, dandruff care, and hair fall therapies all count. The confusion starts because every salon menu uses different names for similar things.

At our salon, a treatment always starts with a diagnosis. We check your scalp, your lengths, past colour or chemical work, and your home routine. That ten-minute look saves you from paying for the wrong treatment.

02 Hair spa: the repair workhorse

Hair spa is what most damaged hair needs first. It deep-cleans the scalp, massages it to improve circulation, and pushes a concentrated mask into the hair shaft with steam. Dry, rough, sun-beaten hair comes out softer and easier to brush in one sitting. Our treatment menu covers hair services only, and we keep it that way on purpose.

One session lasts three to four weeks with decent home care. For badly damaged hair, we suggest three sessions spaced two weeks apart; each builds on the last until your hair holds moisture on its own.

03 Keratin and nanoplastia for frizz that will not quit

If frizz returns the moment Gurgaon humidity hits, a smoothing treatment beats repair-only options. Keratin smoothening relaxes the hair and seals the cuticle so humidity stops swelling every strand. Nanoplastia does similar work more gently with proteins and amino acids, and suits fine or coloured hair.

Both take two to three hours and last three to five months. Your routine shrinks to a wash and a quick blow-dry. Ask us for the honest keratin-versus-nanoplastia comparison before you decide; the right choice depends on your hair history, not the price board.

04 Dandruff, hair fall, and scalp problems

Many hair problems start at the scalp, not the lengths. Flakes, itching, oily roots with dry ends, and seasonal shedding all respond to the right scalp treatment: a detox cleanse, an exfoliating scrub, and a calming mask, sometimes with a head massage to improve circulation. Diagnosis matters here, because dandruff and a dry scalp look similar and need opposite products.

Be patient with scalp work. One session calms things down, but months of buildup usually needs two or three visits to clear. We will also check your shampoo and washing frequency, because the wrong product used daily can undo a good treatment in a week.

Questions we hear at the salon

What is the best hair treatment for dry, frizzy hair?

For most people, a professional hair spa is the right first step. It restores moisture and calms the scalp in one sitting. If the frizz is structural rather than damage, keratin smoothening or nanoplastia works better. We diagnose first and tell you which fits.

How long does a hair spa result last?

About three to four weeks with normal home care. Badly damaged hair usually needs three sessions spaced two weeks apart. A sulphate-free shampoo at home stretches every result.

Is keratin treatment safe for coloured hair?

Yes, but order matters: colour first, then keratin, with at least a week between. Nanoplastia is gentler and suits fine or previously coloured hair. Tell us your colour history at the consultation.
