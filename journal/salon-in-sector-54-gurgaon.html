01 How to pick a salon near Sector 54

A good salon near Sector 54 is one with honest pricing, a real consultation before the service, and stylists who tell you plainly what suits you. The Stylist Unisex Salon in Sector 52 and Sector 51 fits that test, and both locations are a short drive from Sector 54. Most visits take 30 to 75 minutes, depending on the service.

Start with three checks: does the salon consult before the service, does it quote a price before starting, and does it have reviews from real local clients. A salon that skips the consultation or adds charges at the chair is not saving you money, it is costing you trust.

Also check the basics: clean tools, a tidy chair, and staff who listen. Those small things tell you more about a salon than a glossy photo does. If you feel rushed or pushed into a service you did not ask for, walk away and try elsewhere.

03 Prices and the 40 percent off

We quote the price before we start, so there is no surprise at the end. Our flat 40 percent off runs till 30 October only, so a visit today or tomorrow counts. A Diwali visit on 8 November will be at the regular price unless the offer is extended, and we will be straight about that when you book.

A low price is not the same as good value. A cut that grows out badly, or a facial that irritates your skin, costs more in the long run than a fair price done well. Ask what is included, and compare like with like.

05 What to tell us when you book

Tell us your hair or skin history in one line: colour last month, sensitive skin, dandruff, or a service that went wrong elsewhere. That one line helps us pick the right stylist, the right product, and the right amount of time, and it prevents most problems before they start.

If you are booking for a wedding or Diwali, say so and give the event date. We can then plan the gap between services, for example threading a day before, colour a week before, and a hair spa a day or two before, so everything settles in time.

The consultation test: If a salon will not consult before the service, it has not earned your booking. A five minute talk prevents most bad hair days.
