01 What a good manicure actually includes

A manicure is more than nail polish. At The Stylist, a classic manicure starts with soaking, cleaning under the nails, shaping, gentle cuticle care, a hand massage, and then polish if you want it.

Shaping matters more than most people think. Square, round, oval, and almond shapes all change how your hands look, and the right shape depends on your nail bed and how you use your hands every day.

Cuticle care should be gentle. We push cuticles back and tidy loose skin, but we do not cut aggressively, because sore cuticles invite infection and take weeks to settle.

02 Classic manicure or gel manicure: which one suits you

A classic manicure uses regular polish. It is quick, easy to change, and a good choice if you like switching colours often or if your nails are weak and need a break from stronger products.

A gel manicure is cured under a lamp and lasts two to three weeks without chipping. It suits holidays, weddings, and busy weeks when you cannot redo polish every few days.

Gel is not for everyone. If your nails peel easily, or you pick at polish, gel can lift and take nail layers with it. Tell us about your nail history and we will suggest the safer option.

03 What happens during your appointment

We start by removing old polish and checking your nails and cuticles. If a nail is broken, infected, or very sore, we will tell you honestly if it needs rest or a doctor instead of polish.

Next comes soaking, shaping, cuticle care, and a short hand massage with cream. Then we apply a base coat, two coats of colour, and a top coat, with drying time between coats.

A classic manicure takes about forty five minutes. Gel takes a little longer because of curing time. If you want nail art or extensions too, say so when you book on 81781 59945 so we allow enough time.

04 Nail shapes and colours that work for everyday life

Short, rounded nails are the easiest to live with if you type all day, cook, or look after children. Longer almond or square shapes look striking, but they chip faster if you use your hands hard.

For work, nude, soft pink, and classic red are safe choices that suit most skin tones. For a party, deep maroon, navy, or a subtle glitter accent on one nail adds interest without going over the top.

If you are unsure, ask to see shades on a nail wheel next to your hand. Colours look different in the bottle and on your skin, and a quick check saves a colour you regret.

05 How to make your manicure last longer

Wear gloves for washing dishes and cleaning, because hot water and detergents lift polish fast. Keep a little cuticle oil by your bed and use it most nights to stop nails drying out.

Do not peel gel polish off. Peeling takes nail layers with it and leaves nails thin for weeks. Come back for a proper removal, which is quick and keeps your nails intact.

Questions we hear at the salon

How long does a manicure last?

Classic polish lasts about five to seven days with care. Gel lasts two to three weeks. Gloves for cleaning and daily cuticle oil both help either type last longer.

Is gel bad for my nails?

Gel itself is not the problem. Picking it off or skipping proper removal damages nails. With correct application and removal, most nails stay healthy.

Can I get a manicure if my nails are short or bitten?

Yes. Short nails still benefit from shaping, cuticle care, and polish, and regular manicures often help people stop biting because the nails look cared for.

Do you do nail art and extensions too?

Yes, we offer nail art and extensions at the salon. Mention it when you book so we allow extra time beyond a standard manicure.
