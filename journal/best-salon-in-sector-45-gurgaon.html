Every salon in Gurgaon claims to be the best. The word is on hoardings, on Google listings, on Instagram bios. But best is not a claim. It is a record. Here is how to read that record before you trust your hair to it, and why a growing number of Sector 45 clients make the ten-minute drive to our salon in Sector 52.

01 Best means consistent, not famous

One great haircut can be luck. Fifty great haircuts is a system. The real test of a salon is not what happens on your first visit, when everyone is on their best behaviour. It is the fifth visit, the tenth visit, on a busy Saturday evening when the chairs are full. A good salon delivers the same standard every time.

Ask yourself: do the regulars keep coming back. A salon full of repeat clients is doing something right. Regulars are the review no marketing can fake.

02 Read the reviews like a detective

Star counts are easy to game. Detailed reviews are not. Look for reviews that name a stylist and describe the service in specific terms: what was cut, what was coloured, how it turned out. One-line five-star reviews tell you nothing. A page where every review appeared in the same week should make you suspicious.

Read the negative reviews too, and watch how the salon responds. A calm, specific reply to a complaint shows a business that takes feedback seriously. Silence, or a defensive argument, shows the opposite.

03 The honesty test

Here is the fastest way to judge a salon: ask for something that will not work for your hair, and see what happens. A good stylist tells you no. Your hair is too damaged for that colour. That cut will not suit your face shape. A simple trim is all you need right now. A salon that agrees to everything is not serving you, it is billing you.

This applies to upselling too. A salon that pushes treatments you did not ask for is running a sales operation. A salon that suggests only what your hair needs, and skips the rest, is running a salon.

04 Hygiene is not optional

Clean combs, fresh capes, sanitised scissors, a floor swept between clients. None of this is luxury. It is the minimum. You can check it in thirty seconds by looking around the chair you are about to sit in. If the basics are missing, walk out.

Colour safety is part of this. Patch tests for new clients, sealed colour tubes opened in front of you, labelled bottles you can read. These are non-negotiable in a salon that calls itself the best.
