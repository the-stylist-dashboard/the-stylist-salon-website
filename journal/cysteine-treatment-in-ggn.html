01 What cysteine treatment does

Cysteine treatment works by softening the bonds in the hair just enough to relax the wave and settle the frizz, while keeping the hair feeling natural and soft. The result is hair that falls smoother, dries faster, and needs less styling every day, and it does not leave the hair feeling stiff or coated. It is a good middle path between a blow dry that washes out and a strong straightening treatment that changes the hair completely.

The treatment suits wavy and frizzy hair best, and it works well on hair that has been coloured too, because it is gentler than most chemical straightening. Very curly hair will come out softer and straighter, but not pin straight, and the stylist will tell you honestly what to expect before starting. A patch test and a hair check come first, every time.

02 How the cysteine session works

A cysteine session takes two to three hours, so plan your day around it. The stylist washes the hair, applies the cysteine cream section by section, lets it work, then dries and irons the hair to seal the finish. After that, the hair is washed again and dried smooth, so you leave with the finished look on the same day and you can see the result right away.

Bring a photo of the finish you want, and tell the stylist about any colour or chemical work your hair has had in the last few months. Colour and cysteine can be done together, but the order matters, and the stylist will plan it so your hair stays healthy. If your hair is very dry or damaged, the stylist may suggest a hair spa first to build strength before the treatment.

03 Caring for hair after cysteine treatment

The first two days after cysteine matter most. Do not wash the hair, tie it up tightly, or tuck it behind your ears, because the hair is still setting and a crease can stay in. Use a sulphate-free shampoo and conditioner, and wash less often than before, because treated hair stays cleaner for longer and needs less washing.

A hair spa once a month keeps cysteine-treated hair soft and shiny, because any chemical treatment dries the hair over time. Avoid swimming in chlorinated water without a cap, and use a heat protectant if you use a straightener or a dryer often. With this care, the smooth finish lasts two to three months, and the regrowth blends in softly instead of showing a hard line.
