01 Why men need facials too

Most men in Gurgaon deal with the same skin problems: oiliness, blackheads, dullness from pollution, and irritation from shaving. A face wash alone does not clear clogged pores or fix ingrown hairs, and that is where a facial earns its place.

Male skin is thicker and oilier than female skin on average, and daily shaving adds irritation on top of that. A facial designed for men uses stronger cleansing, careful exfoliation, and a massage that actually helps with tension in the jaw and forehead, not just a light touch.

You do not need to know anything about skin care to get a facial. Tell the therapist what bothers you, whether it is oiliness, blackheads, dryness, or razor bumps, and they will handle the rest.

02 What happens during a men's facial

Your visit starts with a look at your skin and a few questions about shaving, sun exposure, and any products you use. That decides which products suit you, because a harsh treatment on freshly shaved skin can sting.

The facial usually includes deep cleansing, exfoliation, steam, blackhead removal, a face and head massage, and a mask suited to your skin type. Oily skin gets a clay mask, dry skin a hydrating one.

The whole session takes about forty five minutes to an hour. You leave with skin that looks cleaner and feels fresher, and slight redness after extraction settles within a couple of hours.

03 Facials and shaving: getting the timing right

Do not shave right before a facial. Freshly shaved skin is more sensitive, and exfoliation on top of it can cause redness and irritation. Shave the evening before, or skip shaving that morning, and let the therapist know when you last shaved.

After a facial, wait a day before shaving again if you can. Your skin has just been exfoliated and treated, and a razor straight away can undo some of that calm. Use a gentle moisturiser and sunscreen in the meantime.

If razor bumps and ingrown hairs are your main problem, mention it when you book. Your therapist can focus extra attention on the beard area and suggest a routine that reduces bumps over time.

04 How often should men get a facial

Once a month is a good rhythm for most men, because it matches how fast pores clog up with pollution, sweat, and oil in Gurgaon. Men with very oily skin or a lot of blackheads may benefit from one every three weeks at first.

A facial before a wedding, an interview, or an important event is worth planning properly. Book it three to four days ahead, not the day before, so any mild redness settles and your skin looks its best when it matters.

Between facials, three habits matter most: wash twice a day with a suitable cleanser, use sunscreen every morning, and change your razor blade regularly.

Questions we hear at the salon

Is a men's facial different from a regular facial?

Yes, in the details. It uses products and pressure suited to thicker, oilier male skin, focuses on the beard area and shaving irritation, and skips anything you do not need.

Should I shave before a facial?

No. Shave the evening before or skip shaving that morning. Freshly shaved skin is sensitive and exfoliation on top of it can cause redness.

How long does a men's facial take?

About forty five minutes to an hour, including cleansing, exfoliation, steam, extraction, massage, and a mask.

How often should men get a facial?

Once a month suits most men. Very oily skin may need one every three weeks at first, then monthly for maintenance.
