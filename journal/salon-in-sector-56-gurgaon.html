A good neighbourhood salon is worth more than a fancy one across town. You need somewhere close, somewhere consistent, somewhere the stylist remembers your hair. If you live in Sector 56, here is what to look for, and what we offer five minutes from your door.

01 What a neighbourhood salon should handle

Your regular salon should cover the whole family's routine needs under one roof: haircuts for men and women, hair spa, hair colour and highlights, facials, cleanups, waxing, threading, manicure, pedicure, and men's grooming like beard shaping and shaves. One salon for everything means one stylist team that knows your hair, and no running across town for a facial and a haircut on different days.

We stick to what we are good at, and we say so plainly.

02 The thirty-second hygiene check

You can judge a salon in half a minute. Are the combs and brushes clean. Is the cape you are given fresh. Is the floor swept between clients. Can you read the brand names on the product bottles. These small things tell you how the salon treats the things you cannot see, like sanitised tools and sealed colour tubes.

Colour deserves special attention. A proper salon patch-tests new clients and opens sealed colour tubes in front of you. This is basic safety, and any salon that skips it is cutting corners where it matters most.

03 Unisex means genuinely unisex

Many salons call themselves unisex but clearly cater to one side. A truly unisex salon has stylists who are equally comfortable with a woman's layered cut and a man's fade, with beard shaping taken as seriously as blowouts. The men's and women's sections should feel like the same quality of attention.

At The Stylist, unisex is in the name because it is how we work. Our stylists cut men's hair all day and women's hair all day. Nobody gets the junior while the senior handles the other section.

04 Honest pricing, no surprises

A neighbourhood salon lives on repeat visits, which means pricing has to be something you can plan around. Look for a visible price list that says what each service includes. The warning signs: prices that jump at the counter, treatments added without asking, and a haircut that somehow needs an expensive add-on to be finished.

We price plainly and quote before we start. When you call 8178159945, you can ask the exact price for your service and your hair length. The number you hear on the phone is the number you pay at the counter.

05 Hair spa, done properly

Gurgaon hair takes a beating: hard water, dust, heat. A monthly hair spa sitting is the single most effective repair routine for dry, rough, frizzy hair. The treatment deep-conditions, smooths the cuticle, and includes a proper head massage that improves blood circulation to the scalp.

One honest note: we do hair spa only, and we say that upfront so nobody is misled. If what your hair needs is deep repair and relaxation for the scalp, our hair spa is exactly that.
