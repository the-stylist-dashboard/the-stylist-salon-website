Haircuts Get the cut you asked for

Haircut in Sector 50, Gurgaon

The Stylist Unisex Salon · 21 October 2026 · 6 min read

Most bad haircuts are not the stylist's fault. They happen because the customer and the stylist imagined two different things. A good haircut starts before the scissors come out: in what you tell the stylist, the photos you show, and knowing how often to come back. Here is how to get the haircut you actually want in Sector 50, Gurgaon.

01 Tell your stylist exactly what you want

"Just trim the ends" means different things to different people. Be specific. Show where the hair should land: collarbone, chest, shoulder. Say how much to take off in real terms, two fingers of length, not "a little". Say whether you want layers, and whether you want a fringe.

Just as important: say what you hate. If you never want short layers around your face, say it up front. If you are growing your hair out, say that too, because a stylist who knows the plan will cut differently from one who thinks this is a one-off visit.

02 Bring reference photos, but bring the right ones

Two or three photos are plenty. Pick people whose hair texture looks like yours, Indian hair with similar thickness and curl pattern. A cut that looks great on fine, straight hair will fall very differently on thick, wavy hair. Front and back views help the most, because the back is where most misunderstandings happen.

Use the photos as a starting point, not a promise. Tell the stylist which part of the photo you like: the length, the layers, the fringe. That gives them room to adjust the cut to your face and hair instead of copying blindly. For layering ideas on Indian hair, see our layered haircuts guide for Indian hair.

03 Face shape basics for Indian hair

You do not need a perfect face-shape diagnosis, but a few rules of thumb help. Round faces look longer with height on top and length below the chin, and shorter with width at the cheeks. Oval faces suit most cuts. Square faces soften with layers and texture around the jawline. Heart-shaped faces balance out with volume around the chin.

One Indian-hair reality: our hair is often thick and grows fast, which means blunt cuts get heavy quickly. Soft layers or texturing at the ends keep the cut light without losing length. Tell your stylist if your hair puffs up in humidity, because that changes how much bulk needs to come out.

04 How often to trim

Waiting until the ends split is waiting too long. A regular trim schedule keeps every cut looking fresh and lets you grow hair out without damage creeping up the shaft. Use this as your starting point:

Hair typeTrim everyWhy
Short hair (pixie, short bob)4 to 6 weeksShort cuts lose their shape fast as hair grows
Shoulder-length hair6 to 8 weeksKeeps the ends neat and the style intentional
Long hair8 to 12 weeksPrevents split ends from travelling up
Growing it out10 to 12 weeksA light dusting of the ends, not a real cut

If you cannot remember when your last trim was, run your fingers down the ends. If they feel rough, catch on each other, or look thinner than the rest, it is time. Wondering whether you need a trim or a full cut? Read our hair trim vs haircut guide.

05 What a fair price looks like in Gurgaon

Prices vary across Gurgaon, but there are sensible ranges. A men's haircut usually costs Rs 300 to Rs 800 depending on the salon and the stylist's experience. A women's haircut usually runs Rs 800 to Rs 2500, with price rising for longer hair and senior stylists.

Be careful with prices far below these ranges, especially combo offers of cut plus colour at throwaway prices. Cheap colour usually means cheap products, and those show up in your hair within weeks. A fair price for a good cut is worth it; a bad cut takes months to grow out.

Come with clean, dry hair. Skip the heavy oiling the night before your haircut. Oiled hair hides the natural fall and texture, so the stylist cannot judge how the cut will actually sit. We wash your hair at the salon before cutting anyway.

Questions we hear at the salon

How often should I get a haircut?

It depends on your hair length and style. Short hair needs a trim every 4 to 6 weeks to keep its shape. Shoulder-length hair does well every 6 to 8 weeks. Long hair can go 8 to 12 weeks, as long as the ends are not splitting.

How much does a good haircut cost in Gurgaon?

A men's haircut in Gurgaon usually costs between Rs 300 and Rs 800 depending on the salon, and a women's haircut usually runs Rs 800 to Rs 2500. Price rises with stylist experience and hair length. If a price looks far below these ranges, ask what is actually included.

Should I wash my hair before a haircut?

Come with clean, dry hair and skip heavy oiling the night before. Oiled hair hides the natural fall and texture, so the stylist cannot judge how the cut will actually sit. We wash your hair at the salon before cutting anyway.

What should I do if I do not like my haircut?

Tell the stylist within a day or two, calmly and specifically, about what bothers you. Most issues, uneven layers or too much bulk, can be fixed with a small adjustment visit. Waiting weeks makes it harder to correct.
