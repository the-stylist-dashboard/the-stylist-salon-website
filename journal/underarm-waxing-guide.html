Underarm Waxing: What First-Timers Should Know

The Stylist Unisex Salon · 28 September 2026 · 6 min read

You have shaved your underarms for years and you are tired of the shadow by evening, the rough look, the ingrowns. A friend says waxing changed everything for her. But underarms feel like a brave first choice: the skin is thin, it is a sweaty area, and you have questions you do not want to ask out loud. Ask them here. We have heard them all.

01 Why underarms are different from legs

Underarm skin is thinner and looser than leg skin, and the hair grows in multiple directions, which is why shaving leaves shadow and waxing needs a skilled hand. The therapist holds the skin taut and works in small sections, changing direction with the growth. It takes ten to fifteen minutes. Because the area is small, even first-timers are usually surprised how fast it is over.

This is also why underarm waxing is a poor DIY project; the angles are awkward and the skin needs proper tension.

02 The first-timer prep list

Let the hair grow to about a quarter inch; roughly two weeks after your last shave. Skip deodorant, antiperspirant and perfumed products on the day. Wear a loose sleeveless or short-sleeved top so nothing rubs afterwards. Come freshly showered; the therapist appreciates it and the warm water softens the hair. If you are nervous, say so. We would rather go slower than have you tense up through the whole thing.

Avoid scheduling right after a heavy workout; calm, dry skin waxes cleaner than sweaty post-gym skin.

03 Pain, sweat and ingrowns: the honest trio

Yes, it stings, but the small area means it is over quickly, and most clients say the second visit is noticeably easier. Sweat is not a problem for the therapist; we have seen everything and we clean the area first. Ingrown hairs happen when dead skin traps new growth; gentle exfoliation a few days after waxing, once the skin has calmed, keeps them rare. The dark look usually softens over a few sessions as you stop shaving.

Regular waxing trains the growth cycle, which is why long-term clients barely notice the appointment anymore.

04 Aftercare that actually matters

No deodorant for 24 hours; the alcohol and fragrance sting freshly waxed skin. Skip the gym, hot yoga and swimming for a day or two, and wear breathable fabrics. Questions we hear at the salon

Does underarm waxing hurt more than legs?

The sting is sharper because the skin is thinner, but the area is small so it is over in minutes. Most first-timers say the anticipation was worse than the actual wax. The second session is noticeably easier as regrowth comes in finer.

How long should I grow hair before underarm waxing?

About a quarter inch, which usually means two weeks without shaving. Underarm hair grows fast, so this is not a long wait. Shorter hair will not grip the wax properly and you will need a second pass. When in doubt, wait two more days.

Can I use deodorant after underarm waxing?

Wait 24 hours. Freshly waxed skin is sensitive and the alcohol and fragrance in most deodorants sting and can cause bumps. After a day, resume as normal. On wax day, plan loose clothing and skip the gym instead. Your skin will thank you.

Will waxing darken my underarms?

Waxing itself does not darken skin. In fact, many clients notice the area looking clearer over time because they stop shaving, which is what causes most of the shadow and roughness. Persistent darkening has other causes; ask the therapist if it worries you.
