01 What to say when you call

Start with the service you want, because that decides how much time is set aside. Say whether you want a haircut, colour, hair spa, facial, manicure, or makeup, and whether you want more than one service in the same visit. Tell the salon about your hair or skin, such as colour history or sensitive skin, so the right products and the right stylist can be planned in advance.

Then say when you can come, and give two or three options if you can, because the first choice may already be taken, especially on a weekend. If you have a function coming up, say the date, so the booking can be planned around it. A clear call at the start saves a second call later, and it means the salon can prepare for your visit properly.

02 Booking more than one service in one visit

Combined visits save a second trip, but they need a longer slot, so tell the salon at booking if you want more than one service. A cut with a facial, a manicure with a pedicure, or hair and makeup together are all common combinations, and they work well when they are booked as one visit. Booking them as one visit avoids a rushed job or a long wait between services.

For a wedding or a function, book the full set together, such as hair, makeup, and nails, so the stylist and the artist can plan the whole look as one. Tell the salon the date of the function and your outfit colour, because the right shades and the right timing are easier to pick in advance. A combined booking also means you leave the salon ready for the function, without a rush between services.

03 Picking the best time to visit

A weekday booking is usually calmer than a weekend one, and it gives the stylist more time for your service. Mornings are quieter than evenings, so a morning slot suits a long service such as colour, keratin, or bridal makeup, because there is no rush and the stylist can take the time it needs. Evenings suit a quick trim or a cleanup after work, when you do not need a long slot.

In wedding season, book early, especially for functions in the weeks ahead, because slots fill fast. Book a week ahead for a function, and two to three weeks ahead for bridal hair and makeup. If your date is fixed, call as soon as you know it, because the best slots go first and a late booking may mean a time that does not suit you.
