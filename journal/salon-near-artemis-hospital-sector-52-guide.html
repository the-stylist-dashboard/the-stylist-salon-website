Finding a Salon Near Artemis Hospital, Sector 52

The Stylist Unisex Salon · 28 September 2026 · 6 min read

If you live or work around Artemis Hospital in Sector 52, you have probably walked past a dozen salon boards on High Street 52 and wondered which one deserves your Saturday. This is a straight guide to the neighbourhood: where things are, what to check before you book anywhere, and the practical details of a visit.

01 The neighbourhood: High Street 52 and Artemis Hospital

Artemis Hospital is the landmark everyone in this part of Gurugram knows, and High Street 52 sits right across from it. The market is a compact high street with cafes, clinics, daily-need shops and, yes, a lot of salons. Sector 52 and neighbouring Sector 51 are dense residential pockets, which means most salons here survive on repeat local customers rather than passing footfall. That is good news for you. A salon that lives on regulars cannot afford many bad haircuts.

The area is well connected for anyone coming from nearby sectors. Sohna Road is minutes away, and Cyber City commuters pass through this corridor daily. If you are choosing between a salon near home and one near work, pick the one you will actually revisit. Consistency with one stylist beats hopping between salons every time.

02 What to check before booking anywhere nearby

Proximity should not be your only filter. Before you book anywhere in the neighbourhood, check the basics.

  • Hygiene first: fresh towels per client, clean tools, single-use blades, a swept station. Walk in and look before you book.
  • Consultation before service: a good salon asks about your hair history and what you want before picking up the scissors. If they skip straight to the chair, be cautious.
  • Patch tests: for colour, bleach or any chemical service, a patch test is non-negotiable.
  • Transparent pricing: you should know the cost before the service starts, not after.
  • Realistic advice: be wary of anyone who promises transformations with no downsides. Honest stylists tell you what will not work too.

None of this is about one salon being better than another. These are the same checks that apply everywhere, and any decent neighbourhood salon will pass them comfortably.

03 Practical details: parking, timing, what to bring

High Street 52 has market parking, but it fills up on weekend evenings. A weekday visit or a morning slot is calmer. Call ahead if you are coming for something time-consuming like colour or a treatment, so the slot is blocked. Bring reference photos for cuts and colour. And if you are trying a new salon, start with a small service: a haircut, a cleanup, threading. Trust is earned, not assumed.
