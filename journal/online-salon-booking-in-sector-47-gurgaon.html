01 The two-minute booking

Here is all it takes. Call or message 81781 59945, say which service you want and when you would like to come in, and we confirm your slot on the spot. No app to download, no account to create, no form asking for your life story. Two minutes, and your chair is reserved.

A direct line beats a booking app because you are talking to the people who run the chairs. If a stylist calls in sick or a slot opens up early, we tell you right away and adjust. Apps show you a calendar; we show you the actual plan.

02 What to mention when you book

A few details make the booking smoother. Tell us the service: haircut, colour, keratin, hair spa, head massage, or a combination. For colour and keratin, mention your hair length, because that decides how much time to block. If you have a preferred stylist, say so; if not, we will match you with the right one.

Booking for more than one person? Say that too. Family and group bookings need back-to-back chairs, and we plan the order so nobody waits around. One message with the full list saves everyone a round of back-and-forth.

03 Same-day and last-minute slots

Need a slot today? Call as early in the day as you can. Weekday mornings and afternoons usually have space, and even on busy days we can often fit in a haircut or a head massage between longer appointments. Evenings and weekends are the first to fill, so those need a day or two of notice.

If your first choice is taken, we will offer the nearest free slot, sometimes at our other branch. And if you are running late, call us. A five-minute heads-up lets us hold your chair; a no-show with no word means we have to give it away.

04 Rescheduling without drama

Plans change, and that is fine. Just call 81781 59945 and let us know, and we will move you to the next slot that suits you. With a little notice there is no charge and no fuss. Calling ahead also frees your slot for someone else, which we genuinely appreciate.

What we ask is simple: do not just not show up. A missed slot is a chair that sat empty while someone else could have used it. One quick message is all it takes to keep things smooth for everyone.

05 Walk-ins versus bookings

We do accept walk-ins when a chair is free, and on quiet weekday mornings you will usually get one. But a booking guarantees your time. During busy hours, walk-ins can mean a long wait or a polite "come back later". Two minutes on the phone is a small price for certainty.
