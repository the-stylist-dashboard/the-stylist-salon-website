01 Picking the right groom haircut

Start with the outfit, because a sherwani, a suit, and a kurta each suit a slightly different hair finish. A sherwani suits a neat, classic cut with a side part, while a suit suits a sharper, more modern cut with texture on top. A kurta suits a relaxed cut that still looks tidy, because the whole look is softer. Bring a photo of your outfit to the booking, so the stylist can plan the cut around it.

Think about your hair type too, because thick hair, thin hair, and curly hair each need a different cut. A good stylist will tell you honestly what suits your hair, and will show you what the cut will look like before starting. If you have never had a proper stylist cut before, tell the stylist, because the first cut is also a chance to learn what suits you for the future.

02 When to book the groom haircut

Book the haircut two or three days before the wedding, so the cut has time to settle and the hair looks natural on the day. A cut done on the wedding morning often looks too fresh in photographs, and there is no time to fix it if something is not right. If you want a trial cut first, book it two weeks before the wedding, so you can see the style and change it if needed.

Avoid trying a brand new style for the first time at the wedding, because a new look can surprise you in the photographs. If you want a change, try it a month before, so you have time to grow it out or adjust it. The wedding is not the time to experiment, and a cut you already know suits you is the safest choice.

03 Pairing the cut with beard and makeup

A groom haircut looks best when the beard is shaped in the same visit, because the whole face is planned as one. A fresh cut, a shaped beard, and a light makeup base together give the groom a clean, finished look for the wedding day. Book the haircut and the beard trim together, and the makeup on the day itself, so the hair has time to settle and the makeup is fresh.

Tell the stylist about your glasses, if you wear them, because the cut around the ears and the sideburns is adjusted so the frames sit neatly. If you wear a turban or a safa, mention it, because the hair underneath needs to sit flat and the cut is planned around it. Bring a photo of your full outfit if you can, so the stylist can see the whole look.
