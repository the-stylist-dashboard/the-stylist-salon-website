A good facial does more than make you glow for the evening. It resets skin that has been sitting in traffic fumes and Gurgaon's hard water for weeks. Here is how to pick the right facial in Sector 55, and what separates a real one from a fancy face rub.

01 The skin analysis comes first

Every facial at our salon starts with a proper look at your skin and questions about your routine. This step decides everything: oily skin needs a deep-cleansing clay facial, dry skin needs a hydrating creamy facial, pigmented skin suits a vitamin C brightening facial, and sensitive skin needs a gentle fragrance-free formula.

If a salon asks you to pick a facial without looking at your face first, you are shopping blind. A free skin analysis is the difference between a facial that suits you and one that fights your skin.

02 Cleanup versus facial: what is the difference

People mix these two up constantly. A cleanup is the practical sibling: cleansing, steaming, and extraction of blackheads and whiteheads, usually wrapped in thirty minutes. A facial is the full routine: cleansing, exfoliation, extraction, massage, mask, and moisturising, usually an hour or more.

Congested with blackheads around the nose and chin? Book a cleanup. Dull skin or an event coming up? Book the facial. Many clients alternate: a facial one month, a cleanup the next. Our facial guide for Sector 43 covers the same routine.

03 What a proper facial actually includes

A proper facial has six stages: double cleanse, gentle exfoliation, steam plus careful extraction, face massage with a cream suited to your skin, a mask chosen for your concern, and moisturiser with sun protection. The massage matters most: it boosts circulation, which is where the post-facial glow comes from.

Two warning signs. Extraction should never hurt to the point of tears, and a mask should not burn for more than a minute. If either happens, say so immediately.

04 How often you really need one

Once every four to six weeks matches the skin's renewal cycle, and that is enough for most people. Cleanups can fill the gaps if your skin congests fast. Aggressive brightening facials done too often can leave skin thinned and reactive.

What matters more than frequency is home care: sunscreen every morning, a gentle cleanser every night, and no picking at blackheads. A monthly facial works on top of a solid routine, not instead of one.

The three-day rule: book your facial three to five days before a wedding or party, never the night before. Facials draw out deep congestion, so a spot can surface in the next day or two. The buffer lets your skin settle into the glow exactly when you need it.

05 What men should know about facials

Men's skin is thicker and oilier on average, and shaving leaves the jaw area prone to ingrowns and irritation. A men's facial handles that: stronger cleansing, careful work around the shave line, and a mask that does not leave you shiny. Good skin is not a gendered thing.

Questions we hear at the salon

Which facial should I choose for my skin type?

Oily skin suits a deep-cleansing clay facial. Dry skin needs a hydrating creamy facial. Dull or pigmented skin benefits from vitamin C brightening. Sensitive skin should stick to a gentle, fragrance-free formula. A skin analysis before the facial settles it.

How often should I get a facial?

Once every four to six weeks, matching the skin's renewal cycle. Cleanups can sit in between if you need extractions. Aggressive facials done too often can leave the skin overworked and sensitive.

Is a facial safe before a wedding or party?

Yes, but three to five days before the event, not the day before. Facials bring out deep-seated congestion, so a spot can surface in the next day or two. If you have never had a facial, do a trial run a month earlier.
