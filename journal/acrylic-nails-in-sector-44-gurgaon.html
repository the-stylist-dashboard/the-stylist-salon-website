01 What acrylic nails are

Acrylic nails are extensions built over your natural nails using a liquid and powder mix that hardens into a strong, smooth layer. They give you length and shape that natural nails often cannot hold, and they are the best option if your own nails break easily or you want a bold shape like almond, coffin, or stiletto.

The process starts with preparing your natural nail, then shaping the extension, then finishing with polish, gel colour, or nail art. A full set usually takes one and a half to two hours.

Acrylics are not the same as gel extensions. Acrylic is harder and holds sharp shapes better, so it suits long lengths and detailed art. Gel feels a little more flexible. If you are unsure, tell our nail artist what the nails are for.

02 Choosing length and shape

Length should match your daily life, not just the photo you liked online. If you type all day, cook, or look after small children, a short to medium length will be far more comfortable and will last longer. Long nails look stunning but they catch on things, and catching is how breaks happen.

For shape, oval and squoval suit most hands and are easiest to live with. Almond looks elegant and lengthens the fingers. Coffin and stiletto look dramatic but need more care and fills.

For your first set, play safe and go a little shorter than you think you want. You can always go longer next time.

03 What a good acrylic set should feel like

A well-done set feels secure but not tight, and it should not hurt. Soreness or burning during application means something is wrong, so speak up straight away. Good prep matters more than thick product.

Check the free edge and the side walls before you leave. The nails should be even in length, smooth on top, and the same shape on both hands. If one nail looks different, ask for it to be fixed there and then.

Hygiene matters too. Tools should be clean, files should not be shared without proper cleaning, and your natural nails should never be filed paper thin. If a salon rushes this, walk away.

04 How long they last and when to refill

A full set of acrylics lasts two to three weeks before the gap at the cuticle becomes noticeable. That is when you need a fill, where the new growth area is filled in and the shape is tidied up. Most clients get fills every two to three weeks and a fresh full set every couple of months.

Do not pick or peel acrylics off at home. Peeling pulls layers off your natural nail and leaves it thin and sore. Salon removal takes about thirty minutes and keeps your nails intact.

If a nail lifts or cracks, get it fixed quickly rather than waiting for your next fill. Water trapped under a lifted nail can cause problems, and a small fix now is cheaper.

Questions we hear at the salon

Do acrylic nails damage your natural nails?

Done and removed properly, they cause minimal damage. Problems come from peeling them off, over-filing, or leaving lifted nails unrepaired. Professional removal protects your natural nails.

How long does a full set take?

Usually one and a half to two hours for a full set, depending on length and design. Fills are quicker, usually forty five minutes to an hour.

Can I type and do daily work with acrylics?

Yes, if the length suits your routine. Short to medium lengths are easy for typing, cooking, and daily tasks. Very long nails take more getting used to.

How do I remove acrylic nails safely?

Have them soaked off at the salon. It takes about thirty minutes and keeps your natural nails healthy. Never peel or force them off at home.
