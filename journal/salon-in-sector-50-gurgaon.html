01 Finding a salon near Sector 50

Sector 50 sits close to Sector 52 and Sector 51, so a salon in this pocket should be easy to reach without a long drive across Gurugram. Check the address before you book, and pick a salon you can reach comfortably for repeat visits, because haircuts, facials, and nail work all need regular upkeep. A salon that is hard to reach once is harder to return to every few weeks, and regular care matters more than a single visit.

Look for a salon that covers the services you actually use, rather than one that lists everything but does a few things well. If you need haircuts and colour, check the stylists and the consultation. If you need skin or nails, check the therapists and the hygiene of the work area. A short visit or a phone call usually tells you more than a long list of services online.
