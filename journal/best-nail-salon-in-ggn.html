01 What makes a nail salon good

A good nail salon uses clean, well-kept tools, shapes the nails to suit your hands, and takes the time to prep the nail properly before any polish or extension goes on. The prep matters most, because polish and extensions only last when the nail surface is clean and the cuticles are handled gently. A salon that rushes the prep is cutting corners where it shows most, in how long the set lasts.

Honest advice is the other sign of a good nail salon. If your nails are weak, the artist should say so and suggest strengthening them first, rather than selling the longest extension. If a design will not suit your daily routine, the artist should say that too, and suggest a shape and length that will actually last for you. A salon that advises honestly is looking after your nails, not just filling a slot.

03 How to pick the right shape and length

The shape should suit your hands and your routine, because a very long stiletto looks striking but breaks easily if you work with your hands all day. A short almond or square shape is the safest pick for everyday wear, because it looks neat, feels comfortable, and lasts longer. If you want length for a wedding or a party, book the extensions a day or two before the function, so they are fresh and the polish is unchipped.

Bring a photo of the shape and the colour you like, because it saves time and avoids confusion at the chair. The artist can also suggest colours that suit your skin tone and your outfit, if you are not sure what to pick. If you have never had extensions before, start with a medium length, so you can see how they feel before going longer next time.
