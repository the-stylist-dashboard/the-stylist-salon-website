01 What face waxing covers

Face waxing covers the upper lip, chin, jawline, cheeks, and sideburns, and it is done with a gentle wax that suits facial skin. The upper lip and chin are the most common areas, because the hair there shows first, and a quick wax keeps them smooth for three to four weeks. The full face can be waxed too, but it is better to start with one area and see how your skin reacts before booking the full face.

A good wax for the face is a soft wax or a strip wax made for sensitive skin, and it should be applied at a comfortable temperature, never hot. The skin should be clean and dry before the wax goes on, and a light dusting of powder helps the wax grip the hair instead of the skin. If your skin is sensitive, tell the therapist before the wax, so a gentler wax can be used.

02 How to prepare your skin

Prepare your skin the day before, by washing your face well and skipping heavy creams and makeup on the morning of the wax. Do not use a scrub or an exfoliant on the same day, because it leaves the skin raw and the wax can pull too hard. If you use any skin treatment cream, tell the therapist, because some creams make the skin too thin for waxing, and the wax should be delayed.

Let the hair grow to a short length before the wax, because wax needs a little hair to grip, and very short hair will not come off cleanly. About a week of growth is enough for most people, and the therapist can tell you if the hair is long enough. If you are unsure, call 81781 59945 and ask, so you do not arrive for a wax that cannot be done.

03 Aftercare that keeps skin calm

After face waxing, keep the skin calm for the rest of the day, by skipping makeup, hot water, and strong sunlight for a few hours. A cold compress or a plain aloe gel soothes the skin, and a gentle moisturiser keeps it soft. Do not touch the waxed area with dirty hands, because the pores are open and can get irritated easily.

A little redness is normal for a few hours, and it fades on its own. If the redness lasts more than a day, or if bumps appear, call the salon and ask what to use. Avoid steam, saunas, and heavy workouts for a day, because heat and sweat can irritate freshly waxed skin. Book your next wax in three to four weeks, when the hair has grown back enough.

Questions people ask

Does face waxing hurt?

It stings for a few seconds, and the feeling fades fast. A gentle wax and a skilled hand keep the discomfort low, and the skin calms down within a few hours.

How long does face waxing last?

Three to four weeks for most people, because the hair is removed from the root. Regular waxing also makes the hair grow back finer over time.

Can I wear makeup after face waxing?

Wait a few hours before makeup, so the skin can calm down. A light moisturiser is fine, but heavy makeup on freshly waxed skin can irritate it.
