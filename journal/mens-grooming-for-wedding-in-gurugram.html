Mens Grooming for Wedding in Gurugram

01 Haircut timing for a wedding

Book the wedding haircut three to five days before the function. That gap lets the cut settle, so it looks natural in photos instead of freshly clipped. A cut on the morning of the wedding can still show sharp lines that had no time to soften.

Bring a photo of the style you want, and say whether you will wear a turban, a cap, or an open hairstyle. Headwear changes how the cut should be shaped, and the stylist plans around it.

02 Beard shaping and shaving

Beard shaping is best a day before the function or on the morning, depending on how fast your beard grows. A clean neckline and tidy cheek lines make the whole look sharper, even in a quick photo.

If you are growing a beard for the wedding, say so at the first visit. The stylist can shape it in stages over two or three visits, so the final shape is planned, not guessed at the last appointment.

03 Skin prep for grooms

Book a mens facial or cleanup a week before the wedding, not the day before. Skin needs a few days to settle, and a last minute facial can leave redness that shows in close photos.

Keep the home routine simple in the final week and avoid a new product for the first time. A groom who tries a new cream two days before the wedding is risking a reaction at the worst possible time.

04 Group bookings and the wedding morning

Brothers, fathers, and close friends often need grooming on the same day. Book the group together and say how many people are coming, so the team sets enough chairs and stylists and the morning stays on time.

On the wedding morning, arrive on time, with clean hair and skin, and tell the team the ceremony time. A clear ceremony time lets the team order the cuts and shaves so everyone is ready with time to spare.

05 The week before the wedding: a simple checklist

A week before the wedding, book the facial or cleanup, and confirm the haircut and beard appointments. That week is also the time to settle the outfit and the hairstyle, so nothing is decided on the morning itself.

Three to five days before, get the haircut. A day before, get the beard shaped, and on the morning, allow time for a final tidy-up and styling. Spreading the work this way keeps each step calm and unhurried.

On the wedding morning, arrive on time with clean hair, and bring a photo of the agreed style. A clear photo removes guesswork, and an on-time arrival keeps the whole group on schedule for the ceremony.

After the wedding, a hair spa and a beard tidy help you recover from a long week of functions. Book that follow-up a few days after the wedding, once the celebrations are over and your normal routine is back.
