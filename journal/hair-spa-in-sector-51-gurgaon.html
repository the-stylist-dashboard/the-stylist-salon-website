01 What a hair spa actually does

A hair spa is a deep wash, a relaxing head massage, a nourishing mask, and steam, finished in about 45 to 60 minutes. It suits dry, frizzy, or tired hair, and anyone who wants hair that falls soft and photographs well in the evening. Done on Karva Chauth morning, it leaves hair smooth and settled well before the puja.

A hair spa is not just a wash. We start by talking about your hair: dry ends, oily roots, dandruff, or colour that needs care. Then comes a deep cleanse, a head massage that helps you relax, a mask picked for your hair type, and steam to help the mask work properly. We finish with a gentle blow dry or natural dry, as you prefer.

The result is hair that feels softer, looks shinier, and is easier to style. It will not change your hair type overnight, and we will not promise that. What it does well is restore moisture and calm frizz, which is exactly what festive styling needs.

02 Who should get a hair spa now

If your hair feels rough after sun, heat styling, or colour, a hair spa is a good pick before a function. It also suits anyone with a dry scalp, because the head massage and mask calm irritation and flaking. Tell us about dandruff or hair fall when you book, so we pick the right mask.

If you have just had colour or smoothening, tell us that too. A hair spa after colour needs a gentler mask and cooler water, and we will adjust. Hair that is freshly coloured and over-treated in the same week can look dull, so timing matters.

03 Hair spa on Karva Chauth morning

A hair spa works well on the morning of Karva Chauth, because hair settles quickly after a spa, unlike skin after a strong facial. Book between 11 AM and 1 PM if you can, so you are home in good time for the afternoon prep and the puja muhurat at 5:38 PM.

Ask us to style simply after the spa, or leave hair to dry naturally if you plan to style it yourself later. Heavy heat styling straight after a spa undoes some of the softness, so a light blow dry or a loose style is the better choice for the evening.

04 How often to get a hair spa

Once every three to four weeks suits most people, and once a month is enough for healthy hair. If your hair is very dry or damaged, we may suggest every two to three weeks for a short while, then stretch the gap as it improves. More often than that is rarely needed.

Between visits, use a mild shampoo, skip very hot water, and keep heat styling to a minimum. A little care at home makes each hair spa last longer, and your hair will need less fixing at the next visit.

Questions we hear at the salon

How long does a hair spa take?

About 45 to 60 minutes, including wash, head massage, mask, and steam. Add a little time if you want a full blow dry afterwards.

Is a hair spa good on Karva Chauth morning?

Yes. Hair settles quickly after a spa, so a morning visit leaves hair soft and ready well before the evening puja.

Will a hair spa fix hair fall?

It helps scalp health and reduces breakage from dryness, but it is not a cure for medical hair fall. Tell us about hair fall when you book and we will be honest about what to expect.

How often should I get a hair spa?

Once every three to four weeks suits most people. Very dry or damaged hair may need every two to three weeks for a short while.
