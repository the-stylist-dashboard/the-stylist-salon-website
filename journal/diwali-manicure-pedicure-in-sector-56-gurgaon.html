Diwali Manicure and Pedicure in Sector 56, Gurgaon

The Stylist Unisex Salon · 30 October 2026 · 6 min read

For Diwali, book your manicure and pedicure two to three days before the festival, and pick gel polish if you want colour that lasts through cooking, cleaning, and several days of functions. A full manicure plus pedicure visit takes about ninety minutes to two hours.

01 Why nails matter for Diwali

Diwali photos show hands a lot: holding diyas, exchanging gifts, and serving food. Neat nails and soft feet complete a festive outfit in a way people notice, even if they cannot name why. A manicure and pedicure together leave both hands and feet ready, rather than one polished and one forgotten.

Festive footwear also makes pedicures worth it. Open sandals, juttis, and heels show feet clearly, and dry heels or rough skin stand out in bright light. A pedicure smooths heels, tidies cuticles, and leaves feet comfortable for long evenings on your feet.

02 Gel polish or regular polish

Gel polish lasts about two weeks without chipping, which suits Diwali week, when hands are busy with cooking and cleaning. Regular polish is gentler to remove and suits people who change colour often, but it may chip within a few days of heavy housework.

If you pick gel, tell us about past nail damage or sensitivity, because removal needs soaking, not peeling. Peeling gel off takes layers of your natural nail with it, and that damage lasts longer than the polish. We remove gel properly, even when it takes a few extra minutes.

03 What a good pedicure includes

A proper pedicure starts with a warm soak, then nail shaping, cuticle care, heel filing, a scrub, and a relaxing massage, finished with polish if you want it. The massage part matters, because feet carry you through long festive days, and a good pedicure leaves them less tired, not just prettier.

Tell us about cracked heels, fungal concerns, or diabetes before the pedicure starts, so we can adjust pressure and tools. Honest information here keeps the treatment safe, and a good salon will ask these questions without making it awkward.

04 Timing your Diwali nail visit

Book nails for 5 or 6 November if Diwali is on 8 November, so polish is fresh but you still have a day or two for cooking and prep without worrying about chips. Gel booked earlier in the week also works, since it holds up well for two weeks.

Avoid a brand new nail extension or a very long shape right before Diwali if you will be cooking a lot, because long nails and heavy kitchen work do not mix well. A medium, rounded shape is the practical festive choice, and it still looks elegant with traditional outfits.

Questions we hear at the salon

When should I book a Diwali manicure and pedicure?

Two to three days before Diwali, so around 5 or 6 November 2026. Gel polish booked earlier in the week also lasts well.

How long does a manicure and pedicure take?

About ninety minutes to two hours for both together, a little longer if you add gel polish or nail art.

Does gel polish damage nails?

Not when applied and removed properly. Never peel gel off, because peeling removes layers of your natural nail.

Should I get a pedicure if I wear closed shoes?

Yes, because a pedicure also cares for heels, cuticles, and tired feet, not just polish. Comfort matters as much as looks.
