02 Cuts that suit different face shapes

The right cut depends more on your face than on any photo you bring. Round faces usually suit layers and some length around the sides, which make the face look longer. Longer faces suit softer layers and a bit of width at the sides. Square faces soften with textured ends instead of a hard, blunt line.

Hair texture matters just as much. Thick hair needs weight taken out or it puffs up in Gurgaon humidity. Fine hair needs a cut that keeps the ends looking full instead of thinning them out. A good stylist checks all of this before picking up the scissors, and will tell you honestly if a photo you like will not work with your hair type.

03 What to say in your consultation

The consultation is where a good cut is decided, so use it well. Tell your stylist how you style your hair on a normal morning, how much time you actually spend on it, and how often you are willing to visit for upkeep. Mention what you hated about your last cut, because that tells us more than what you liked.

Bring one or two photos if you have them, but be ready for honest advice. A photo of a celebrity with different hair texture and a full styling team is not always realistic, and a good stylist will say so and suggest the closest version that works for you. That honesty is what separates a decent salon visit from a great one.

04 Colour, styling, and finishing touches

A cut often pairs well with colour or styling, and your stylist can plan both in one visit. Highlights, balayage, or a global colour add depth to a fresh cut, but they need a patch test if you are colouring for the first time. Tell us when you book on 81781 59945 so we allow enough time for both services.

The finish matters too. A proper blow dry shows you how the cut is meant to sit, and your stylist should show you how to recreate it at home with basic tools. If you have a function coming up, a hair spa before the cut can leave your hair softer and easier to shape, so ask about combining the two.

05 How often to come back, and how to book

Men usually need a shape-up every three to four weeks to keep the cut looking sharp, especially for short styles and beard shaping. Women with medium to long hair can stretch to six to eight weeks for a shape-up, though fringe trims in between help a lot. Colour clients need root touch-ups every four to six weeks, and highlights last longer, often eight to twelve weeks.

The three-week test: A good cut should still sit well after three weeks of your normal routine. If it only looks good on day one, tell your stylist next time so the cut can be adjusted.
