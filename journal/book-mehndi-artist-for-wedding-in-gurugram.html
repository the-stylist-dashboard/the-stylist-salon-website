01 When to call, and what to say

Call as soon as the wedding date is fixed, especially in peak wedding season, because wedding mehndi slots go first and the application itself needs a full day planned around it. On the call, say your wedding date, the main function date, and whether you want bridal work, party work for family, or both. That lets the team hold the right slot and plan how many hands need work on the day.

Also say how many people need mehndi, and whether the bride wants full traditional work or a lighter design. A bride alone and a bride plus family are different bookings, and the time needed changes a lot. Booking the full group in one call beats adding people later, when the slot may already be full.

02 Picking the design before the day

Bring your outfit or a photo of it, and reference photos of designs you like and designs you do not want. Tell the artist about your sleeve length and jewellery, because both change how a design reads on the hand. A design planned around the outfit looks planned in photos, while a design picked without the outfit can clash on the day, however well it is applied.

Decide the coverage before the application day: full hands and arms, hands only, or a lighter design with open space. Full work takes hours and needs a calm slot with no rushing, so do not plan a long function or a heavy outfit trial for the same day. A slightly simpler design applied well beats a very dense design that runs late.

03 How the application day runs

Most brides book the application one or two days before the main function, so the colour deepens in time and the hands look their best in photos. On the day, wear a loose-sleeved outfit, keep your hands free of lotion and oil before the artist starts, and arrange help with simple tasks once the paste is on. Plan food and rest around the mehndi, not the other way round, because a calm application day gives a cleaner design.

After the paste comes off, keep the design away from water for as long as you can, and avoid soap on the hands for the first hours. Warmth helps the colour deepen, so keep your hands warm and avoid water-heavy work until the colour has settled. Judge the final shade after the first day, not straight after the paste is removed.

04 Booking for family and guests too

If family members or guests also want mehndi, book them in the same plan, with their own slots on the same day or the day before. Party mehndi for family is usually lighter and faster than bridal work, but it still needs a proper slot, not a squeezed gap at the end of the bridal application. Tell the team the headcount early, so the day runs on time for everyone.
