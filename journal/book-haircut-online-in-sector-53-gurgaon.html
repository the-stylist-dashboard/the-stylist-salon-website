Book a Haircut Online in Sector 53, Gurgaon

The Stylist Unisex Salon · 22 October 2026 · 5 min read

Booking a haircut should take two minutes, not twenty. If you live in Sector 53, our Sector 52 branch is five minutes away, and booking is one phone call. Here is exactly how it works, what to tell us, and how to get the slot you want.

01 How booking actually works

There is no app to download and no form to fill. Call 8178159945. Tell us the service you want: a haircut, a haircut plus beard trim, a haircut plus hair spa. Tell us your preferred day and time. We confirm your slot right there on the call. That is the whole process.

Why a phone call and not an online form. Because a two-minute conversation answers what a form cannot: which stylist suits your hair, how long your service will take, and whether your preferred time is realistic.

02 What to tell us when you call

Four things make your booking smooth. First, the service: be specific. Second, your hair length and type, so we match you with the right stylist. Third, your preferred stylist, if you have one. Fourth, two time options, in case your first choice is full.

If it is your first visit, say so. We will leave extra time for the consultation, which is free. Bring two or three reference photos. Words like "a bit shorter" mean different things to different people; photos remove all doubt.

03 The best times to book

Weekday mornings and early afternoons are the quietest. You get the most relaxed consultation, the least waiting, and your pick of stylists. Evenings after 6 PM get busy, especially Thursday through Sunday. Saturday and Sunday evenings are the peak: book those a day or two ahead.

If your schedule is tight, tell us when you call. We will find the slot with the least waiting around it. And if you are flexible, ask what is free today. Same-day bookings are common on weekdays.

04 Walk-ins: when they work and when they do not

Walk-ins are welcome any day except Tuesday. On a quiet weekday morning, you will be in the chair within minutes. On a busy Saturday evening, you might wait, or the stylist you want might be booked.

Simple rule: if you want a specific stylist, or you are coming on a weekend, call ahead. If you are flexible about who cuts your hair and it is a weekday, walk in. Either way, you are welcome.

05 What happens at your appointment

Arrive five minutes early if you can. You get a free consultation first: the stylist asks about your hair history, what you want, and what you use at home. Then the cut, with a wash and a basic style included. You see the price before we start, so there are no surprises at the counter.

After the cut, the stylist shows you how to maintain it at home and tells you honestly when to come back. Most short cuts need a revisit in four to six weeks; longer styles can go eight.

06 Getting here from Sector 53

Sector 53 is right next door. Drive towards Artemis Hospital and you will reach High Street 52 in five to seven minutes. We are at Shop No. 86, High Street 52, opposite Artemis Hospital. Parking is right outside the strip.

We are open Wednesday to Monday, 11 AM to 9 PM, closed Tuesdays. Our second branch at Ground Floor, The Arzoo Apartments in Sector 51 works the same way if that side is closer to you.

The two-option trick: when you call, give us two time options instead of one. It doubles the chance we slot you in without a callback.
