01 What to decide before you pick a colour

Good hair colour starts with a conversation, not a shade card. At The Stylist, we ask about your skin tone, your natural hair colour, how much grey you have, and how much upkeep you are willing to do. Those four answers narrow the choice faster than flipping through fifty swatches.

From Sector 53, you are close to both our salons, and a short consultation before your appointment saves a lot of regret later. Bring a photo of a look you like, but be ready for honest advice if that shade needs bleach your hair cannot handle yet.

02 Shades that suit Indian skin and hair

Indian hair is usually dark and thick, so warm browns, caramel, copper, and chocolate shades tend to look natural and grow out gracefully. Cooler ash shades can work, but they need more lifting, more upkeep, and a good purple shampoo at home.

If you have warm undertones, golden brown and honey highlights will light up your face without looking harsh. If your skin is cooler, a soft chocolate brown or a muted burgundy is often the safer first step into colour.

03 Root touch-up, global colour, and highlights explained

A root touch-up covers regrowth only, usually every four to six weeks, and keeps your colour looking fresh without recolouring the whole head. It is the lowest-maintenance option for people who already have a colour they love.

Global colour changes the whole head to one shade. It gives the most even, polished result, but it needs regular root work. Highlights, by contrast, add lighter strands through your hair for depth and movement, and they grow out more softly.

04 What happens at your appointment

We start by checking your hair history: past colour, keratin or smoothening, and any sensitivity. If you are new to colour, we do a patch test first, because a reaction is rare but not worth risking.

Next we apply the colour, check it at intervals, wash it out, and finish with a conditioning treatment and a blow dry. Global colour usually takes two to three hours, while highlights can take longer depending on how many foils you need.

You will leave with clear aftercare advice and a realistic date for your next visit. If you want to add a haircut, tell us when you book on 81781 59945 so we allow enough time.

05 How to keep your colour fresh at home

Use a shampoo made for coloured hair, wash with lukewarm water, and cut back on daily heat styling. A colour-protecting mask once a week does more for your shade than any expensive serum used now and then.

Sun and hard Gurgaon water fade colour faster than most people expect. A hat on long sunny days and a shower filter, if you can manage one, both help your colour last between visits.

Questions we hear at the salon

How much does hair colour cost in Sector 53?

It depends on your hair length, the type of colour, and how much product you need. Call us on 81781 59945 with a photo of your hair and we will give you an honest price range before you book.

Will hair colour damage my hair?

Any colour changes the hair a little, but the right product, the right developer strength, and proper aftercare keep damage low. We will not push a shade that needs heavy bleach if your hair is already weak.

How long does hair colour last?

Root colour needs a touch-up every four to six weeks. Highlights and balayage last longer between visits because they grow out more softly, often eight to twelve weeks.

Do you do a patch test before colour?

Yes, for first-time colour clients. It takes a few minutes and it rules out sensitivity before we apply colour to your full head.
