Treatment decision guide

Start with the result you want.

Use these goals to begin the conversation. Your stylist can narrow the options after seeing the hair in person.

I want less frizz, but I want to keep movement.

Keratin or cysteine may be considered when smoother, shinier and easier-to-manage hair is the goal without prioritising a pin-straight result.

I want a straighter, sleeker finish.

Nanoplastia, smoothening or rebonding may be considered. The recommendation depends on the degree of straightness, previous colour or bleach, current hair strength and future maintenance.

My hair looks dry, rough or stressed.

Hair botox or a hair spa may suit a conditioning and softness goal. If the hair is heavily lightened or chemically treated, disclose the full history before adding another service.

I want even colour or grey coverage.

Global colour or a root touch-up can create more even coverage. Shade choice depends on the existing base, any previous colour and the target finish.

I want dimension, brightness or a gradient.

Highlights, face-framing placement, balayage or ombré can be planned around the desired contrast. Some target shades need lightening, while others do not.

I want curls or lasting waves.

A perm changes the texture into a selected curl or wave pattern. Consultation helps align the technique with length, density, existing treatments and preferred curl size.

Haircuts, styling and hair spa

What is included in a haircut, and how should I prepare?

A haircut is planned around your texture, density, face shape, current length and daily styling routine, not only the price on the menu.

Included
A short consultation, shape and length planning, the cut itself and a finished shape check. Ask while booking whether washing or a full blow-dry is included in the selected service.
Process
The stylist reviews how the hair falls, agrees on length and layering, cuts in sections and refines the perimeter and balance before finishing.
Time
Most cuts need about 30 to 60 minutes. Restyling, very long hair or detailed layers can take longer.
Prepare
Bring a reference image and explain how you normally part, tie and style your hair. Disclose extensions or a recent chemical treatment.
Aftercare
Ask how to reproduce the finish, which brush or product suits the cut and when the shape is likely to need refreshing.
Limits
A reference cut will sit differently on different textures and densities. The stylist may adapt fringe, layers or length to suit your hair behaviour.

Time
Allow about 30 to 90 minutes. Detailed updos, dense hair and accessory placement can take longer.
Prepare

Aftercare
Keep the finished hair dry, avoid excessive touching and follow the stylist's advice for pins, hold product and safe removal.

Included

What general aftercare applies after hair services?

The correct routine depends on the service, so follow the instructions given for your exact treatment rather than combining advice from different treatments.

Washing
Respect first-wash timing where relevant and use the recommended gentle or colour-safe cleanser and conditioner.
Heat
Use heat protection, avoid repeated high-temperature passes and reduce unnecessary styling stress.
Maintenance
Schedule root, tone, conditioning or shape refreshes according to visible need, not a universal calendar.
Warning signs
If the scalp or skin reacts unexpectedly, stop adding products and seek appropriate professional or medical advice.
