01 What a bridal booking actually includes

A bridal makeup booking is not one sitting. It is usually two appointments: a trial and the wedding day itself. At the trial, you sit down with the artist, talk through the look you want, and try the full face once. Photos get taken in daylight and under flash so you can see how the makeup reads on camera. On the wedding day, the artist arrives with everything prepared: base products matched to your skin type, eye makeup, lashes, hair styling, and saree or lehenga draping if you want it. Ask for a written list of what is included before you pay anything. Some quotes quietly leave out lashes, hair, or the touch-up kit, and you only find out on the morning of the wedding. A clear list keeps everyone honest.

02 Match the makeup to the venue and the season

Most Gurgaon weddings between October and February are outdoors in the evening, and winter makeup has its own rules. Cold air dries the skin out, so hydration and a well-prepped base matter more than heavy layering. Summer weddings, usually indoors under strong lights, need makeup that holds up against heat and camera flash. Tell your artist where the wedding is and what time it starts. An artist who understands the venue lighting will adjust the base and contour so you do not look washed out or shiny in photos. This is also why a trial under similar conditions helps so much. If your wedding is an evening outdoor event in Sector 50, say so up front, because the plan changes with the light.

03 The trial is the most important appointment

Never book bridal makeup without a trial. The trial is where you find out whether the artist's hand matches your taste. Bring reference photos, but pick photos of faces with a skin tone and eye shape close to yours, or the reference will not translate. Wear a top with a neckline similar to your outfit so the overall look reads right. Step outside and check the makeup in daylight. Take phone photos and flash photos. If something feels off, say it at the trial, not on the wedding morning. A good artist welcomes changes. A defensive one is a warning sign.

04 When to book bridal makeup in Sector 50

Wedding season in Gurgaon runs roughly October to February, and good artists fill up fast. If your wedding falls in those months, book two to three months ahead. For off-season weddings, four to six weeks is usually enough. 05 Prepare your skin, but do not overdo it

The best makeup sits on calm, hydrated skin. Sleep properly the night before, drink water, and moisturise. Do not try any new skincare product in the week before the wedding. That is how allergic reactions and surprise breakouts happen. If you want facials before the wedding, start at least a month out and stop a week before the big day. Waxing and threading should be done two to three days prior, not the day before, so redness has time to settle. Exfoliate gently, skip anything harsh, and let your skin rest. Calm skin takes makeup far better than skin that has been over-treated.

Before you pay: ask for a full written breakdown of what is included - lashes, hair styling, draping, the touch-up kit - plus the artist's arrival time and how long the session takes. Surprises on the wedding morning are the last thing you need.

A basic bridal look with quality products costs less than a full HD or airbrush package with hair and draping. Always ask for a written breakdown so you know exactly what the quote covers. At our salon, call 81781 59945, describe what you need, and we will give you a straight quote with no hidden add-ons.

Should I do a trial before booking bridal makeup?

Yes, always. The trial is where you and the artist agree on the look, and it takes an hour or two. If an artist refuses a trial or charges an absurd fee for one, treat that as a warning sign and keep looking.

Will my makeup last through a full wedding day?

It should, if the prep is done right. A good base starts with skin prep, a primer suited to your skin type, and setting at the right steps. Keep a small touch-up kit with blotting paper, powder, and your lip colour. Most brides need one quick touch-up between functions, and that is completely normal.

Airbrush or traditional makeup, which is better?

Neither is automatically better. Airbrush gives a light, even finish and suits long days. Traditional HD makeup gives more coverage control and suits most skin types. The right choice depends on your skin and the look you want. Ask your artist to explain why they recommend one over the other for your face, not just quote a price difference.
