01 Start with your skin, not the menu

Most people pick a facial by its name or its price, and that is backwards. Oily skin with blackheads needs deep cleansing first, because no glow product sits well on clogged pores. Dry, tight skin needs hydration first, because brightness without moisture just looks flat. Ask your therapist to look at your skin in daylight before you confirm anything.

Tell the therapist about recent breakouts, sensitivity, and any product that stung or caused redness. That short conversation decides more than the brand on the bottle. A facial planned around your skin will look better a week later, not just for an hour after you leave.

02 What a proper facial includes

A proper facial runs in order: cleansing, exfoliation, extraction where needed, a face and neck massage, a mask, and moisturiser. The massage step matters more than people think. It is not decoration. It helps the skin relax and helps product absorb, and it is the part clients remember when they decide whether to come back.

After a facial, keep the rest of the day simple. Skip heavy makeup for a few hours, do not scrub or exfoliate the same evening, and use sunscreen the next morning, since fresh skin is more sensitive to sun. If mild redness shows up, it usually settles by the next day. Call the salon if redness or irritation lasts longer than a day or two, because that tells the therapist something about your skin for next time.

Extraction should be gentle and only where needed. If your skin is inflamed or you have active acne, say so before the facial starts, because the plan changes. A good therapist will adjust the steps rather than push through a fixed routine.

03 When to book before Diwali

Book three to five days before Diwali, not on the day itself. Skin can look mildly pink for a day after a deep cleansing facial, and that gap lets it settle so makeup sits cleanly on the night. Diwali falls on Sunday 8 November 2026, so the sensible window is the first week of November, and those slots fill fast.

For regular upkeep, once a month is a sensible rhythm for most skin types. If your skin is sensitive or acne prone, ask your therapist for a plan that fits your skin, not a fixed schedule copied from a poster.
