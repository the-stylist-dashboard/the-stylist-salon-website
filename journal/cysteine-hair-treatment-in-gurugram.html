01 What a cysteine treatment does

A cysteine treatment works on the hair fibre to reduce frizz and make hair smoother and easier to style. It suits hair that frizzes in humidity, takes long to dry, or needs daily heat styling to look neat. The result is softer, calmer hair that still moves naturally, which is why many clients prefer it to a treatment that forces hair completely straight.

It is not a promise of pin-straight hair, and any salon promising that from this treatment alone is overselling it. If you want pin-straight hair, say so at consultation, because the right treatment may be different. Honest advice at this stage saves a disappointing result and a second appointment to fix it.

02 Who it suits, and what to mention first

Cysteine suits wavy, frizzy, and unruly hair, including hair that has been coloured, as long as the stylist knows the full history. Tell your stylist about recent colour, bleach, keratin, or smoothening work, because treated hair changes the plan and the appointment length. Colour and treatment need the right order, so mention any colour plans at consultation too.

If your hair is very fine and limp, ask whether this treatment is right for you, because smoothing a head of hair that already lacks volume needs a different approach from smoothing thick, frizzy hair. A good stylist will adjust the plan or suggest a different option, rather than selling the same treatment to every client.

03 How long it takes, and aftercare

Allow a half day for the appointment, because the steps take time: cleanse, sectioned application, processing, drying, and heat sealing. The exact time depends on length, density, and how your hair takes the treatment, so keep your day clear rather than booking something tight right after.

Aftercare protects the result. Use gentle cleansing, avoid harsh shampoo in the first weeks, and use heat protection when you style. Ask your stylist to write down the aftercare for your hair type, because general advice online does not fit every head of hair. Swimming in chlorinated water and washing too often in the first days shorten the life of the result.

04 Before wedding season, and booking

Wedding season is a common reason to book this treatment, because smooth, manageable hair makes styling easier across several functions. Book it with enough gap before the first function, so the hair settles and you can see how it behaves in daily life before the wedding week. Do not book it the day before a function.
