Winter Skin Dryness in NCR: A Practical Guide

The Stylist Unisex Salon · 28 September 2026 · 6 min read

By mid-December in Gurugram your face feels tight by 10 AM, your hands look older than they are, and no amount of water seems to fix it. Winter here is dry in a way that surprises people. Cold air, indoor heaters and long hot showers strip moisture faster than your routine can replace it. Here is what actually helps, and what is just marketing.

01 Why NCR winter hits skin harder

Delhi NCR winter is not just cold, it is dry. Daytime humidity drops hard, and most homes and offices run heaters or blowers that dry the air further. Hot showers feel great in January and strip the skin's natural oils at the same time. The result is the classic winter set: tight cheeks, flaky patches around the nose and mouth, itchy shins and cracked knuckles. If your skin was fine in October and angry by December, the weather did it, not your age.

02 The routine that actually works

You do not need twelve products. You need the right order and a heavier moisturiser than your summer one.

  • Gentle cleanser: foaming washes that leave skin squeaky are too harsh for winter. Switch to a cream or lotion cleanser.
  • Moisturiser on damp skin: apply within a minute of washing your face. Damp skin holds the moisture in.
  • Layer on the driest days: a hydrating serum under your cream when the air is at its worst. Skip it on days your skin feels fine.
  • Sunscreen still: winter sun in NCR is weaker but it is still there, especially on morning commutes. Keep the habit.
  • Night: a slightly richer cream than your day one. That is the whole routine.

03 Hands, lips and feet

The face gets all the attention and the extremities do all the suffering. Lips crack because they have no oil glands of their own. Hands get washed constantly and rarely get moisturised after. Feet in open footwear dry out even in winter.

  • Lips: a simple balm, reapplied. Matte lipsticks make dryness worse, so layer balm underneath.
  • Hands: keep a hand cream at your desk and one by the sink. Apply after every wash.
  • Feet: moisturise after your shower and wear socks for an hour. It works embarrassingly well.

04 Where the salon fits in

When home care is not enough, a hydrating facial or a gentle cleanup resets things. Facials here run Rs 799-1999 depending on the treatment, and in winter the hydrating ones earn their keep. What to skip: anything aggressive when your skin is already irritated or flaking. Tell your therapist your skin is winter-sensitive before they choose products. Questions we hear at the salon

Why does my skin get so dry in Delhi winter?

Cold air holds less moisture, indoor heaters dry the air further, and hot showers strip the skin's oils. Together they pull water out of your skin faster than your routine can replace it. The fix is a heavier moisturiser, gentler cleansing and lukewarm showers, not drinking more water. Water intake helps the body, but it does not rehydrate dry skin.

Should I still wear sunscreen in winter?

Yes. Winter sun in NCR is weaker but ultraviolet light is still present, especially during morning commutes and on clear days. Sunscreen also acts as an extra barrier layer over moisturiser. If your winter moisturiser already has SPF, that counts. The habit matters more than the product.

Are facials worth it in winter?

A hydrating facial or gentle cleanup can help when home care is not enough, especially for flaky patches and dullness. Book it when your skin is calm, not when it is actively irritated or cracked. Tell your therapist your skin is winter-sensitive so they pick suitable products. In winter, gentler treatments beat aggressive ones every time.

How do I stop my lips from cracking every winter?

Lips crack because they have no oil glands of their own. Use a simple balm and reapply through the day, especially before stepping out. Layer balm under matte lipstick, which makes dryness worse. Avoid licking your lips. Saliva evaporates fast and leaves them drier than before.
