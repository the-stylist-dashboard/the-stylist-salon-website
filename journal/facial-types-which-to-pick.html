Facial Types Explained: Which One Matches Your Skin

The Stylist Unisex Salon · 28 September 2026 · 7 min read

You are staring at a salon menu with twelve facials and no idea what half of them mean. Fruit, gold, pearl, wine, anti-ageing, hydra. The names sound like a dessert counter. A client told us last month she had been booking "whatever was on offer" for two years. This guide decodes the common facial types in plain language: what each session involves, who usually picks it, and how to choose without guessing.

01 The classic facial: the baseline everything else builds on

The classic facial is cleanse, exfoliate, extract, massage, mask, moisturise. No fancy actives, no machines doing the heavy lifting, just a thorough version of the standard sequence done well. It suits first-timers and anyone who wants solid maintenance without committing to a speciality treatment. If you have never had a facial, start here and see how your skin responds before trying anything stronger.

02 Fruit and glow facials: the pre-event favourites

Fruit facials use creams and gels with fruit extracts, papaya and orange being the common ones, and they are picked for brightness and a fresh look. The session follows the classic sequence with fruit-based products handling the exfoliation and masking. Clients usually book these a few days before a party or a photoshoot because the glow shows up quickly. If your skin is very sensitive, mention it upfront, since fruit-based products can tingle.

03 Gold, pearl and other luxury facials: what you are paying for

These follow the same sequence with premium product lines and a longer massage. The honest truth from the salon floor: the experience is lovely, the products feel rich, and clients enjoy every minute. Whether the gold leaf itself does anything special is beside the point for most buyers; they are paying for the ritual and the extra hands-on time. Our facials run Rs 799 to 1999, and the luxury variants sit at the top of that range.

04 Anti-ageing and firming facials: firmer-feeling skin, not a time machine

These use richer creams, longer massage with upward strokes, and masks aimed at mature or tired-looking skin. Clients usually notice their skin feeling firmer and looking more rested for several days afterwards. No facial reverses ageing; a good one makes your skin look its best for a stretch of time. 05 Hydra facials: the machine-led option

Hydra-dermabrasion uses a device with an exfoliating tip, suction and serum infusion instead of relying only on manual massage. The session feels different: cool, wet and methodical, with almost no downtime. It suits people who want visible freshness without the redness that sometimes follows heavy manual extraction, and it has become the most requested option for pre-event weeks.

06 How to actually choose

Start with your skin, not the menu. Dull and tired: fruit or glow. Never had one: classic. Special occasion in a week: fruit, gold or hydra. Mature skin wanting firmness: anti-ageing. Sensitive: say so first and let the therapist pick mild products. And drop the idea that costlier always means better for you. The right facial is the one matched to your skin on that day.

Questions we hear at the salon

Which facial is best for dull, tired skin?

Fruit or glow facials are the usual pick for dullness, since they focus on exfoliation and brightness and the results show quickly. A hydra facial is the other strong option if you want visible freshness without much downtime. Tell the therapist your skin feels dull and let them confirm.

Are gold facials worth the money?

They are worth it if you enjoy a luxurious, unhurried session with premium products and a long massage. The experience is genuinely pleasant. But for pure results on dull or tired skin, a well-done fruit or hydra facial at a lower price often delivers just as much.

Can I get a facial with sensitive skin?

Yes, but speak up before the session starts. The therapist can choose milder products, skip strong exfoliation, and keep the massage gentle. A patch test on new products is sensible. Avoid booking a strong treatment right before an important event.

How do I know a facial suited my skin?

Your skin should feel comfortable, look fresher, and show no lingering redness or new breakouts in the following days. Mild tightness right after is common. If irritation lasts beyond a day or two, tell the salon; the products or technique need adjusting next time.
