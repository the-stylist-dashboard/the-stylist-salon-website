01 What a party makeup appointment is

A party makeup appointment is a booked visit where the artist does your makeup for a party, a sangeet, a reception, or a family function, in a set slot with enough time for the full look. The visit starts with skin prep, then base, eyes, lashes, and lips, and the artist checks the look with you before you leave. Hair styling can be added in the same visit, so you leave ready for the function, without a second trip.

The appointment is different from a bridal booking, because it is a single function look, not a week-long plan, so it takes less time and costs less. It suits guests, bridesmaids, and the groom's family, as well as anyone who wants to look polished for a special evening. Tell the artist how long the function lasts, because a look that must hold for six hours needs a different base from one that only needs to last two.

02 When to book the appointment

Book a party makeup appointment a few days before the function, because wedding season slots fill fast in Gurugram, and a weekend evening slot is the first to go. If you book on the day itself, call early in the morning, so the salon can fit you in before the function rush. A weekday function is usually easier to book than a Saturday evening one, and it gives the artist more time for your look.

Tell the salon your function start time when you book, so the appointment can be placed with a gap for dressing and travel. If you are coming with a group, such as bridesmaids or sisters, book together, because a group booking needs more time and more than one artist. A group that books together also leaves ready together, without a long wait between people.

03 What to tell the salon when you book

Tell the salon the date and time of your function, the outfit you will wear, and whether you want hair styling in the same visit, so enough time is set aside. Mention your skin honestly, including any sensitivity or past reactions, because the base and the prep are picked for that. Bring a photo of your outfit and a photo of the makeup look you like, because both help the artist understand your taste fast.

Also say how you usually wear makeup, because a guest who never wears makeup will feel odd in a heavy look, while someone who loves a bold lip will feel dull in a soft one. The artist can match the look to you, but only if you say plainly what you like and what you do not. A clear brief at booking saves time at the chair and gives a better result.
