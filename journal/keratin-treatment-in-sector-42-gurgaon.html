If your hair frizzes every time Gurgaon's humidity shifts, keratin might be the answer. Here is what it does, how long it lasts, and whether it is right for your hair.

01 What keratin does to your hair

Keratin is the protein your hair is already made of. The treatment fills damaged gaps in the hair shaft and seals them with heat, leaving smoother hair that lies flat and dries faster. It wears off gradually over three to five months.

It suits frizzy, wavy and slightly unruly hair best. If your hair is already fine and flat, tell your stylist you want frizz control rather than straightening, and they will adjust the technique.

02 Keratin versus smoothening

Smoothening uses stronger chemicals to restructure the hair and lasts six to eight months, but it changes your natural texture more permanently. Keratin is gentler: less restructuring, a more natural finish, and a fade-out instead of a regrowth line.

Pick keratin if your main enemy is frizz and you want easier mornings. Pick smoothening if you want properly straight hair for longer. If your hair is damaged, neither should be done until it is repaired; a consultation sorts this out in ten minutes.

03 The 72-hour rule and other aftercare

The first three days decide a lot. Do not tie your hair up, tuck it behind your ears, or wear clips, because the keratin is still setting and marks can lock in. Do not wash it for 48 to 72 hours, then switch to a sulphate-free shampoo and conditioner; regular shampoo strips the treatment out fast.

Avoid sea water and chlorinated pools without a cap, or rinse your hair immediately afterwards. And go easy on heat styling: you should not need the straightener much now.

The week-one test: if your hair still puffs up within a week, the treatment was rushed or the product was wrong for your hair. A proper result survives a humid Gurgaon day with nothing but a blow-dry.

04 Who should skip keratin for now

Keratin is not for everyone. If your hair is breaking from previous chemical work, repair it first. If you are pregnant or nursing, many stylists advise waiting because of the fumes during the heat-sealing step. And if your hair is very short, the value drops.

Be honest about your washing habits. If you wash daily with strong shampoo and swim every morning, keratin will fade in weeks, not months. The treatment works for the routine you actually have.

05 What it costs and what the price covers

Keratin is priced by hair length. Ask what the quote includes: the treatment, the first salon wash and finish, and the aftercare products for home. Regular shampoo afterwards can halve the treatment's life.

Be cautious of keratin priced far below the market. Cheap products can contain high levels of formaldehyde, which creates the harsh fumes. We use formaldehyde-free products and quote the full package upfront.

Questions we hear at the salon

How long does a keratin treatment last?

A good keratin treatment lasts three to five months, fading gradually. Sulphate-free shampoo and less heat styling stretch it toward the longer end. Swimming in chlorinated water without protection shortens it, so wear a swim cap or rinse your hair right after the pool.

What is the difference between keratin and smoothening?

Smoothening changes the hair's structure with stronger chemicals and lasts six to eight months, but it alters your natural texture more permanently. Keratin coats and seals the hair with protein, tames frizz, and wears off gradually. For frizzy but healthy hair, keratin is usually the gentler pick.

Is keratin treatment safe for coloured hair?

Yes, keratin works well on coloured hair and can lock the colour in. Colour your hair before the keratin session, not after, because the treatment seals the cuticle and later colour will not take as evenly. Wait at least two weeks between the two.
