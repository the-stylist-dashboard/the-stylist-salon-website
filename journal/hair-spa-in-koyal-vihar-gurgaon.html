01 What a hair spa session includes

A hair spa at The Stylist starts with a wash that clears dust and product build up. Your stylist then works a mask through the lengths, gives a proper head massage, and finishes with steam so the mask can sink in. The point is simple: clean scalp, soft lengths, and hair that is easier to manage at home.

If your hair is dry, rough, or frizzy after Gurugram heat and hard water, this is the session to start with before you consider a chemical treatment. It gives you a clear read on what your hair actually needs, because a clean, well fed head of hair behaves very differently from one weighed down by build up.

02 Who it suits, and who should wait

A hair spa suits most hair types, including coloured hair that feels dry and a scalp that feels tight. It suits men and women, and it is a good first step if you are unsure what your hair needs. Tell your stylist about recent colour, bleach, keratin, or smoothening work before the session starts, so the mask and the massage pressure can be adjusted.

How often you need a hair spa depends on your hair. Very dry or frizzy hair benefits from a session every two to three weeks at first, then once a month for upkeep. Normal hair usually holds a monthly rhythm well. Your stylist should adjust the gap based on how your hair feels between visits, not sell you a fixed package. If your hair starts feeling heavy or coated, the gap is too short or the mask is too rich for your hair type.

If you have an active scalp infection or open cuts on the scalp, wait and ask your stylist first. That is not a sales line. A mask and steam on broken skin will not help, and a good stylist will tell you so and suggest when to come back.

03 How long it takes, and what to ask for

Allow 45 to 60 minutes. When you call, say you want a hair spa, tell the team your hair length and whether your scalp feels dry, oily, or sensitive, and ask for a slot that leaves time for the steam step. Do not rush the last ten minutes. That is when the mask and steam do most of their work.

After the session, ask what to use at home for the next two washes. Most people undo a good hair spa with a harsh shampoo the same evening. A gentle wash and a light conditioner keep the softness for much longer.
