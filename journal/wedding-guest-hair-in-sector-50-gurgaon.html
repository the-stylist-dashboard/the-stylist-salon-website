Wedding & styling A guest look that lasts the whole function

Wedding Guest Hair in Sector 50, Gurgaon

The Stylist Unisex Salon · 30 October 2026 · 6 min read

For a wedding as a guest, pick a blow dry with soft waves or a secure updo, book it for the day of the function, and do any colour refresh at least three days earlier. A guest styling visit takes about forty five minutes to ninety minutes, depending on length and the style you pick.

01 Blow dry or updo: which lasts longer

A blow dry with soft waves suits most outfits and photographs naturally, but it can fall flat in heat or humidity over a long evening. An updo, a low bun, or a braided style holds up better through dancing, heat, and hours of photos, and it suits heavier outfits and statement earrings.

Tell your stylist how long the function runs and whether it is indoors or outdoors. Outdoor winter weddings in Gurugram still get warm under lights, so a style that survives heat matters more than a style that only looks good for the first hour.

02 Colour refresh before a wedding

If your colour has grown out, refresh it three to five days before the wedding, not the day before. Fresh colour can look harsh in photos, and a few days of settling gives a softer, more natural result. A gloss in the same visit can add shine without changing your shade.

Roots showing in wedding photos bother many guests, so check your parting and hairline in daylight a week before the function. If roots are visible, that is the time to book, not the night before, when slots are scarce and colour has no time to settle.

03 Planning around your outfit

Bring your outfit photo to the styling visit, or at least know whether your neckline is high, low, or embellished. High necklines pair well with hair up, so the outfit detail shows, while simpler necklines suit hair down with waves. Earrings matter too: heavy earrings suit hair pulled back, so they do not tangle.

If you wear a dupatta or a veil-style drape, tell your stylist, because pins and placement change with it. A style planned without the drape in mind often needs redoing at the venue, which is the last thing you want before photos.

04 Making the style last all evening

Wash hair the day before, not the morning of the function, because slightly lived-in hair holds a style better than freshly washed, slippery hair. Use a light styling product, not heavy oil, and let your stylist finish with a hold that suits your hair type.

Carry a few extra pins and a small comb in your bag, and ask your stylist to show you one quick fix for your style. Two minutes of guidance at the salon saves a lot of worry during the function, and most styles only need one small adjustment all evening.

Questions we hear at the salon

Should I wash my hair before wedding guest styling?

Wash it the day before. Slightly lived-in hair holds waves and updos better than freshly washed, slippery hair.

How long does wedding guest hair styling take?

About forty five to ninety minutes, depending on hair length and whether you pick a blow dry, waves, or an updo.

When should I refresh hair colour before a wedding?

Three to five days before the function, so colour settles and looks natural in photos.

Will my hairstyle last a long wedding function?

Yes if it is planned for heat and dancing. Updos hold longest, and a good blow dry with the right hold lasts most evenings.
