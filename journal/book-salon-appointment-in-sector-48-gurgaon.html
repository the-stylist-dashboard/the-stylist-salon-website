Book Salon Appointment in Sector 48, Gurgaon

The Stylist Unisex Salon · 25 October 2026 · 6 min read

Booking a salon appointment should take two minutes, but it often turns into a confusing exchange of messages, unclear prices, and a slot that does not fit the service. If you live in Sector 48, here is exactly how booking works at The Stylist Unisex Salon in Sector 52, near Artemis Hospital. One phone call, a few clear details, and your chair is reserved. No apps to install, no accounts to create, and no waiting for a callback that never comes.

01 Call or walk in: how it works

The simplest way to book is to call 81781 59945. Tell us the service you want, your preferred day, and a rough time, and we will confirm a slot on the call itself. You will also get a price estimate for most services, so there are no surprises when you arrive.

Walk-ins are welcome too, but weekends and evenings fill fast, especially during wedding season and festivals. If you need a specific stylist or a long service like balayage or bridal makeup, calling ahead is strongly recommended. A walk-in for a quick trim on a weekday morning is usually fine.

02 What to tell us when you book

Four details make booking smooth: the service, your hair length and any past colour or chemical treatments, your preferred stylist, and any allergies or sensitive skin so we can plan a patch test.

If you are booking for an event, mention the date. We can work backwards from it and advise when to book colour, trials, and styling so everything lands on the right day. This one detail prevents most last-minute booking stress.

03 Picking the right slot

Weekday mornings are the quietest and best for long services like colour, keratin, or bridal trials, because your stylist has more time and the salon is calmer. Weekday evenings and Saturdays are the busiest, so book those at least a few days ahead. Sundays are popular for family visits and full packages.

For services that need setting time, like colour or a hair spa, arrive on time or a few minutes early. A late start pushes the whole schedule, and your stylist may have to shorten the service to fit the next client. If you are running late, a quick call lets us adjust rather than cancel.

04 What to expect when you arrive

When you arrive, we confirm the service and price before starting. For colour or chemical treatments, a short consultation and patch test come first if you are new. For cuts, your stylist will discuss the plan, check your hair, and confirm the length before cutting.

Bring photos if you have a specific look in mind, and wear something that shows your usual neckline for cuts, since collars change how a cut looks. If you are unsure about anything, ask before the service starts. Questions are always easier to answer before scissors or colour are involved.

05 Rescheduling and cancellations

Plans change, and that is fine. If you need to move your appointment, call 81781 59945 as early as you can. Early notice lets us offer your slot to someone on the waitlist and find you a new time that still works before your event.

Repeated no-shows without a call make it harder to hold popular slots, especially on Saturdays. A quick message or call costs you ten seconds and keeps your booking history clean, which matters when you need a last-minute slot during a busy week.

Booking shortcut: Save our number, 81781 59945, in your phone after your first visit. Repeat booking then takes one call, and we already know your hair history, your preferred stylist, and your usual services.

Questions we hear at the salon

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome, but appointments guarantee your slot and stylist. For weekends, evenings, and long services like colour or bridal work, booking ahead is strongly recommended.

How do I book a salon appointment?

Just call 81781 59945, tell us the service and your preferred day and time, and we confirm the slot on the call. You can also walk in during opening hours.

Can I book an appointment for today?

Often yes, especially on weekdays. Call in the morning and we will tell you honestly what slots are open. Same-day weekend bookings are harder, but we will fit you in if a cancellation opens up.

What if I am running late for my appointment?

Call us as soon as you know. A short delay is usually fine, but a long delay may mean shortening the service or moving it. A quick call lets us adjust the schedule instead of cancelling your visit.
