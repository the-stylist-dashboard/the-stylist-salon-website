01 When to start bridal skin care

Start bridal skin care four to six weeks before the wedding, with one facial every two weeks, so the skin improves slowly and any reaction has time to settle. The first facial is a test, because it shows how your skin responds, and the later facials build on what worked. A single facial a week before the wedding can add a glow, but it cannot fix weeks of dullness or dryness on its own.

Book the last facial three to five days before the main ceremony, not on the day itself, so any mild redness has time to calm down. A facial on the wedding morning leaves no time for the skin to settle, and makeup sits better on calm skin than on freshly treated skin. Keep the last facial gentle, with products your skin already knows.

02 What bridal skin care includes

Bridal skin care usually includes a series of facials picked for your skin, with cleanups between them if the skin needs it, and a de-tan if the skin has darkened in the sun. Dry skin suits a hydrating facial, oily or acne-prone skin suits a deep-cleansing facial, and sensitive skin suits a gentle facial with mild products. The plan is built around your skin, not around a fixed package, because no two brides have the same skin.

03 Skin care between salon visits

Between facials, keep your routine simple: a gentle wash, a moisturiser that suits your skin, and sun protection every day, because Gurugram sun and dust undo good work fast.
