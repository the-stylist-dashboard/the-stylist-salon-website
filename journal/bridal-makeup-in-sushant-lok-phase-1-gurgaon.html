01 When to book

Book as soon as your wedding date is fixed, especially in peak wedding season. Early booking secures a trial slot and leaves time to adjust the look before the wedding. Brides who book late often get a slot, but they lose the trial window, and the trial is where most problems get solved.

If your wedding falls in the busy weeks after Diwali, book even earlier. November and December dates go first, and a trial needs a separate day well before the wedding, not the evening before.

02 What a trial actually covers

A trial is not a full rehearsal for fun. It tests the base on your skin, the eye look against your outfit, and the hairstyle against your jewellery and veil. Wear or bring a photo of your outfit, and bring the jewellery you plan to wear, because both change how a look reads.

Some brides need a second short trial, and that is normal. If you change your outfit, your jewellery, or your mind about the eye look after the first trial, book a short follow-up rather than hoping it works on the wedding day. A second trial costs less time than fixing a look under wedding morning pressure, and it lets the artist lock the final plan with no guessing left.

Bring reference photos of looks you like and looks you do not want, and a note of any skin sensitivity or allergy. Tell the artist if your skin reacts to certain products. A trial that skips these questions is not a trial, it is just a makeup session.

03 Planning the wedding morning

Work backwards from the time you must leave for the venue, then fit hair, makeup, and draping into one timeline with gaps for photos and delays. One team handling hair and makeup helps here, because the handover between two separate artists is where mornings usually slip.

Keep the morning calm on purpose: eat before makeup starts, keep your outfit and jewellery in one place, and agree who is in the room. A crowded room slows everything down, and rushing the last twenty minutes is what ruins a good look.

04 Booking, and what a quote covers

The final quote depends on the look, the products, the hairstyle, and the wedding day plan, so it is given after consultation, not as a fixed online price. No artist name or fixed bridal price is promised here, because the look decides the quote.
