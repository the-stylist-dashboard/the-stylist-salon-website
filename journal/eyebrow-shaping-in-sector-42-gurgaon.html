Eyebrow Shaping in Sector 42, Gurgaon

The Stylist Unisex Salon · 24 October 2026 · 6 min read

Eyebrows are the smallest service on our menu and the one with the biggest return. The right shape opens up your eyes, balances your face, and makes the rest of your makeup optional. The wrong shape does the opposite, and it takes weeks to grow back. That is why shaping is a skill, not a quick add-on, and why we map your brows before touching them.

01 Shape follows your face, not a trend

Brow trends come and go, but your bone structure does not. A good shape starts where your brow naturally begins, arches at the point that suits your eyes, and ends where it frames the face. Round faces suit a slightly higher arch, long faces suit a straighter brow, heart-shaped faces suit a soft arch.

We do not copy celebrity brows onto every face. The brow that suits your friend may fight your features. We study your face shape and existing growth, then shape to what is there. Working with your natural brow beats fighting it.

02 Threading: the honest workhorse

Threading is our main shaping method because it is precise, quick, and needs no chemicals or heat. A twisted cotton thread pulls hair out in clean lines, giving sharper edges than waxing and suiting sensitive skin. Most shapings take ten to fifteen minutes.

It suits almost everyone, including people who react to wax. The sensation is a brief sting that fades in seconds. New to threading? Tell us and we will go slower on the first pass. Regular clients barely notice it by the third visit.

03 Mapping before the first thread

Mapping separates a careful shaping from a rushed one. We mark three points: where the brow should start, where the arch peaks, and where it should end, measured against your nose and eyes. Two minutes of measuring is why our brows look intentional.

This matters most for first visits and over-plucked brows. If years of plucking left them thin, mapping shows exactly what to grow back and what to keep. We will give you a simple grow-out plan instead of just taking more off.

04 How often to come back

Every three to four weeks keeps a shape crisp. Brow hair grows in cycles, so a monthly rhythm catches new growth before it blurs the lines. Six weeks is fine for slow growth, but longer usually means restarting the shape instead of maintaining it.

Book your next shaping while you are in the chair. It takes ten seconds and keeps you in the habit. Regular clients spend less time per visit because the shape is already there and we only clean it up. 05 Mistakes worth avoiding

The most common mistake is over-plucking between visits. One stray hair at home is fine; reshaping the arch yourself usually ends in a repair appointment. Leave the architecture to us and only tidy obvious strays underneath.

The second is chasing perfect symmetry. Brows are sisters, not twins. Faces are naturally a little uneven, and forced mirror brows look artificial. We aim for balance that suits your face.

The photo test: take a straight-on photo before your appointment and another a week after. The difference in how awake your face looks is the real measure of a good shaping.

Questions we hear at the salon

Is threading better than waxing for eyebrows?

For most people, yes. Threading is more precise, needs no chemicals or heat, and suits sensitive skin. Waxing is faster for large areas but less exact on brow edges.

How long does eyebrow shaping take?

Ten to fifteen minutes including mapping. First visits can take a little longer if the brows need a full reshape or a grow-out plan.

How often should I get my eyebrows shaped?

Every three to four weeks keeps the shape crisp. Monthly visits catch new growth early, and each appointment is quicker once the shape is established.
