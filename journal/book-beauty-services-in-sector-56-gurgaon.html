Book Beauty Services in Sector 56, Gurgaon

The Stylist Unisex Salon · 26 October 2026 · 6 min read

Booking beauty services should be simple, but it often is not. You call one place for hair, another for nails, and a third for makeup, and somehow nothing lines up. At The Stylist, one call books the lot: hair, skin, nails, mehndi and makeup, planned in the right order for your day. If you live in Sector 56, our Sector 52 salon is a short drive away, and this guide explains exactly how booking works.

01 What you can book in one call

Start with hair: cuts, colour, highlights, balayage, keratin, smoothening and nanoplastia, plus hair spa and hair massage. Then add skin services like facials, cleanup, waxing, threading and de-tan. Nails follow with manicures, pedicures, gel nails and nail art. Mehndi and makeup, including bridal and party looks, round out the list.

Booking them together matters because order affects results. Colour before a facial is fine, but a facial right before makeup needs timing. Our team plans the sequence when you book, so services do not clash and you are not sitting around between them. Tell us the occasion and your available hours, and we will build the schedule.

02 How booking works, step by step

Call 81781 59945 and tell us what you need, your preferred day, and roughly how much time you have. We will suggest a slot, confirm the services, and give you a price range for each one before you commit. If a service needs a longer slot, like keratin or bridal makeup, we keep that time aside so you are never rushed.

On the day, arrive a few minutes early, especially for combined bookings. Your stylist starts with a short consultation to confirm the plan, and any change is agreed with you before work begins. If you need to move or cancel, call us early so we can find you a new time.

03 What to tell us when you call

Four details make booking smooth. First, the services you want and whether they are for an occasion. Second, your hair and skin history, like past colour, keratin or sensitivity, so we pick the right approach. Third, your time limits, so we do not book a three-hour plan into two hours. Fourth, any stylist preference, because regular clients often ask for the same person.

Photos help too. If you have a makeup or colour reference, keep it ready to show at the appointment. It gives your stylist a clear target and cuts down on guesswork. You do not need to send it in advance, just bring it along.

04 Timing your booking around events

For weddings and big occasions, book early. Bridal makeup and mehndi slots go weeks ahead, especially in wedding season, and hair colour should be done at least a week before the event so the tone settles. A cut two to three weeks before lets the shape settle and leaves room for a small fix if needed.

For a party or function, a few days ahead is usually enough for hair and skin services, though weekends still fill fast. A facial is best done three to five days before an event, not the day before, so any redness settles. Your stylist will confirm timing when you book.

05 Getting here from Sector 56

Sector 56 sits close to Golf Course Extension Road, so our Sector 52 salon near Artemis Hospital is only a short drive away. Our Sector 51 location at The Arzoo Apartments is another option. Both are open Wednesday to Monday, 11 AM to 9 PM, closed Tuesdays.

Weekend slots go first, so call 81781 59945 ahead for Saturday or Sunday. Weekday bookings are easier to get and suit longer combined sessions, when the salon is quieter and there is more room to plan.

The occasion rule: When you book for an event, tell us the date first and the services second. Planning backwards from the event date is how we fit colour, facials and trials in without anything landing too close to the big day.
