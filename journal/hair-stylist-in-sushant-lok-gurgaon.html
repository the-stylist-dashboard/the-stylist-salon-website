Hair Stylist in Sushant Lok, Gurgaon

The Stylist Unisex Salon · 22 October 2026 · 6 min read

Sushant Lok has no shortage of salons. Walk down the market road or scroll through the society groups and you will find plenty of names. But finding a hair stylist you actually return to is a different job. Here is how to do it, and why a good number of Sushant Lok residents drive ten minutes to Sector 52 instead.

01 Choose the stylist, not the distance

Hair grows about a centimetre a month. A bad haircut follows you for six to eight weeks. Against that, a ten-minute drive is nothing. The right question is which stylist will cut your hair right, and keep getting it right.

This is the mistake most people make: they optimise for a five-minute walk and then live with a five-week haircut. Flip it. Find the stylist first. The drive is the easy part.

02 What to ask on your first visit

Start with the consultation, not the price list. A good stylist asks what you liked and disliked about your last few haircuts, looks at how your hair falls on its own, and studies your hairline before picking up scissors. They should tell you what will not work for your hair type, not just what you want to hear.

Ask to see real client photos, on their phone or the salon's Instagram. Look for hair like yours: same texture, similar length.

03 Hair that survives Gurgaon life

Gurgaon is rough on hair. Dust on the roads, hard water in most towers, and summers that push frizz to the limit. A good stylist plans for this instead of pretending it does not exist.

Regular trims every six to eight weeks do more for your hair than any miracle product. And if your hair feels dry, rough, or unmanageable, a proper hair spa treatment once a month changes the texture completely. We do hair spa only, no exceptions, and it is one of our most booked services.

04 Be careful with colour

Colour is where cheap salons do the most damage. The rules are simple: insist on a patch test, insist on seeing the sealed colour tube opened in front of you, and be suspicious of prices that look too good. Good colour products cost money, and salons charging very little are cutting that corner.

Also ask about aftercare before you book. Colour needs the right shampoo and conditioner, and the stylist should tell you that honestly at the start.

05 For men: consistency matters more than the first cut

Men get haircuts every three to four weeks, which makes consistency the real test. The first cut is easy to get right with a photo. The fifth cut is where you see whether the stylist actually understands your hair. Ask for the same stylist every visit. They learn your growth pattern, your cowlicks, how your beard meets your jawline.

From Sushant Lok Phase 1, take Golf Course Extension Road towards Sector 52. It is about ten to twelve minutes by car. Our branch is at Shop No. 86, High Street 52, right opposite Artemis Hospital. You cannot miss the strip. Park, walk in, and ask for a consultation.

We are open Wednesday to Monday, 11 AM to 9 PM, closed Tuesdays. Our second branch at Ground Floor, The Arzoo Apartments in Sector 51 serves the same area from the other side.

Bring photos, not adjectives: words like "a bit shorter" mean different things to different people. Two or three reference photos remove all doubt.

Is it worth travelling for a stylist instead of using the nearest salon?

For a haircut, yes. A ten-minute drive for a cut you love is better than a two-minute walk to a cut you regret. Choose the stylist, not the distance.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Two or three photos of the look you want, and photos of your own hair on a good day and a bad day. Tell the stylist what products you use at home. That information is worth more than any description you can put into words.
