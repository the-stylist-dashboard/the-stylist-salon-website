It is the artist's hand, the henna quality, and timing your appointment right. Here is how to pick the right artist, what bridal and party mehndi involve, and when to book.

01 Bridal mehndi vs party mehndi

Bridal mehndi covers both hands, often to the elbows, plus the feet, with dense traditional or fusion designs. Application takes 3 to 5 hours, then the paste stays on for 6 to 8 hours or overnight for a deep stain.

Party mehndi is the lighter version: simple designs on the hands, done in 20 to 45 minutes. It suits Karwa Chauth, Teej, Diwali, or attending a wedding as a guest. Tell your artist the occasion, because the design density and the time slot are completely different.

02 How to pick the right mehndi artist

Start with the portfolio. Look at recent work, not filtered stock photos, and check the lines are clean and the filling even. Ask to see healed results too; a design that stains patchy is not good work.

Then ask about the henna. The key question: organic henna or instant chemical cones? Organic henna stains a rich reddish-brown and is gentle on skin. Instant black cones contain harsh chemicals that stain fast and dark but can cause rashes and burns. A good artist answers this proudly; a vague answer is a red flag.

For bridal work, ask for a trial: a small patch shows the line work and how your skin takes the stain. Confirm hygiene basics too, fresh cones and clean hands. Bridal mehndi is hours of close work.

The stain test: ask the artist to show you photos of their work 24 to 48 hours after application, not just fresh. The healed stain is the true report card of the henna quality.

03 Booking timeline before a wedding

For a November to February wedding, book 4 to 6 weeks ahead. Experienced artists get claimed early, and the good ones rarely have last-minute openings on auspicious dates.

Bridal mehndi is applied 1 to 2 days before the wedding, giving the stain time to deepen from orange to reddish-brown. Any earlier and it fades before the big day; any later and it never reaches full depth. Skip facials or exfoliating treatments that day, since fresh exfoliation takes the stain unevenly.

For festivals like Karwa Chauth, booking a week or two ahead is usually enough.

Questions we hear at the salon

How far in advance should I book a mehndi artist?

For a wedding, book 4 to 6 weeks ahead, especially in the November to February season when good artists fill up fast. For festivals like Karwa Chauth, a week or two is enough. Bridal mehndi is applied 1 to 2 days before the wedding.

What is organic henna and why does it matter?

Organic henna is made from natural henna powder mixed with essential oils like eucalyptus, without the harsh chemicals found in instant black cones. It stains a deep reddish-brown and is far gentler on skin. Chemical cones stain fast and dark but can cause rashes and burns. Always ask your artist what goes into the cone.

How long does bridal mehndi take to apply?

Full bridal mehndi for both hands, often up to the elbows, plus feet, usually takes 3 to 5 hours depending on the design density. Party or guest mehndi takes 20 to 45 minutes. Plan a relaxed morning or afternoon, eat beforehand, and keep the day clear after the sitting.

How do I get a dark mehndi stain?

Leave the paste on for 6 to 8 hours or overnight, avoid water on the hands for the first day, and keep hands warm, since warmth deepens the stain. A lemon-sugar dab helps the paste stick. The stain starts orange and deepens to reddish-brown over 24 to 48 hours.
