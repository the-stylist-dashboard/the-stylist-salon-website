Unisex Salon in Sector 57, Gurgaon

The Stylist Unisex Salon · 21 October 2026 · 6 min read

Most households run two salon routines: one place for her, another for him, on different days in different parts of Gurgaon. A unisex salon fixes that with one roof, one trusted team, and one slot that sorts the whole family. If you live in Sector 57, here is what a good unisex salon offers, and why our clients switched to the one-salon habit.

01 What unisex really means

Unisex does not mean generic. It means the salon serves men and women properly, with stylists trained on both: men's cuts, beard shaping, and shaving on the same menu as women's cuts, colour, balayage, and keratin, plus skin, nails, and makeup. One trusted place for the whole family.

The training matters more than the label. A good unisex salon trains every stylist across the full menu. The stylist who cuts sharp fades all day brings that precision to women's short cuts, and the colourist's eye for tone improves men's grey coverage too. Cross-training makes everyone better.

02 The practical wins: one trip, one booking

The biggest benefit is simple logistics. Couples book back-to-back or parallel appointments instead of losing two weekends. Parents get everyone sorted in one visit. You learn one salon's system, one phone number, one set of faces you trust.

Consistency is the quieter win. When the whole family goes to one place, the stylists learn your household's hair over time: the fast-growing crown, the curls that need weight removed just so. That memory is worth more than any loyalty discount.

03 For the men: what is on the menu

Men's services at a proper unisex salon go well beyond a quick trim: precision haircuts, beard shaping, clean shaving, and men's facials built for thicker, oilier skin. Hair spa and head massage are popular with our male clients too, since Gurgaon's dust and hard water leave scalps dry and irritated.

One note we are strict about: we do hair spa and head massage only. That focus is deliberate. We would rather do a short menu well than a long menu badly.

04 For the women: the full range

Women's services run the full menu: haircuts, global colour, highlights, balayage, keratin and smoothening, hair spa, and head massage. Skin covers facials, cleanups, waxing, threading, and de-tan. Nails cover manicures, pedicures, gel work, and nail art. Bridal and party makeup round it out.

Because everything sits under one roof, combinations are easy: colour plus a hair spa in one visit, a facial while your colour processes, bridal trials with hair and makeup planned together.

05 How to judge a unisex salon's quality

Do not take the word unisex on faith. Check that both sides of the menu are genuinely strong: ask to see men's work and women's work, and ask whether the same stylists handle both or the salon runs two separate teams. Either model works, but know which one you are walking into.

Run the usual checks too: clean tools, fresh capes, readable product labels, clear pricing before you sit down, and a consultation where the stylist listens. A unisex salon should meet every standard a specialised salon meets, on both sides of the chair. See our unisex salon guide for Sector 51 and our hair spa guide for Sector 56.
